# "6 7": viewer script for the owner's approval

**Source:** the owner's own story and humour (22 Nov 2026). Their words are kept as they were said. Only the order is
set for the video, plus three short narrator lines (marked ➕) so a stranger can follow the cut.

**Format system:**
- **Frame 0:** motion plus a two-line banner.
- **Pace:** a visual change every ~1.8 s.
- **Setups (six):**
  1. the living room, cosy;
  2. Isaac's phone;
  3. the red jump-scare light;
  4. candlelight over the board;
  5. lights off, phone torches;
  6. the floating, in a cold glow.
- **Length:** about 50–60 s.
- **Camera:** the new background blur and slow creep on every close-up.

**Voices:**
| Voice | Who | Caption colour |
|---|---|---|
| ME (narrator) | an Aussie teen, telling it to his mates | white |
| ISAAC | the guy with glasses: giggly, a menace | yellow |
| GLENDON | the host, panicking | orange |
| ISAAC'S PHONE | a meme video's audio, tinny through the phone speaker | phone white |

**Premise (one sentence):** three mates mess with a Ouija board, it keeps spelling "6 7", and Isaac won't stop doing the
bit, until it's the last thing he ever says.

---

## FRAME 0 · 0:00 (the end, first)
*(Pitch dark. Isaac floating in the air, hands at his throat, a cold glow on him. Banner: **HIS LAST WORDS WERE…** /
**"6 7"**. A low hum, his strangled breath.)*
> **ME:** "6 7." The last words that came out of my best friend's mouth.

## THE CHILL · 0:05
*(The living room at night, lamps on, cosy. Three teens slumped on the couch, faces lit by their phones.)*
> **ME:** We were chilling at Glendon's place. In the living room, scrolling mindlessly.

*(Close on Isaac, the one with glasses, grinning at his phone. Insert: his screen, a cartoon cat being bounced up and
down, "huss huss huss" on screen.)*
> **ISAAC'S PHONE:** In this town, everyone is partying except for me. I'm locked in. HUSS HUSS HUSS HUSS.

## THE BOARD · 0:14
*(A hard cut: the lights dip red; Glendon's face pops up right in front of the lens out of nowhere, holding up a Ouija
board, lit from below. A sting.)*
> **ME:** Then Glendon pulls out a Ouija board.
> **ME (to Glendon):** Geez, don't scare me like that.

*(An unsettling noise creeps in under everything from here: a low hum, faint whispers.)*

## THE SPIRIT · 0:22
*(Candles. Three hands on the planchette.)*
> **ME:** Then all three of us hold on to the thing that goes on the Ouija board.

*(It slides around all the letters on its own… then lands on 6. Then 7.)*
> **ME:** It floats around all of the letters… but goes to 6… and 7.

## THE BIT · 0:31
*(Isaac yanks his hand off, laughing his head off, doing the hand move.)*
> **ISAAC:** 67! 67! 67! 67!
> **ME:** Isaac, stop playing around mate. You're not supposed to have your hands off the thing, or else you'll get
> cursed.

## OUT OF CONTROL · 0:40
*(Close on the board: our two hands, dragged by the planchette: 6, 7, 6, 7, faster and faster.)*
> **ME:** Then me and Glendon's hands move automatically. Going through 6 7. Again. And again.
> **GLENDON:** STOP IT, it's getting out of control!!!

## LIGHTS OFF · 0:47
*(Every light snaps off. Black. Phone torches flick on. Isaac's laugh, still going, from the dark.)*
> **ME:** The lights go off. ➕ And Isaac's still laughing.

## THE END · 0:51
*(Torchlight finds Isaac: his hands fly to his neck, choking. His feet leave the floor and he rises into the air, in a
cold glow.)*
> **ME:** Then all of a sudden, his neck starts choking. He holds onto it… and he floats in the air.

*(We scream.)*
> **ME:** ➕ And his last words?

*(Back to the frame-0 picture: Isaac in the air.)*
> **ISAAC (muffled, strangled):** 6… 7…

*(A hard cut to black on "7". The ending loops into the first frame.)*
