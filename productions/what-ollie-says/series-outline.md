## Part 2 · The Water Game
**Dates:** dawn and day on Thu 12 Nov. Mark is on his night shift. The night is 03:00 Fri 13 Nov (clip 1,213).

- **Hook:** dawn. Their garden is drowned, and next door's lawn is bone dry.
- **Discovery:**
  - Ava confronts Kate with the footage. Kate breaks: *"I had a son before you. He drowned. I let go of him."*
  - Then, white-faced, Kate repeats her grandmother's saying: *"What you let go of over the water, the water takes."*
  - She asks Ava not to tell Dad.
- **They try to stop it:** Kate locks herself in her room. Ollie sleeps in Ava's room. Ava's door has no lock.
- **The night, step by step:**
  1. At 3:00 Ollie sits up beside Ava, eyes shut, and says Kuba's line in the toddler's voice. Ava holds him and he goes limp, still asleep.
     - Both beds have their heads against the shared wall, so the nursery camera next door hears him. That is clip 1,213.
  2. Kate's lock turns: she unlocks it in her sleep. She pauses outside Ava's door and whispers *"Kubuś?"* Ava holds the handle shut. Kate doesn't force it, and her footsteps go downstairs.
  3. From the window Ava sees her mother wading into the river alone, calling her son. Ava screams "MUM!" Nothing reaches Kate.
  4. Ava runs, ordering "Stay here!", and leaves Ollie asleep on her bed.
  5. In the river Ava grabs Kate's arm. Kate keeps walking, hip-deep and stronger than her, and Ava can't drag her back without both of them going under. Touch doesn't reach her.
  6. Ollie, now awake, has followed barefoot. At the edge he says his words. Kate stops, turns, wades back and takes his hand.
  7. The night plays out at the edge: "Take him!", her hand opens, and the water pulls. Ava, already in the water, catches him.
- **Set-piece:** Kate wading in alone with Ava unable to hold her; Ollie at the edge bringing her back.
- **Ending:** Ollie, soaked: *"If I don't say my words, Mama goes in the water by herself."*
- **What it proves:** keeping Ollie away doesn't stop the curse. It sends Kate into the river alone.

## Part 3 · Wierzbno
**Dates:** day on Fri 13 Nov. Mark is home from 06:00 and swaps his Friday shift. The night is 03:00 Sat 14 Nov (clip 1,214).

1. **Hook:** *"My mum's name is on a memorial for the dead."* The stone, in close-up.
2. **Friday afternoon, Ava alone:**
   - The wristband in the tin reads "NN" (unknown woman). Ava: *"Nobody ever knew she lived."*
   - The memorial page: Kate's name listed as missing, and under the photo an old comment, *"Kasia and her little Kuba. We still light a candle."* Ava now knows the name **Kasia**.
   - Tomasz Kowal "gave his life saving a child".
   - Ava messages the memorial page.
3. **Why Mark is involved:**
   - He came home at 6 a.m. Friday to Ava and Ollie soaked and Kate shaking. Ava told him Mum sleepwalks into the river. Neither Kate nor Ava tells him about Kuba.
4. **Why they think it can work:**
   - Separating Ollie from Kate sent her to the river alone.
   - Ollie: *"In the old house it was only in my room. She just held my hand in bed."* For three years a night that stayed indoors was harmless.
   - So the plan is containment: keep Ollie where she can find him, and seal the house from the river.
5. **The plan, Friday evening:**
   - Mark locks the back and front doors and pockets both keys.
   - He wedges a towel under the back door and sits on a kitchen chair against it, wide awake.
   - Ollie sleeps in his own bed, with Ava on the floor beside him.
   - Mark: *"She can do her hand thing. She's not getting outside."*
6. **03:00:** Kate comes in, eyes open, kneels and takes his hand. Ava lets the ritual run, as agreed.
7. **Downstairs:**
   - Water seeps in under the locked back door, through the towel and past Mark's feet, then across the kitchen and **up the stairs**.
   - Mark, the water engineer: *"That's not possible."* He follows it up.
8. **Ollie's room:**
   - Water spreads round the bed. "Take him!" Kate's hand opens over a flooded floor, and the current drags Ollie off the bed toward the door.
   - Ava and Mark grab him together, and the water lets go.
9. **Dawn:** the carpets are dry, and Mark stares at the dry stairs. Ava, holding the photo: **"If Kuba drowned, why isn't his name on the stone?"**

**What it proves:** there is no safe place. It also plants the two keys to the finale: the name Kasia, and the missing name on the stone.

## Part 4 · 1,214 Nights
**Dates:** day on Sat 14 Nov (the archive holds 1,214 clips). Evening: Mark is called out to a real flood. Ewa's reply comes that night. The roof is 03:00 Sun 15 Nov.

- **Hook:** a wall of 1,214 night-camera clips, all at 03:00.
- **The archive:**
  - Mark wants to go through the nursery camera with Ava, but is called out before he sees much. Ava goes through it alone.
  - **Clip 1** (Fri 14 Jul 2023): three-year-old Ollie's first night. Perfect Polish in a toddler's voice that was never taught.
  - **Clip 388** (Sun 4 Aug 2024): four-year-old Ollie to the camera, *"Daddy, Mama did the water game again."*
  - **Clip 412** (Wed 28 Aug 2024): *"Mama, don't be sad."* It fits what Kate saw, so it changes the ending: she lies down beside his bed.
  - **Clip 903** (Thu 1 Jan 2026, Ollie at a sleepover): the empty bed, with water pooling where a child should be.
  - **Clip 1,207** (Sat 7 Nov 2026, with the garden camera's view of the same night): Ollie, desperate, *"I didn't fall in, Mama! I'm here!"* She saw the boat go under, so this contradicts her memory. Nothing changes and the night goes on: *"Weź go!"*
  - **The garden camera:** the river climbing the lawn, night after night, since September.
- **Ewa's message:** Kuba is alive, and how. The boat turned over by the willows; Tomasz climbed a willow and tied the boy to it with his firefighter's belt; he was found that afternoon.
- **The 2008 flashback** shows the belt on Tomasz as he takes Kuba from Kate's arms.
- **Ava understands:**
  - The night holds only what Mum saw. It can be **continued** (clip 412), never **contradicted** (clip 1,207). The true ending has to start where Mum's memory stops.
  - She has two tools: the true ending, and the name "Kasia".
  - She prepares the Polish on her translator and makes a pinkie promise with Ollie: "wait for me".
- **Escalation:** a real flood warning, Mark called in, and Kate locked in her room. She goes out of her window onto the extension's flat roof.
- **Set-piece:**
  - Kate at the roof edge in the rain, the flood all round the house.
  - Ollie climbs out to keep her from the water, and stays silent at the L5 moment, keeping his promise.
- **Ending, the vote:** **A**, give the night its true ending. **B**, wake her.

## Part 5 · Finale A: the true ending
- **Hook:** back on the roof, mid-line.
- **First attempt:** Ollie, frightened, gets out only the end of Ava's whisper: *"I'm alive, Mama."* It has nothing to connect to: no willows, no boat. It is rejected: *"Weź go!"* again, and his heel slips on the wet upstand. Ava's fist is already in his pyjama top.
- **Second attempt:** Ava whispers the continuation from the last thing Kate saw, and Ollie says it in the child's place:
  > *"The boat turned over by the willows. Tomasz held me. He climbed the willow and tied me to it with his belt. They found me that afternoon. I'm alive."*

  Every detail hooks onto her memory:
  - **the willows**, where she saw the boat flip;
  - **Tomasz**, whose name she shouted;
  - **his belt**, which she saw when he took Kuba.
- **Why it works:** the night now runs past the moment it always stopped. "Take him!" was answered by Tomasz, the man she asked. The boy was received by a person, not by the water, so nothing is owed. The river sinks back.
- **After:**
  - The role leaves Ollie, and the toddler voice never returns. The photo dries.
  - Kate remembers at most a dream of a man in a tree, and still believes she killed her son.
  - **Ava's choice:** telling Mum that Kuba lived means telling her how Ava knows, which means three years of nights walking Ollie toward water. She stays silent and answers Ewa: *"I can't tell you. I'm sorry."*
- **Final image:** 03:00, silence, Ava awake.

## Part 5 · Finale B: wake her
- **Hook:** back on the roof, mid-line.
- **The choice:** Ava shouts **"KASIA!"**, the one word from that night besides the child's voice.
- **Kate wakes inside the night,** with the 2008 flood laid over the garden, a little boy's hand in hers, over the water. Every instinct of that memory says let go.
- **Why it's dangerous:** the 2008 note warned she panics when woken. If she lets go, Ollie is taken.
- **She holds on.** The debt is what you let go of over the water. Holding on, awake, inside that moment takes the gift back, and her broken promise is kept.
- **After:**
  - Kate learns that letting go in 2008 saved Kuba, and that holding on now saved Ollie.
  - She faces what the nights did to Ollie, who has seen her wake screaming another boy's name.
  - Mark learns about a hidden son. They leave the river.
  - Kate calls Jakub. He asks: *"Why didn't you look for me?"*
- **Final image:** clip 1,216, weeks later, Kate awake at Ollie's bedside: *"Goodnight, Ollie."*
