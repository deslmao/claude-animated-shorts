# The Skyscraper Fixed in Secret: story approval

**Format:** action-first. The opening act (welders secretly rebuilding a brand-new skyscraper every night while thousands work in it by day) makes the viewer ask why; the mechanism is shown on one model with one fan; and a true modern reassessment recontextualises the danger without undoing the choice he made.

**Estimated length:** 87 s · **Words:** 273 · **Narration:** 1532 characters (about 1532 ElevenLabs credits) · **Voices:** narrator=george

## Premise
- **question:** Why was a brand-new skyscraper being repaired in secret every night, and was it really about to fall?
- **protagonist:** William LeMessurier, the tower's own structural engineer
- **want:** a safe building
- **fear:** that the tower he designed could blow over, and that admitting it would ruin him
- **protects:** the thousands of people in and around the tower
- **decision:** tell the owners about the flaw in his own building
- **action:** in 1978, welders worked through the night inside a brand-new Manhattan skyscraper, hidden in plywood boxes, gone before the office workers arrived each morning
