# Spine evaluation packet (phase 1): Wet Paint

format (as claimed by the writer): mystery · factual: no (fiction)

## Premise
- protagonist: Mira, fifteen, who leads the school mural and photographs it every morning for the county art prize
- want: to stop whoever is spoiling her mural before Friday's judges, and win
- loses: the mural, if there's one more picture (Mr. Okafor: "One more and it's painted over before the judges"); then, once she understands, the person she thought she was
- obstacle: the pictures are painted while nobody is watching, and the truth is about Mira herself
- conflict: A, Leo is vandalising the mural for revenge; B, someone is using the mural to tell a story about something that really happened

## Rules of the situation
- The mural is judged at 9:00 on Friday for the county youth art prize. Mira leads it.
- Mr. Okafor, the art teacher: one more picture and the whole corner is painted over before the judges come.
- The school's cameras cover the gates and the bike sheds, not the courtyard wall.
- The school opens at 6:45 for breakfast club. Children sign a sheet at the hall door, which opens onto the courtyard.
- The mural's paint tins sit under a tarp by the wall. Acrylic stays tacky for about an hour.
- Leo, suspended for spray-painting the bike sheds, drops his little brother at breakfast club just before 7:00. He sprays; he doesn't use brushes.
- Mira arrives at 7:30 every day to photograph the mural, with her camera on its orange strap.
- Asha, new this term, comes to breakfast club because her mum starts work early. She sits alone on the bench by the mural, drawing.
