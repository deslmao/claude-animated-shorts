## Why this version
- **The hook starts mid-action.** The first frame is already running and counting. "Fourteen?" lands at about 2 s
  and asks the question by itself. It mirrors Part 1's "…eight?", so returning viewers recognise the series
  instantly. New viewers need no context.
- **The recap is one line.** "Last night, Milo's hallway grew an extra door. You told him to run." That is 4 s,
  spoken over the running, never a pause. It catches up anyone who missed Part 1 and rewards the voters: their B
  is the first thing the story honours.
- **Something new every 2–3 seconds, with no slow stretch.** Part 1 lost viewers before 0:30, and its 11.5–21.5 s
  stretch was silent inserts and a slow approach. Here, that stretch holds the photo appearing twice, the
  impossible count, the normal-looking stairs, the voice upstairs and the first text. The strongest reveals are
  around 0:28–0:37: the window message, the stinger, the moved door and the knocks. So the reasons to stay arrive
  just where Part 1 dipped.
- **Every shot advances the story.** No establishing wides and no slow push-ins. The two still holds (shot 8, the
  ordinary stairs; shot 15, the door) are reveals.
- **"Twenty-six" and the ordinary stairs make the step count unexplainable without a word.** Twenty-six is exactly
  twice thirteen.
- **The contradiction is sharp in both directions.**
  - The texts say Juno is outside, and she can see someone upstairs.
  - The voice says the one outside isn't her.
  - The knocks at the front door are the same knocks as the old door in Part 1.

  Neither side is trustworthy, so "which one is her?" is a real question. That is a vote people will argue about.
- **Nothing behind the door is revealed.** The door has moved to the top of the stairs, but stays closed.

## Continuity with Part 1
- **Time.** It picks up seconds after Part 1's final click: same night, same hoodie, same phone, same chat thread. The
  first message explains why Juno is suddenly home ("Left work early"). The timing is still slightly suspicious, which
  is deliberate.
- **The photo** lay by the old door upstairs. Finding it on the stairs (twice) is meant to be impossible, not an error.
- **The house.**
  - The old door, its brass handle, the cold light under it and the two shadows are all Part 1's assets.
  - The stairwell, the banister and the hallway are Part 1's set.
  - The downstairs matches it: same wallpaper, wood floor, trim and night lighting.
- **The voice.** Juno's voice keeps Part 1's split: warm when it calls, cold when it gives an order.
- **Juno's appearance.** She appears only in the photo, as in Part 1.
