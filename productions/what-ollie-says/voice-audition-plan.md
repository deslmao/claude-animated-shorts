# Child voice audition plan

**Runs only after** the Polish in `polish-verification.md` (section A at least) is confirmed. **Not started; no credits spent.**

**Why first:** the hook of Part 1 is a six-year-old saying Polish words he doesn't understand. If that voice sounds adult, synthetic or mispronounced, Part 1 fails, so it is tested before the storyboard is finished and before any larger narration order.

## What gets generated (about 700 credits in total)
| Role | Candidates | Lines | Approx. characters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ollie, 6** | 3 voices | "Not tonight, Mama. I'm tired." · "Daddy says it hears me if I'm scared." · L1 (Polish, calm and sleepy) · L5 (Polish, thin and frightened) | 3 × 135 |
| **Toddler** (Ollie at 3, Kuba at 3) | 2 voices | L1 slowly, word by word (Polish) · L5 as a scream (Polish) · "Mama… don't be sad." | 2 × 95 |
| **Kate** | 2 voices | L4 shouted (Polish) · "I've forgotten all that, love." | 2 × 45 |

The Polish uses the multilingual voice model. Before spending anything I'll check the voice tool can select it for these presets.

## How it's judged
1. **Age:** does Ollie sound like a small child, not an adult doing a child?
2. **Polish:** the native speaker listens to the Polish clips and marks each as natural, acceptable or wrong.
3. **Recital:** L1 must sound learned by heart and calm, not acted.
4. **Match:** the English and Polish lines must sound like the same child.

## If no child voice passes
In order of preference:
1. A slightly older child voice, kept to whispers and half-asleep delivery.
2. A real recording of a Polish-speaking child for the two Polish lines only (two short sentences), with the generated voice for the English.
3. Pitching up a young adult voice. This is a last resort and usually audible.

## After the audition
- Cast Ollie and Kate, then order **Part 1 only**: about 470 characters, about 600 with retakes.
- The toddler result is recorded for later parts; nothing more is ordered.
