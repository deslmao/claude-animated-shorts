# "I Got a Text From Myself 10 Minutes in the Future": Part 1 (v2)

**Story id:** `future-text`. **Target length:** about 85 s; the narration and dialogue total 171 words.

## 1. Core story: one cause-and-effect chain, no loose mysteries

**The premise in the first 8 seconds:**
- At 8:02, Milo gets a text from **his own number**, stamped **8:12**.
- The text says **"Whatever you do, don't get on the bus."**

**Goal:** get to today's **maths final**. Miss it and he spends the summer retaking it.

**Why "don't get on the bus":**
- Future Milo says why, and the reason comes true on screen: **the bus breaks down on the bridge, and everyone on it misses the exam.**

**Why the predictions matter:**
- Each prediction is something future-Milo **saw at this bus stop ten minutes ago**, so each one builds trust and leads to the next.
  1. **Theo's phone.** Theo, his best friend, is about to drop his phone. Milo catches it, so the warning has already helped once.
  2. **The car.** "Step back. Now." A speeding car drenches the exact spot they were standing on. Milo pulls Theo back just in time. Now the warnings are about danger, and Milo is convinced.
  3. **The bus.** Milo begs Theo not to get on. Theo laughs ("Says who? Your phone?") and boards.
     - Milo has to choose between his friend and his exam, and lets the bus go.

**Clear cause and effect:**
- **Milo skips the bus and runs, so he makes it to the exam.** He bursts in at **8:12**.
- **The bus breaks down on the bridge, so every other seat is empty.**
  - Theo's text with a photo of the bus, smoking on the bridge, shows it happened exactly as predicted.

**The twist, surprising but simple:**
- The time is **8:12**, the minute the first text was sent.
- Milo realises **he is the one who sends it**. Now it's his turn: he can send **one text, ten minutes back, to himself at the bus stop.**

**The decision is concrete:**
- **A: SAVE YOURSELF.** Send the same warning. He gets to the exam again, but Theo and the class stay stuck.
- **B: STOP THE BUS.** Tell his past self to stop the bus and get everyone off. He might save them all, or miss the exam himself.

## 4. The A/B decision
- **A: SAVE YOURSELF.** He sends the same warning, makes the exam, and leaves Theo and the class stuck.
- **B: STOP THE BUS.** He sends a new warning to his past self: stop the bus and save everyone, at the risk of missing the exam himself.

## 5. Why both choices make a strong Part 2
- **A** keeps the timeline safe but costs a friendship. Part 2 can be about guilt, and about what else future-Milo knows.
- **B** rewrites the morning: past-Milo gets a *new* first message and must stop a bus. It's an action episode, and changing time has consequences.

Neither choice decides the series' later answers.
