automated QA script. Nothing has been changed since the render.

## Minor (can be accepted)
- **13.5–16.5 s:** Gus is small, partly behind the bikes, and his bench barely bends. The bike handover still reads in the 16 s two-shot and in the 72 s flashback.
- **35–36.5 s:** no actual hug (they stand side by side); the clink is carried by the sound.
- **40.5–42.5 s:** the door bang and the stones rolling out aren't visible; the storm reads through the sound and the cool light.
- **46–47 s:** the snapping washing line isn't visible.
- **58–60.5 s:** Milo reads as standing rather than sitting on the kerb, and Gus isn't visible as a speck. The silent beat still works.
- **64–65.5 s:** Gus isn't visible in the sky behind Milo at the bus stop.
- **77.5–78.5 s:** the neighbours and windows are small.
- **QA "duplicate" frames 375, 1000, 1002 and 1917** (12.5, 33.3, 33.4 and 63.9 s): single-frame holds at shot starts or ends, invisible at 30 fps, and not near any chunk join.
- **Close-ups:** the checks warn about cropped hands in a few close-ups.

## Working well

- **31.5–35 s:** Gus on the ceiling with his lightbulb excuse, then drifting back up alone after the call. Its flashback at 51.5–53 s makes the twist click.
- **53.5–61 s:** "I was… the last one": close-ups, then genuine silence (−70 LUFS from 56.5 to 58.6 s), then the single music-box note.
- **72–74 s:** Juno's flashback to the bike and "Ugh. He fixed my bike."
- **79–81.5 s:** Gus crashing down and the star-shaped crack in the road.
- **82–85.5 s:** the cafe chair callback, "This is your fault.", and the pancake-war callback.
- **Sound:**
  - every section sits between −12.9 and −14.7 LUFS (the crash peaks at −11.8);
  - the music is 13 LU under the voice and dips 3 dB more under words, so it never competes with the dialogue;
  - the mood changes follow the approved plan.
- **Pace:** something new happens every 2–4 s throughout. The only longer still passages are the planned reveal and silence (53.5–61 s) and the closing shot.
