# The Skyscraper Fixed in Secret
format: action-first
why: The opening act (welders secretly rebuilding a brand-new skyscraper every night while thousands work in it by day) makes the viewer ask why; the mechanism is shown on one model with one fan; and a true modern reassessment recontextualises the danger without undoing the choice he made.
length: 85
voice: george
voices: lemessurier=brian
mood: curious
factual: yes

## Premise
- question: Why was a brand-new skyscraper being repaired in secret every night, and was it really about to fall?
- protagonist: William LeMessurier, the tower's own structural engineer
- want: a safe building
- fear: that the tower he designed could blow over, and that admitting it would ruin him
- protects: the thousands of people in and around the tower
- decision: tell the owners about the flaw in his own building
- rule: none
- conflict: none
- action: in 1978, welders worked through the night inside a brand-new Manhattan skyscraper, hidden in plywood boxes, gone before the office workers arrived each morning

## Writer notes
**Fixes, verified against primary sources** (research.md):
- **The order of events:** the bolts were learned of in May, the student's question came in June.
- **The protagonist** is named in beat 2.
- **The strike claim** is accurate.
- **Why people stayed in the tower:** generators and a weather watch.
- **The ending** reuses the beat-3 demonstration.

The risk is that it's still the densest of the three (church, braces, bolts, corners, damper). The fan is the one tool for all of it.
