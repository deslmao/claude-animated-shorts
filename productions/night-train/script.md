# The Last Train (Part 1 of a vote series: viewer script for the owner's approval)

**Idea:** the owner's own story (21 Nov 2026). **Format:** an "Imagine…" POV horror chase, about 75 seconds, that
ends on a binary choice; the most-liked answer decides Part 2.

**Voices:**
| Voice | Who | Caption colour |
|---|---|---|
| The narrator | calm, second person ("you") | white |
| The man | hoarse, cracked, screaming, then a whisper | red |
| Your thoughts | a scared teen, whispered | pale yellow |
| The train announcer | calm, flat, through the speakers | green |

**Premise (one sentence):** on the last train home, a screaming man with no reflection follows you through an empty
train, until the speakers announce a stop that doesn't exist.

The man is "a man in a soaked, torn coat", not "a homeless man": the scare is that he isn't alive, and the channel
shouldn't make homeless people the monster (it's also safer for advertisers).

---

## FRAME 1 · 0:00
*(Close: a man in a soaked, torn coat, eyes wide, lunging forward in his seat, screaming straight at the camera.
Sound from frame 1: the train rattling, his scream.)*
> **THE MAN:** WHY DID THIS HAPPEN TO ME? WHY ME?!
> **NARRATOR:** Imagine you're on the last train home, and the man in front of you won't stop screaming.

## WHO HE IS · 0:06
*(Wide: the carriage at night, 9:47 on the display. You in your seat; three seats ahead, the man, turned round,
staring at you. Above the windows, the line map. Planted: one stop on it you've never seen. Nobody mentions it yet.)*
> **NARRATOR:** It's 9:47. He's three seats in front of you. Turned all the way round. Staring right at you.
> **THE MAN:** I was just going home! Just like you!

## HE ISN'T NORMAL · 0:16
*(Close on his coat: water dripping onto the floor.)*
> **NARRATOR:** His coat is soaking wet. It hasn't rained all week.

*(You turn to the dark window. Your reflection, the empty seats, the lights. Where he's sitting: nothing.)*
> **NARRATOR:** You look at the window. You can see yourself. You can see the empty seats. But where he's sitting…
> there's nobody there.
> **NARRATOR:** He has no reflection.
*(A heartbeat starts.)*

## YOU MOVE · 0:31
> **YOU (thought):** Don't look at him. Just get up. Walk.

*(You stand and walk to the door at the end of the carriage. It slides open; you step through.)*
*(Behind you, the door slides open again.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Behind you, the door slides open again. He's following you.

## THE EMPTY TRAIN · 0:40
*(The next carriage: empty. Through the glass, the one after it: empty.)*
> **NARRATOR:** And then you notice: this carriage is empty. So is the next one. It's just you… and him.

## THE RUN · 0:46
*(You run: doors, poles, carriage after carriage, the lights flickering, your breath loud.)*
> **NARRATOR:** You run. As fast as you can. You don't look back.

*(You stop at the last carriage, bent over, gasping. Silence apart from the rails.)*
> **YOU (thought, panting):** I… I lost him. I must have lost him.

## THE JUMPSCARE · 0:56
*(You straighten up and turn round. His face is right in front of yours. A hard sting. He smiles.)*
> **THE MAN (a whisper):** This is your stop.

## THE STOP THAT DOESN'T EXIST · 1:00
*(The speakers chime.)*
> **ANNOUNCER:** The next stop is… Death City.
> **NARRATOR:** You've ridden this line every day for years. There is no Death City.

*(The train stops. The doors hiss open onto a dark platform in thick fog; a sign: DEATH CITY.)*
> **NARRATOR:** The doors open. Do you get off… or stay on and run for the next stop?

*(Panels: **A · GET OFF** / **B · STAY ON**. Cut to black within half a second.)*

## Clarity checklist
| The viewer must understand | Told by |
|---|---|
| the situation, in line 1 | "Imagine you're on the last train home, and the man in front of you won't stop screaming." |
| where he is, and that he's fixed on you | "He's three seats in front of you… Staring right at you." |
| the first wrong detail | "His coat is soaking wet. It hasn't rained all week." |
| the impossible thing | "He has no reflection." |
| he's coming after you | "He's following you." |
| nobody can help | "It's just you… and him." |
| the stop is impossible | "There is no Death City." |
| the choice | "Do you get off… or stay on and run for the next stop?" |

**The rewatch clue for the pinned comment:** the line map above the windows at 0:06 already shows DEATH CITY between
two stops you know.

## Production notes
- **Sets:** a new night-train set: two or three carriages with sliding end doors, dark windows showing the passengers'
  reflections, the line map, a destination display, and the foggy DEATH CITY platform.
- **The reflection:** the window shows you and the carriage but not him (rendered as a real mirror image of the scene
  without him).
- **Sound (ElevenLabs):**
  - the train rattling at night, from frame 1;
  - his scream;
  - sliding doors;
  - running footsteps and panting;
  - flickering lights;
  - a heartbeat;
  - the jumpscare sting;
  - the announcement chime;
  - the doors hissing open.
- **Same look as the breakfast Short:** cold light and the dark closing in around the frame.
- **Length:** about 75 s, with escalation every 5–10 s and no gap over 1.5 s except the held breath before the
  jumpscare (about 0.8 s, with the rails still loud).
