## Cover
**File:** `part1-cover.jpg` (1080 × 1920). One design for all three apps.

**What it is:** frame 150 of the film, the hook: Ollie upright in bed, eyes shut, seen through Ava's phone (REC,
03:00:05). Below it, on a dark band: "HE'S NEVER / HEARD POLISH." in white and "SO WHO IS TALKING?" in yellow.
Nothing is painted in; the picture is the engine's render of that frame.

**Placement:** the face sits at about y 520–1000 and the text at about y 1230–1620, both inside Instagram's 3:4 grid
crop (y 240–1680). Inside the player, the yellow line can sit under the apps' bottom text; the title lines stay clear.

**How to set it:**
- **YouTube Shorts:** upload `part1-cover.jpg` if the Details page offers it; otherwise choose the frame at **0:05**.
- **TikTok:** "Edit cover", then add `part1-cover.jpg` from your photos, or pick the frame at **0:05**.
- **Instagram Reels:** "Edit cover", then "Add from camera roll", and pick `part1-cover.jpg`. Check the grid preview.

## Before you post
1. **Captions and Polish subtitles are burned in.** Don't turn on the apps' auto-captions, or the text will double.
2. **Use the same numbers everywhere** (1 and 2, as on the end card). Count all three platforms together for Part 2.
3. **AI disclosure.** The voices are synthetic (ElevenLabs). On YouTube, the altered-content label is for realistic
   footage, so "No" is reasonable for this cartoon. On TikTok and Instagram, the AI-generated label is the transparent
   choice.
4. **Music** is original (synthesised in-house), so no copyright claim is expected.
5. **Pin the comment after the video is live.** YouTube doesn't allow pinning on a scheduled video.
6. **Part 2 opens on the result** ("Most of you said…"). Both choices happen in the locked Part 2.
