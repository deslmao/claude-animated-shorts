## Clarity block
- **Premise:** "Imagine your boyfriend's being chased by the police. Then he escapes… by changing his shirt."
- **The rule:** "the police remember exactly what you look like. Your car. Your face. Your clothes."
- **The trick:** "a spare outfit. He saved it there, on purpose." and "It has to be the whole outfit."
- **The answer, named:** "they lost his description. New clothes, out of sight, and the police stop looking for that shirt."
- **The joke's setup:** the real Gordon wears the same shirt all video; it's shown close in line 11.

## How the motion targets will be met (the plan)
1. **Every Blender plate is rendered wide** (a much bigger field of view than the frame), and the camera **pans, tilts
   and zooms from the plate's own lens position**. A turn from that point is exact perspective over a flat plate, so the
   city moves correctly behind the cast. That gives real camera motion without breaking the picture. Target: a moving
   camera in 15–25 % of frames.
2. **Something is always moving:**
   - the cars drive;
   - the police lights flash;
   - the characters keep idle and gesture beats;
   - every shot has a continuous drift, and no camera move ever eases to a stop.

   No full-screen still cards: the boot and the HUD are in the 3D shot.
3. **Measured before the final:** the 540p preview goes through the same frame script as STUDY.md, and any hot spot is
   fixed before the final render.
