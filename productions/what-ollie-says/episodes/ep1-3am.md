# Episode 1: 3:00

> **Superseded (16 Nov 2026).** This script predates the locked supernatural story and is kept for reference only. The locked version is [`../series-outline.md`](../series-outline.md), with the rules and dates in [`../story-lock.md`](../story-lock.md). The script will be rewritten from the outline.

**Runtime:** about 104 s.

**Purpose:** hook the audience, make them love the brother and sister, and leave them with one burning question: *who is out there with Ollie at 3 a.m.?*

**Ends on:** "Not tonight, Mama. I'm tired."

**Looks:**
- **Present-day night (N):** cold moonlit blue, one warm source per room (Ava's fairy lights, the red LED clock), rain on glass.
- **Day (D):** warm amber kitchen.
- **2008 fragment (F08):** cold teal, desaturated, slightly slowed, muffled sound. Brief.

**Subtitles:** the Polish lines here are **not** subtitled until Ava translates them (beat 6). At first the audience is as lost as she is.

## Script
**COLD OPEN. INT. AVA'S BEDROOM. NIGHT.**
Fairy lights. A child's crayon drawing pinned to the wall: two stick figures, ME + AVA.
AVA's knuckles knock three times on the wall. A beat. Three small knocks answer. She smiles and pulls the duvet to her chin.

The red LED clock flicks from 2:59 to 3:00.

Through the wall: OLLIE's voice. Small. Calm. Words she doesn't know.

**OLLIE (O.S.) [PL provisional L1]:** *Mamo, woda jest przy drzwiach.*

Ava's eyes open. She fumbles for her phone and hits record. She knocks three times.

Nothing. A door creaks. Footsteps, one heavy, one light. A shadow slides across the gap under her door. The stairs. Far below, a bolt slides.

*Title: WHAT OLLIE SAYS AT 3 A.M. · PART 1*

Through the closed window, a muffled shout. Ava gets up, cracks the window open and holds her phone out into the rain. The garden drops away into black.

From far down, a child's voice, thin as thread:

**OLLIE (O.S.) [PL provisional L5]:** *Obiecałaś, że nie puścisz.*

**INT. KITCHEN. MORNING.**
Rain-grey light. MARK, in hi-vis, home from his shift, frowns at the bolt at the top of the back door.

**MARK:** Top bolt was open again.

**KATE** *(at the stove, cardigan, not turning)*: Wasn't me.

**AVA:** Not me.

**OLLIE** *(eyes on his cereal)*: Mummy, more milk?

Under the table, Ava plays the recording into the translator app. DETECTED: POLISH.
*On screen:* "Mama, the water's at the door." … "You promised you wouldn't let go."

**AVA:** Ollie. What's "woda"?

**OLLIE:** What's what?

Ava turns the phone toward her mother.

**AVA:** Mum, this is Polish, isn't it?

Rain begins to drum on the window. Kate stops, mug in her right hand. A pale scar runs across her palm.

*FLASH (2008): a small hand slides out of a woman's hand, above black water.*

Kate puts the mug down.

**KATE:** I've forgotten all that, love.

She turns away. Mark, yawning, pauses at the stairs. Behind him, through the window: the garden, a low fence, a gate, and the river, high and brown. A small white camera sits under the eave.

**MARK:** Nights again. Top bolt, every night. River's right there.

**INT. OLLIE'S BEDROOM. EVENING.**
Ava tucks Ollie in. He points to the little camera on his shelf.

**OLLIE:** Daddy says it hears me if I'm scared.

**AVA:** Then you're never on your own.

**OLLIE:** Three knocks?

**AVA:** Three knocks.

**INT. AVA'S BEDROOM. NIGHT.**
Her phone is propped on the open sill, recording. Ava sits on her bed in her hoodie, wide awake. The clock reads 2:58.

3:00.

**OLLIE (O.S.) [PL provisional L1]:** *Mamo, woda jest przy drzwiach.*
*Subtitle:* Mama, the water's at the door.

She knocks. Nothing. Footsteps. The bolt. Ava goes to the window.

**EXT. GARDEN, FROM AVA'S WINDOW. NIGHT.**
Rain. The lawn falls toward the river. Past the reach of the garden lamp, at the gate, a pale tall shape and a small one, barely there.

A woman's voice tears through the rain.

**WOMAN (O.S.) [PL provisional L4]:** *Weź go!*

On the sill, the translator writes: **TAKE HIM!**

**OLLIE (O.S.) [PL provisional L5]:** *Obiecałaś, że nie puścisz.*
*Subtitle:* You promised you wouldn't let go.

Ava stumbles back and dials DAD. It rings, and rings.

**MARK (V.O., VOICEMAIL):** It's Mark, leave a—

She hangs up. Downstairs, the back door. Heavy footsteps up the stairs. A door closes. A long silence. Then small, wet footsteps.

**INT. OLLIE'S BEDROOM. NIGHT.**
Ollie is in bed, eyes closed, duvet tucked to his chin. Ava lifts the duvet. His feet are wet, blades of grass stuck to them. On his wrist, red marks: fingers.

He murmurs without opening his eyes.

**OLLIE:** Not tonight, Mama. I'm tired.

**AVA** *(a whisper)*: …Mama?

She turns to the dark doorway behind her.

CUT TO BLACK. *End card: PART 2: THE WATER GAME*
