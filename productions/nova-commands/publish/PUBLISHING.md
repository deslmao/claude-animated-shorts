## Cover
**File:** `cover.jpg` (1080 × 1920). One design for all three apps.

**What it shows:** a frame from the ending. Pip, in her yellow coat, looks up at Nova, who she no longer knows, in the
freed, colourful street. On a dark band below:
- "WHAT IF YOU COULD COMMAND ANYONE?";
- **"SHE SAVED EVERYONE."** in white;
- **"NOBODY REMEMBERS HER."** in gold.

Nothing is painted in.

**Placement:** the faces sit at about y 800–1050 and the text at about y 1235–1500, inside Instagram's 3:4 grid crop
(y 240–1680).

**How to set it:**
- **YouTube Shorts:** upload `cover.jpg` if offered. Otherwise choose the frame at **1:58**.
- **TikTok:** in "Edit cover", add `cover.jpg` from your photos.
- **Instagram Reels:** in "Edit cover", choose "Add from camera roll" and pick `cover.jpg`.
