## Cover
**File:** `cover.jpg` (1080 × 1920). One design for all three apps.

**What it shows:** a frame from the film: seat 13's empty bench under its "13" plate, the window beside it fogged
from the inside, a rider in the next row. On a dark band below: "NOBODY SITS IN" / **"SEAT 13"** in school-bus yellow
/ "Not even when the bus is packed." It gives nothing away. Nothing is painted in.

**Placement:**
- The text sits at about y 1210–1630, inside Instagram's 3:4 grid crop (y 240–1680).
- Inside the player, the apps' bottom text can cover the last line. "SEAT 13" stays clear.

**How to set it:**
- **YouTube Shorts:** upload `cover.jpg` if the Details page offers it. Otherwise choose the frame at **0:08**.
- **TikTok:** in "Edit cover", add `cover.jpg` from your photos, or pick the frame at **0:08**.
- **Instagram Reels:** in "Edit cover", choose "Add from camera roll" and pick `cover.jpg`.

## Before you post
1. **The captions and the time card are burned in.** Don't turn on the apps' auto-captions.
2. **AI disclosure.** The voices are synthetic (ElevenLabs).
   - **YouTube:** the altered-content label is for realistic footage, so "No" is reasonable for this cartoon.
   - **TikTok and Instagram:** the AI-generated label is the transparent choice.
3. **Music and effects** are original (synthesised in-house), so no copyright claim is expected.
4. **Post one Short a day at most,** at a consistent time (early afternoon US Eastern is a good default).
5. **First-day check:** look at "stayed to watch" in YouTube Studio. Above 70% means the opening works.
