# What If… You Could Stop Time? · Part 1: Sixty Years (viewer script for the owner's approval)

**Series:** "What If…" (Thinkora). An original cyberpunk love story in two parts. Part 1 runs about 1:45 and ends on a
cliffhanger and a vote.

**Narrator:** Theo, 17, telling his own story. **Sol, 17**, is a hacker with a glowing visor. Each speaker has their own
voice and caption colour.

**Premise:** in a neon city where the rich live above the smog, a poor boy gets an illegal implant that stops time,
but every second costs a day of his life. Then he falls for a girl who dreams of seeing the sea.

**The rule (said at 0:40):** squeeze your fist and time stops. Every second costs one day of your life. A counter on
your wrist shows the days you have left.

---

## COLD OPEN · 0:00
*(Neon rain. A bullet frozen in mid-air, an inch from a girl in a glowing visor. The raindrops hang still. The world
is drained to blue, except Theo, walking through the frozen rain toward her.)*
> **THEO:** Imagine you could stop time.
> **THEO:** Every second costs you a day of your life.
> **THEO:** This is the girl I spent them on.

*(Card: WHAT IF… YOU COULD STOP TIME? · PART 1)*

## THE CITY · 0:10
*(The Towers, gold and clean, rise above a layer of brown smog. Below: the Lowline, all rain, cables, neon and crowds.)*
> **THEO:** In Vantage City, the rich live in the Towers, above the smog.
> **THEO:** The rest of us live under it, in the Lowline. Nobody down here has ever seen the sky.

*(A tiny flat. Mum in her medic jacket at the door, a hand on Theo's head.)*
> **MUM:** Eat something. I'll be back by sunrise.
> **THEO:** You always say that.
> **MUM:** And I always am.

*(Rain on the window. Sirens. Close-up card: a hospital screen, COVERAGE REQUIRED.)*
> **THEO:** She wasn't. She got caught in a street fight.
> **THEO:** The hospital wanted a coverage card before they'd touch her. She didn't have one.

*(Her locker. Inside, in a case marked MILITARY: a sleek black chip.)*
> **THEO:** In her locker, I found something she was hiding. A stolen military implant.

## THE IMPLANT · 0:38
*(A back-alley clinic, sparks. A number lights up on his wrist: 21,915.)*
> **THEO:** I put it in. A number lit up on my wrist. Twenty-one thousand days. Sixty years.
> **THEO:** Squeeze my fist, and time stops. Every second I hold it, the number drops by one day.

## SOL · 0:50
*(The cold open again, from the start: the bullet frozen in front of the girl. Theo pulls her clear and lets go of
his fist. Time crashes back, and the bullet hits the wall behind her.)*
> **SOL:** …How did you do that? You were over there. Then you weren't.
> **THEO:** Lucky, I guess.
> **THEO (narrating):** Her name was Sol. She could hack anything with a screen. She ran with a crew that stole from
> the Towers.
> **SOL:** We could use someone who's that lucky.

## THE ROOFTOP · 1:05
*(A rooftop at night above the neon. Sol shows Theo a cracked holo-photo: a blue sea, a white beach.)*
> **SOL:** That's the sea. A real one. Past the wall, past the smog.
> **THEO:** Have you ever seen it?
> **SOL:** Not yet. One day I'm going to stand in it. And take this visor off, so I see it with my own eyes.
> **THEO:** Then I'm coming with you.
> **SOL** *(smiling)*: Is that a promise?
> **THEO:** It's a promise.

## THE CREW · 1:20
*(A montage of heists: neon, a van, Dex, the crew's big driver, laughing at the wheel. Theo stops time on every job.
The counter drops each time: 18,240… 9,600… 2,100.)*
> **DEX:** Kid, whatever you're doing, keep doing it!
> **THEO:** Every job, something went wrong. Every time, I stopped it. And every time, the number got smaller.

## THE PROMISE · 1:30
*(Sol grabs Theo's wrist and sees the number. Her voice breaks.)*
> **SOL:** Theo… what is this number?
> **THEO:** It's how many days I have left.
> **SOL:** Then stop. Promise me you'll stop. I don't want the sea without you.
> **THEO:** I promise.
> **THEO (narrating):** I meant it. I really did.

## THE CLIFFHANGER · 1:42
*(Sol's visor lights up with a message from Dex: ONE LAST JOB. TWO TICKETS OUT. TONIGHT. Close-up on Theo's wrist.)*
> **THEO:** I had forty-one days left.

*(Silent end card: PART 2 · FORTY-ONE DAYS · Would you keep your promise? 1 = KEEP IT · 2 = BREAK IT 👇)*
