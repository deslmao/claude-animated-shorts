# What If… You Could Stop Time? · Part 1: The Knock (viewer script v2, for the owner's approval)

**The series is now four parts.** Each part runs about 1:40, and every beat is shown on screen, not only told.

| Part | Title | What happens | It ends on |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Knock | Mum, the officer at the door, grief, Theo quits school, the implant | the frozen bullet, an inch from a stranger |
| 2 | The Girl in the Rain | saving Sol (the whole part), who she is, the rooftop, the sea, the promise | "For the first time since Mum died, I wanted to see tomorrow." |
| 3 | Sixty Years | the crew, the jobs, the counter falling, "promise me you'll stop", the vault | a gun at Sol's head and 41 days left (the vote) |
| 4 | Forty-One Days | the minute, the train, the sea | the ending |

**Narrator:** Theo, 17, telling his own story. Each speaker has their own voice and caption colour.

**The look of stopped time (the same every time):**
- The whole world turns blue and stops: the rain hangs in the air, people stop mid-step and the neon stops flickering.
- Only Theo stays in full colour.

---

## COLD OPEN · 0:00
*(Neon rain, frozen blue. A glowing bullet hangs in the air an inch from a girl's face. Theo, in full colour, walks
toward it through the hanging rain.)*
> **THEO:** Imagine you could stop time. Every second costs you a day of your life.
> **THEO:** A year ago, I didn't care how many days I had left.

*(Card: WHAT IF… YOU COULD STOP TIME? · PART 1)*

## THE CITY · 0:10
*(Wide: the gold Towers above a layer of brown smog. Push down into the Lowline: rain, crowds, neon.)*
> **THEO:** In Vantage City, the rich live in the Towers, above the smog. The rest of us live under it, in the Lowline.
> **THEO:** It was just me and my mum. She was a medic on the night shift.

## MUM · 0:20
*(Their tiny flat, evening. Mum puts on her red medic coat at the door. Theo is at the table with his schoolbag.)*
> **MUM:** Eat something. And go to school tomorrow. I mean it.
> **THEO:** You always say that.
> **MUM:** And I'll be back by sunrise. I always am.

*(She ruffles his hair, smiles, and goes. The door clicks shut.)*

## THE KNOCK · 0:33
*(Grey dawn light through the window. Theo has fallen asleep at the table, waiting. A knock. He jumps up, grinning.)*
> **THEO:** You forgot your key again…

*(He opens the door. It isn't Mum. It's a police officer, holding his cap in both hands.)*
> **THEO:** At sunrise, someone knocked. It wasn't her.
> **OFFICER:** I'm sorry, son. There was a street fight. Your mum stopped to help someone.
> **OFFICER:** The hospital wouldn't treat her without a coverage card. She didn't make it.
> **THEO:** Nobody in the Lowline has a coverage card. She spent her life saving people the hospitals turned away.
> And they turned her away.

*(Theo's face falls. He slides down the door frame to the floor. The officer looks away. Only the rain.)*

## NOTHING MATTERS · 0:55
*(A slow montage:*
- *her red coat still on its hook;*
- *Theo in bed in the daylight, not moving;*
- *an empty desk at school;*
- *Theo walking out of the school gate.)*

> **THEO:** After that, nothing mattered. I stopped eating. I stopped answering anyone.
> **THEO:** My teacher saw me walking out.
> **TEACHER:** Theo! If you walk out now, don't come back.
> **THEO:** I didn't.

## THE LOCKER · 1:10
*(Mum's locker at the clinic. Theo clears it out. Her medic badge, a photo of the two of them, and a black case marked
MILITARY. Inside: a sleek chip and a note in her handwriting.)*
> **THEO:** In her locker, I found something she was hiding. A stolen military implant. Someone she'd patched up had
> paid her with it.
> **THEO:** And a note. *"Theo. Never use this."*

## THE IMPLANT · 1:20
*(A back-alley clinic, sparks. The doc is a scruffy man with goggles. A number lights up on Theo's forearm: 21,915.)*
> **THEO:** I didn't care what it did. A back-alley doc put it in for me.
> **DOC:** Squeeze your fist, and time stops. But every second you hold it, that number drops by one. That's one day
> of your life, kid.
> **THEO:** Twenty-one thousand days. Sixty years. It felt like too many anyway.

## THE GIRL · 1:33
*(That night, the Lowline street in the rain. Theo walks with no reason to be anywhere. Ahead, a girl with long pink
hair and a glowing band in it runs from a Tower guard. The guard raises a gun.)*
> **THEO:** That night, I saw a girl running from Tower security.
> **GUARD:** Stop!

*(A gunshot. Close-up: Theo's fist closes on instinct. The world freezes blue: the rain, the crowd, the bullet, an inch
from her face. Only Theo is in colour.)*
> **THEO:** I didn't think. I squeezed.

*(Cut to black on the heartbeat. Silent end card: PART 2 · THE GIRL IN THE RAIN · Would you spend a day of your life
on a stranger? 1 = YES · 2 = NO 👇)*

## What changes on screen (the animation)
- **Two to four shots per beat instead of one,** and slower:
  - a wide shot, then the faces close up, then a held reaction (the knock: Theo grinning, the door, the officer, Theo's
    face, the slide down);
  - holds of one or two seconds on the emotional faces, with no cut.
- **Every action happens on screen:**
  - the coat put on;
  - the hair ruffle;
  - the door shutting, the knock, the door opening;
  - the slide down the frame;
  - the gate;
  - the locker;
  - the fist closing.
- **Stopped time looks the same every time:** the world blue and still, and Theo in full colour.
- **New on screen:** the officer, the teacher, the school gate and an empty desk, the clinic locker, the back-alley doc,
  and a visible gun on the guard.
- **Kept:** Sol's new design, the city, the flat, the voices (Theo, Mum and the guard), and the sound and score
  approach. New voices: the officer, the teacher and the doc.
