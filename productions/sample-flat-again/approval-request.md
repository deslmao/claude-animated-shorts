# Flat Again: story approval

**Format:** immersive + mystery. The viewer is the victim of a small, strange, repeated act (tyres let down, caps left in a neat row), investigates it against a deadline, weighs two kinds of motive with clues they can check (a padlock, a key, a habit, a gesture), and catches the culprit in the act; the reveal turns every act of sabotage into an act of protection.

**Estimated length:** 75 s · **Words:** 215 · **Narration:** 1105 characters (about 1105 ElevenLabs credits) · **Voices:** narrator=georgeHushed, shah=georgeLively, dex=chris, grandad=brian, mum=jessica, you=liam

## Premise
- **question:** Someone keeps letting your bike tyres down before dawn, and leaves the valve caps standing in a neat little row. Who is doing it, and why?
- **protagonist:** you (the viewer), thirteen, with a paper round, living with Mum and Grandad Ray
- **want:** to keep the paper round: three late starts and it goes to Dex
- **fear:** that someone is out to get you, and that it might be someone at home
- **protects:** your round, and your pride
- **decision:** to sit up in the dark and catch whoever it is in the act, instead of hiding the bike
- **loses:** the round (shown: Mr. Shah counting your lates on his fingers); and, without knowing it, your safety on Gallows Hill
- **obstacle:** whoever does it has a key, and the one person who knows why can't say it
- **conflict:** A, someone wants you to lose the round (Dex, who wants it, or Mum, who thinks it's wrecking your sleep); B, someone is trying to keep you off the bike, and can't tell you why
