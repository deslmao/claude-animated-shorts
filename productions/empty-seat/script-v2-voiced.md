# The Empty Seat, v2: the voiced-scene format (draft for the owner's approval)

**Why v2:** the owner chose to switch to the format of the viral reference
(`productions/research/viral-breakdown-mr-gray.md`):
- a voice cast instead of a narrator;
- a first-person camera ("you" are the kid whose eyes we see through);
- a cold open;
- rules stated in dialogue;
- a planted clue;
- a lighting arc;
- a silent end card.

**The story is unchanged** (the approved v1, `script.md`). It's the same events in the same order, now acted.

**Length:** about 245 spoken words in about 1:55, around 130 words a minute. That leaves room for the acted silences,
each filled with sound and action, never a held picture.

## Script
**COLD OPEN (0:00–0:05).** Inside the bus. The windows have gone white with snow. The light is cold, dark blue, and
your breath fogs. A small handprint SLAPS the back window from outside.

> **PRIYA** (a whisper, right beside you): Don't look at the window. Just count.

**CARD:** THREE WEEKS EARLIER · MONDAY 7:52 AM · BUS 9

---

**SCENE 1. The bus, a bright June morning.** Your POV: your hands on your backpack, kids chattering. The empty bench
at row 7, its window fogged.

> **NOAH** (new kid, red hoodie, cheerful, dropping into seat 13): Is anyone sitting here?
>
> *(The chatter stops dead. Every head turns.)*
>
> **PRIYA** (whisper): He sat in it.
>
> **YOU** (thought): (Nobody sits in thirteen. Not even when it's packed.)
>
> *(Air brakes. The bus pulls over. Mr Hale turns round in his seat.)*
>
> **MR HALE** (quiet, not angry): Not that one, son. That's Emily's.
>
> **NOAH**: Who's Emily?
>
> *(No answer. Noah moves.)*
>
> **YOU** (thought, staring at the window): (It's June. So why is her window always fogged up?)

**SCENE 2. Elm Road.** The bus stops at an empty stop. The doors hiss open. Nobody's there.

> **MR HALE** (under his breath): One. Two. Three.
>
> *(The doors fold shut.)*
>
> **YOU** (thought): (Every single morning. And nobody's ever there.)

**SCENE 3. Lunch, your phone, then the library.** Your POV: you type "elm road bus stop". The result: *Elm Rd stop:
closed 1996.*

> **SAM** (leaning over your shoulder): Closed because of her.
>
> *(The 1996 yearbook, a page with a black border: EMILY SHAW, aged 11.)*
>
> **SAM**: Snowstorm. The bus was late. The driver didn't see her at the stop and drove right past. She tried to
> walk to school.
>
> **YOU**: And?
>
> **SAM**: She never got there.
>
> **YOU** (thought): (The driver… was Mr Hale.)

**SCENE 4. The next mornings.** Mr Hale stops at Elm Road, counts to three, and glances up at seat 13 in his mirror.

> **MR HALE** (softly, to the mirror): Morning, Emily.

**SCENE 5. A Monday, three weeks later.** A different driver.

> **THE RADIO** (crackle): Bus nine, Ron Hale's in hospital. You're covering his route.
>
> **STAND-IN DRIVER**: Copy that.
>
> **YOU** (thought, as the Elm Road sign gets closer): (She doesn't know.)
>
> **YOU** (aloud): Miss? You have to stop at Elm Road.
>
> **STAND-IN DRIVER** (chewing gum): There's no stop at Elm Road, hon.
>
> *(The stop flashes past. Silence. Then: SLAP, on the back window. A small handprint. The windows turn white, the
> light drains to cold blue, and snow pours past outside.)*
>
> **PRIYA** (whisper): It's snowing. It's June.
>
> *(SLAP. SLAP.)*
>
> **YOU** (running up the aisle): STOP! Go back! Please!
>
> *(Air brakes. The doors hiss open on the empty stop. Freezing wind.)*
>
> **YOU** (a whisper): One. Two. Three.
>
> *(The doors close. The snow stops. Sunlight floods back in. Seat 13's window fogs up again.)*

**SCENE 6. That night.** Your POV: your phone buzzes. A text from Sam: *mr hale died last night. in hospital. he was
really sick.*

**SCENE 7. The next morning.** The bus is bright, with kids chattering. Two benches are empty now: the windows by seats
13 *and* 14 are fogged.

> **YOU** (thought): (He's riding with her now. So she never gets left behind again.)
>
> *(A kid goes to sit in 14.)*
>
> **PRIYA** (a whisper, the same voice as the cold open): Don't. That's Mr Hale's.

**END CARD** (silent, 2 s): **WOULD YOU SIT IN SEAT 13?** YES / NO · comment below

## Story check
- **The same story as the approved v1.** The only additions are cast voices, and Sam explains 1996 in dialogue (the
  reference's "There's no Mr. Gray. It's a code." move).
- **Mr Hale's death is plainly illness:** "in hospital", "he was really sick". Seat 14's meaning is said in your
  thought.
- **The planted clue:** "Morning, Emily" (Scene 4) shows that he treats her as a rider. That's why he rides with her
  at the end.
- **The cold open is the climax's image** (the snow and the handprint). The last line echoes the first in Priya's voice.

## Design: matching and beating the reference
| The reference | The Empty Seat v2 |
|---|---|
| A flat pastel daylight classroom | A **lighting arc in three states:** warm June sun through the windows (safe), then the snow storm (the windows go white, the light drains to cold blue, frost creeps in at the window edges, breath fog), then the sun floods back. Final: warm again, with two fogged windows. |
| Glowing eyes in the dark | **Fog and frost as the ghost's signature:** her window fogs from the inside, and the frost spreads when she's near. Emily is never shown, only her handprint and her breath on the glass. |
| A POV with hands at the desk | **A POV with your hands:** gripping the backpack straps, holding the phone (texts and a search with a typing animation), on the seat back as you run. |
| A time card | A **title card** in the channel style: a typewriter reveal, "THREE WEEKS EARLIER · MONDAY 7:52 AM · BUS 9" |
| Colour-coded captions | **Colour-coded captions per speaker,** bracketed thoughts, and the radio in its own box with a crackle |
| Intercom, chime and screech | **A bus radio** with a filtered voice, a crackle and a squelch tone. Also the doors' hiss, the air brakes, the indicator, the bus rumble. |
| A drone, then a build | **Emily's music-box theme** (recurring), plus a **drone** under the Elm Road stops and a **build** from the skipped stop to the slap. Then **total silence** for "One. Two. Three.", and the theme in major for the ending. |
| A phone call UI | **Phone sequences:** a search result, the yearbook photo, Sam's text with a typing indicator |
| A silent end card | A silent end card: WOULD YOU SIT IN SEAT 13? |

## What gets built, after approval
