# Round 4 critic report: "GTA 6, Explained to Your Girlfriend · Ep 1"

Judged on the 20 full-resolution frames, motion.jpg and the two REF sheets only. 8 = matches the reference.

## Scores

| Category | Score | Why |

## Hard fails

1. **Frame 01 (f0): the prop isn't held.** Lucy's phone floats on her jacket front at chest height while both hands rest at her sides below it, not touching it. The motion strip shows the same in frames 0-15. This is the very first frame of the film.
2. **Frame 18 (f1320): a face is cut by the frame edge.** Lucy is sliced in half by the left edge: one eye and half her mouth are off-frame.

Other flags (not counted as hard fails):
- **f1000:** the top receipt line ("GTA 6 $69.99") is cut by the top edge. This is probably an intentional scroll, but the half-letters read as a crop error.
- **f300, f330:** the controller lies on the couch beside Gordon's hand rather than in it. Gordon also has no visible thighs or seat contact, so he floats above the cushion.
- **f215, f1320:** the hands and controller are cut at the bottom edge. At f1320 the moon is also cut at the right edge.
- **f1180:** the fishing rod is a short stick with no line. The reel disc sits beside the fist rather than in it.
- **f1210:** the raised arm sprouts from behind the head, which looks anatomically odd. Gordon's legs run off the bottom of the frame.
- **f40:** the hook banner overlaps the phone insert's chat area. This is tolerable, but the phone is cramped by it.

## Top fixes (weakest category first)

1. **Motion (4): make bodies and the world move in every character shot.**
   - **f0-f40:** Lucy should lift the phone into both hands, glance down and type. Fixing the hands also fixes the f0 hard fail.
   - **f215, f845, f1320 (close-ups):** add a slow push-in or arc, plus a head turn or lean, so the faces aren't the only thing changing.
   - **f1120-f1240 (game shots):** add moving traffic, swaying palms and drifting water, and give Gordon an actual action: cast the rod with a line (f1180), dribble or shoot (f1210), walk into frame (f1120).
   - **Inserts:** give each one a camera move or a physical animation (the receipt printing, the calendar page flipping, the stamp slamming with a shake).
2. **Light and world (5): stage the night flat and the inserts as lit 3D sets.**
   - **f215, f300, f330, f845, f1320, f1540:** darken the room. Add a strong coloured TV key from the front-left (blue/cyan) with falloff and a warm practical from the lamp, and rim the characters.
   - **f1120, f1180, f1210 (day exteriors):** add sun-side shading and a contact shadow to the characters to match f1240.
   - **Inserts:** put the phone, calendar, receipt and poster on real surfaces (a desk, a wall, a hand) as the REF does with the dollar bill and the CCTV wall, instead of on blank gradients.
   - **f1180:** replace the flat pier water and placeholder boat.
3. **Camera and the hard fails: re-frame the tight shots and vary the angles.**
   - **f1320:** widen or reframe so Lucy is either fully in or fully out (no half face).
   - **f215, f1320:** pull back enough that the hands and controller are inside the frame.
   - **f300/f330:** make the second shot a real new angle (an over-shoulder from behind the couch, or a low angle from the coffee table), not the same frame.
   - **f300/f330/f1540:** fix Gordon's seated pose with visible thighs on the cushion and the controller in both hands.
   - **Captions:** lift the caption off the graphics in f420, f700 and f1540, and raise its size by about 25%.
