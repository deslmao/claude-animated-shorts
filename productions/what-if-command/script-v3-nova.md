# What If… you could command anyone, but they'd forget you? (draft v3 for the owner's approval)

**Series:** "What If…" (Thinkora). Emotional dystopia, about 2:30.

**Narrator:** Nova, 15, telling the audience her own story in the first person. One voice carries the whole film.

**Premise (one sentence):** in a town split between the rich Blues and the starving Greys, a Grey girl wakes up able to
command anyone by saying her own name, but everyone she commands forgets she exists.

**The rule (said by Nova at 0:55):** "Nova commands you…": anyone who hears it obeys. Then they forget me. Forever.

**The look:** the Greys' side is desaturated. The Blues' side glows warm and gold behind the wall. Colour floods the
town only at the end.

**Characters:**
| Character | Who they are |
|---|---|
| Nova | the narrator |
| Pip, 8 | her little sister |
| Mum | cleans on the Blue side |
| Dad | taken two winters ago |
| Kai | Nova's best friend |
| the Blue guard and the Blue officer | (one line each) |
| the Governor | on the screens |

---

## COLD OPEN · 0:00
*(Night. A curfew siren. A Blue guard drags Pip across the wet street by her grey wristband. Nova runs in front of
him. Her voice drops, shaking.)*
> **NOVA:** Nova commands you… to let her go. At this instant!

*(He lets go. His eyes go empty. He looks at Nova as if he's never seen her, and walks away.)*
> **NOVA (narrating):** That was the first time I used it. I didn't know yet what it would cost me.

*(Typewriter card: WHAT IF… YOU COULD COMMAND ANYONE?)*

## MY TOWN · 0:14
*(A wide shot. A tall wall splits the town in two. Beyond it: gold lights, music, long tables of food. On our side:
grey streets, broken windows, a bread queue in the rain.)*
> **NOVA:** In my town, there are two kinds of people. The Blues… and us. The Greys.
> **NOVA:** The Blues have everything. Big warm houses. Food they throw away.
> **NOVA:** We have a grey band on our wrist, and a number instead of a name.

*(Mum, exhausted, scrubbing a golden dining table piled with cakes. A Blue family eats, not looking at her.)*
> **NOVA:** My mum cleans their tables twelve hours a day. She's not allowed to eat the crumbs.

*(The bread queue. The shutter slams down in front of Pip.)*
> **NOVA:** Every morning, the bread runs out before it gets to us.

*(Home. A tiny cold room. A photo of Dad on the wall.)*
> **NOVA:** Greys don't vote. Greys don't complain. Greys who complain… disappear.
> **NOVA:** Like my dad. They took him two winters ago.

*(In bed, in the dark. Pip whispers.)*
> **PIP:** Do you think Dad's coming back?
> **NOVA:** I didn't answer. I didn't know how.

## THE MORNING · 0:52
*(Dawn. Nova wakes with a gasp. Something hums in the air when she breathes.)*
> **NOVA:** Then one morning, I woke up… and my voice felt different. Heavy. Like thunder hiding in my throat.

*(The bread queue. A Blue officer pulls the shutter down again. Nova steps forward.)*
> **NOVA:** Nova commands you to give them the bread.

*(He opens the shutter and hands out every loaf, smiling, eyes empty.)*
> **NOVA:** When I say my name, and a command, anyone who hears it has to obey.
> **NOVA:** And then they forget me. Like I was never there.

*(That night, Pip, holding a whole loaf, happier than she's been in months.)*
> **PIP:** Promise you'll never forget me.
> **NOVA:** I promised. I never thought it would be the other way round.

## FIGHTING BACK · 1:15
*(A montage. Each command lands a low tone, then silence.)*
> **NOVA:** Nova commands you to open the warehouse.
> **NOVA (narrating):** A hundred families ate that night.
> **NOVA:** Nova commands you to burn the list.
> **NOVA (narrating):** The list of Greys they were taking next. Forty names. Forty families who got to stay.

*(Kai, Nova's best friend, sees her do it. Then his eyes go empty.)*
> **NOVA:** My best friend Kai saw me do it. The next morning, he walked past me at school.

*(Home, the kitchen. Mum looks up from the sink and smiles, politely, at a stranger.)*
> **MUM:** Sorry, love… are you one of Pip's friends?
> **NOVA (narrating, breaking):** My own mum. In our own kitchen. I made her tea, and she thanked me like a
> guest.

## THE TOWER · 1:45
*(The Governor on every screen, smiling. Behind him, on a guard's clipboard: a work camp roster. Dad's photo on it.)*
> **NOVA:** Then I found out where Dad went. Not gone. Working. In the Governor's camp, behind the wall.

*(The broadcast tower. Nova climbs the stairs. A microphone, live to every screen and speaker in town.)*
> **NOVA:** If every speaker in town heard me, I could end it. All of it.
> **NOVA:** But everyone who hears me forgets me. Everyone. Even Pip.

*(Cut: Pip at home, watching the screen, waiting for her sister to come home.)*

*(The tower. Nova breathes in.)*
> **NOVA:** Nova commands you… every one of you.
> **NOVA:** Open the gates. Share everything. And never call anyone a Grey again.

*(The silence in the music. Then, in every street, grey bands fall. The wall gates open. Colour floods the town, gold
and blue spilling onto the grey.)*

## THE ENDING · 2:10
*(Morning, the open gate. Dad walks out of the camp, thin and blinking at the light. Pip screams "DAD!" and runs into
his arms. Mum is behind her, crying.)*
> **NOVA:** Dad came home.

*(Pip, in a bright yellow coat, runs past Nova in the crowd. She stops. She looks up at her, a stranger.)*
> **PIP:** Sorry… do I know you?
> **NOVA:** No. But I know you.

*(Nova walks away through the bright, crowded street, alone.)*
> **NOVA:** I saved my whole town. And not one of them knows my name.
> **NOVA:** That's okay. I remember them.

*(Silent end card: WOULD YOU DO IT? · 1 = YES · 2 = NO 👇)*

## Clarity checklist
| The viewer must understand | Told by |
|---|---|
| the first frame | "Nova commands you… to let her go. At this instant!" + "the first time I used it" |
| that it costs something (the hook) | "I didn't know yet what it would cost me." |
| the two classes | "two kinds of people. The Blues… and us. The Greys." |
| how unfair it is | "Food they throw away." / "not allowed to eat the crumbs" |
| what happens to Greys | "Greys who complain… disappear. Like my dad." |
| where the power came from | "one morning, I woke up… and my voice felt different" |
| the rule | "When I say my name, and a command, anyone who hears it has to obey." |
| the cost | "And then they forget me." |
| the plant | "Promise you'll never forget me." |
| the cost growing | Kai walks past her; Mum: "are you one of Pip's friends?" |
| the stakes of the last command | "everyone who hears me forgets me. Everyone. Even Pip." |
| the payoffs | "Dad came home." / "Sorry… do I know you?" |

## Production notes
- **New sets:**
  - the grey street and bread queue;
  - the wall, with the gold Blue side beyond it;
  - a Blue dining hall;
  - the broadcast tower room;
  - the work-camp gate.
- **Reused:** the home bedroom and kitchen.
- **Grading:** the Grey side desaturated; colour arrives at the final command (an opt-in addition).
- **Sound:**
  - a command tone, then dead silence after each command;
  - rain, sirens, crowd;
  - the score: sad strings at first, hope at the morning, building at the tower, and a single piano for the end.
- **Length:** about 2:30. To hold viewers that long, a new beat lands every 8–12 s and nothing is explained twice.
