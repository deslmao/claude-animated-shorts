# The Skyscraper Fixed in Secret
format: action-first
why: The opening act (welders secretly repairing a brand-new skyscraper at night while thousands work in it by day) makes the viewer ask why; the mechanism (wind on the corners, joints changed from welds to bolts) is shown step by step, and a true modern reassessment recontextualises the danger without undoing the decision.
length: 85
voice: george
voices: hartley=jessica, lemessurier=brian
mood: curious
factual: yes

## Premise
- question: Why was a brand-new skyscraper being repaired in secret every night, and was it really about to fall?
- protagonist: William LeMessurier, the tower's structural engineer
- want: a safe building, and his reputation
- fear: that the tower he designed could blow over
- protects: the people inside and around the tower
- decision: tell the owners about his own mistake, though it could ruin him
- rule: none
- conflict: none
- action: in 1978, welders worked through the night inside a brand-new Manhattan skyscraper, behind plywood walls, while office workers came in each morning, unaware

## Writer notes
The ending is the real twist, and it's honest: modern reanalysis (NIST) suggests the danger was overstated. But the story
stays about the decision made under uncertainty. The risk is that the mechanism in beats 3 to 6 is dense: three ideas
(corner wind, bolts, damper power). Each one gets its own beat and image.
