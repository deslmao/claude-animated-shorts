Judged on the 20 full-resolution frames, the motion strip and the two reference sheets. 8 = matches the reference.

## Top fixes (weakest category first)

1. **Motion (f0–60, f1140–1160, f1395–1410, and every character shot).** Give each character shot at least one body action beyond the mouth. In the hook (f0), have Lucy pick up or tilt the phone, lean in and change her expression within the first 20 frames, rather than only a push-in. In the street walk (f1140+), track or dolly the camera with Gordon so the city parallaxes behind him. In Lucy's talking shot (f1395+), add arm gestures (open palms, point at Gordon). Replace the repeated arms-crossed and arms-down holds (f300, f525, f845) with distinct gestures.
2. **Light and world in real life (f300, f330, f845, f1320, f1540, f0).** Make the "night flat lit by a TV" actually TV-lit: a cool, flickering key from the TV direction on both faces, a warm practical from the pendant lamp, a darker room falloff, and a rim on the characters. Fix the f215 vertical seam. Put the flat inserts (calendar f420, receipt f1000, view counter f700, phone f40/f110/f1430) into the 3D room: on the coffee table, in a hand, or on the TV, with real light and shadow, as in REF 3_18. Populate the pier in f1180: a water texture, boats, a skyline, a railing.
3. **Clean the hard fails before anything else.** f0: re-seat Lucy so the duvet doesn't cut through her, and fix the floating hand bar. f1320 and f1410: reframe or move Lucy so her face is either fully in frame or fully out. f700: move "Trailer 1" above the thumbnail or below the counter. f1180: remove or move the tan slab behind Gordon's head and keep the rod tip in frame. f330: pull out so Lucy's raised hand stays in frame.
