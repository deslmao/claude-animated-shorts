# What If… you could stop time, but every second cost a day of your life? (draft for the owner's approval)

**Series:** "What If…" (Thinkora). A cyberpunk love story, about 2:00. Original: our city, characters, implant and
ending.

**Narrator:** Theo, 17, telling his own story in the first person, the way Nova did.

**Premise (one sentence):** in a neon city where the rich live above the smog, a poor boy gets an illegal implant
that stops time, but every second costs a day of his life, and he spends them to give the girl he loves the one thing
she's never had: the sea.

**The rule (said at 0:10):** squeeze your fist and time stops. Every second you hold it costs one day off the end of
your life. A counter on your wrist shows how many days you have left.

**The look:**
- Vantage City, 2081, the Lowline: neon rain, holo-ads, chrome.
- Stopped time: the rain freezes in the air, and the colour drains to blue, except Theo and whatever he touches.
- The ending is the only daylight in the film.

**Characters:**
| Character | Who they are |
|---|---|
| Theo | the narrator |
| Sol, 17 | a hacker with a glowing visor |
| Mum | a night-shift medic |
| Dex | the crew's big-hearted driver |
| a Tower guard | one line |

---

## COLD OPEN · 0:00
*(Neon street, rain. A bullet hangs in the air, frozen, an inch from a girl in a glowing visor. The raindrops are
stopped all around her. Theo walks through the frozen rain and pulls her out of the way.)*
> **THEO:** Imagine you could stop time.
> **THEO:** Every second costs you a day of your life.
> **THEO:** I spent my last ones on her.

*(Card: WHAT IF… YOU COULD STOP TIME?)*

## THE CITY · 0:12
*(The Towers rise above the smog, gold and clean. Far below, the Lowline: rain, cables, crowds, neon.)*
> **THEO:** In Vantage City, the rich live in the Towers, above the smog. The rest of us live underneath it, in the
> Lowline.
> **THEO:** Nobody down here has ever seen a blue sky.

*(Mum in her medic jacket, kissing Theo's forehead at the door.)*
> **THEO:** My mum was a medic on the night shift. She fixed people the hospitals turned away.

*(Flash: sirens, a street fight, a stretcher. A hospital desk: "COVERAGE REQUIRED".)*
> **THEO:** One night, she got caught in a street fight. The hospital asked for her coverage card before they'd help.
> She didn't have one.

*(Theo alone in their tiny flat. Her locker. Inside: a sleek black chip in a case marked MILITARY.)*
> **THEO:** In her locker, I found what she'd been hiding for someone. A stolen military implant.

## THE IMPLANT · 0:40
*(A back-alley clinic. Sparks. Theo's arm. A counter glows on his wrist: 21,915.)*
> **THEO:** I put it in. A number lit up on my wrist. Twenty-one thousand days. Sixty years.
> **THEO:** Squeeze my fist, and time stops. Every second I hold it, the number goes down by one day.

## SOL · 0:52
*(The frozen bullet, again: the cold open, now seen from the start. Theo pulls Sol out of the way. Time starts. The
bullet hits the wall behind her.)*
> **THEO:** The first time I used it, I saved a stranger.
> **SOL:** …How did you do that?
> **THEO:** Her name was Sol. She could hack anything with a screen. She ran with a crew that stole from the Towers.

*(A rooftop at night. Sol shows Theo a cracked holo-photo: blue sea, a white beach.)*
> **SOL:** That's the sea. A real one. Past the wall, past the smog.
> **SOL:** One day I'm going to stand in it.
> **THEO:** She'd never seen the sky. Neither had I. I wanted to give her the sea.

## THE CREW · 1:15
*(Montage: heists, neon, a van, Dex laughing at the wheel, Theo stopping time in the middle of a job. Each time, the
counter drops: 18,240… 9,600… 2,100.)*
> **THEO:** We did job after job. Every time something went wrong, I stopped time.
> **THEO:** And every time, the number on my wrist got smaller.

*(Sol grabs his wrist and sees the number. Her face falls.)*
> **SOL:** Theo… what is this?
> **THEO:** I told her the truth. She made me promise to stop using it.
> **THEO:** I promised.

## THE LAST JOB · 1:35
*(The Tower vault. Sol at the console. Guards burst in, guns raised.)*
> **THEO:** The last job was for two tickets out of the city. On a train to the sea.
> **TOWER GUARD:** Drop it!
> **THEO:** They were going to shoot her. There were too many of them. To save her, I'd need a whole minute.
> *(Close: the counter reads 41.)*
> **THEO:** I had forty-one days left.

*(Theo squeezes his fist. The world freezes blue. He walks through the frozen guards and carries Sol out. The
counter falls: 40… 20… 3… 1.)*

## THE ENDING · 1:52
*(The only daylight in the film. A train window, then a white beach, then a real blue sea. Sol stands in the water,
crying and smiling, holding the second ticket.)*
> **THEO:** She made it to the sea.
> *(Sol looks at the empty seat beside her on the train, then at the waves.)*
> **THEO:** I didn't. But for one second, I stopped time. Just to watch her see it.

*(Silent end card: WOULD YOU SPEND YOUR LIFE ON SOMEONE? · 1 = YES · 2 = NO 👇)*

## Production notes
- **New world:** the Lowline street (neon, rain, holo-ads, cables), the Towers above the smog, a back-alley clinic,
  a rooftop, the Tower vault, the train and the beach.
- **New effect (opt-in):** stopped time. The rain freezes in mid-air, the frame turns cold blue and the background
  sound cuts to a low hum.
- **Sound (ElevenLabs):**
  - a deep time-stop "whoomp" and a heartbeat slowing in stopped time;
  - neon hum, rain, the crowd;
  - the waves at the end;
  - a synthwave and strings score from Eleven Music.
- **Voices:** Theo (a young male voice, distinct from George), Sol, Mum, Dex, a guard. Each has their own caption
  colour.
