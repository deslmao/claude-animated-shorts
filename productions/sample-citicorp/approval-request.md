# The Skyscraper Fixed in Secret: story approval

**Format:** action-first. The opening act (welders secretly repairing a brand-new skyscraper at night while thousands work in it by day) makes the viewer ask why; the mechanism (wind on the corners, joints changed from welds to bolts) is shown step by step, and a true modern reassessment recontextualises the danger without undoing the decision.

**Estimated length:** 78 s · **Words:** 240 · **Narration:** 1354 characters (about 1354 ElevenLabs credits) · **Voices:** narrator=george, hartley=jessica

## Premise
- **question:** Why was a brand-new skyscraper being repaired in secret every night, and was it really about to fall?
- **protagonist:** William LeMessurier, the tower's structural engineer
- **want:** a safe building, and his reputation
- **fear:** that the tower he designed could blow over
- **protects:** the people inside and around the tower
- **decision:** tell the owners about his own mistake, though it could ruin him
- **action:** in 1978, welders worked through the night inside a brand-new Manhattan skyscraper, behind plywood walls, while office workers came in each morning, unaware
