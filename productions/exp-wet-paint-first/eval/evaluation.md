### plot hole
- applies: yes, it's a mystery with a deduction and a hidden culprit
- result: weak
- beats: 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9
- finding: Phase 1 (I5): (1) Mira now asks the obvious witness at once (2), and the answer is a line the sheet later exposes, so that's fixed. (5) Leo's timeline is fixed: at the gate after school, and in the hall at 6:45 Thursday. (6) The deletion is replaced by an apology to the same chat. A painted girl alone on a bench, next to the actual girl on the actual bench, a tipped tray, someone with a camera, and it takes her four days?" The story does answer it ("You don't remember, do you?", and the bully forgetting is the point), but the "Bench Girl" label makes the forgetting look like stupidity rather than indifference. ## Overall
- keep-watching: yes. A tiny new figure on your prize mural, still wet, with a new panel each day, is a strong pull, and the "why" plus the camera plant carry it past the point where viewers have guessed who. The likeliest drop-off is beats 6 and 7 (camera map, thumbprint, sheets), where the viewer is confirming what they already suspect. Viewers who reach beat 9 stay to the end.
- verdict: ready
- top-fixes: 1. Change the "Bench Girl" caption (or add a dismissed half-recognition at beat 5), so Mira's four-day blindness reads as indifference, not stupidity. 2. Tighten the confirmation middle: merge beat 6's three clues into one and use the seconds for a brief doubt about Asha or a longer hold on the beat 9 reveal. 3. Cut descriptive lines ("I look down at my camera", "A fourth picture", "reads all five pictures"), and make the painted strap and the sheet times big and long enough to read at phone size.
