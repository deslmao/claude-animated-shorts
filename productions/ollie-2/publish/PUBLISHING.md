3. **AI disclosure.** The voices, effects and music are generated (ElevenLabs).

   - **TikTok and Instagram:** the AI-generated label is the transparent choice.
4. **Music and effects** were generated on your ElevenLabs plan (Eleven Music and Sound Effects). ElevenLabs licenses
   paid-plan output for commercial use, so no copyright claim is expected.
5. **Post one Short a day at most.** Count the 1s and 2s across all three platforms for Part 3.
