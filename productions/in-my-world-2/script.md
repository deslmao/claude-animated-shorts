## Why this one hits
- **Irony:** the best arguer in the house has the worst number. **Unfair system:** Mum's "highest number wins", enforced without mercy. **Smug rival:** Dex, an eight "since Tuesday".
- **The loophole is a 6 and a 9:** it is in the rule's own wording, the planted habit (the headstand Mum calls pointless) is the only thing that wins, and the audience sees the 6 before they hear the answer.
- **The humbling:** the rival copies and fails, because an 8 is the same both ways. Final line is a rule-tied button, not a CTA.
