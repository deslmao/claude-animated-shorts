# The Last Train: the script in the owner's own words (v2, for approval)

**Source:** the owner's own telling of the story (21 Nov 2026). **Rule for this version:** the owner's exact words,
slang and phrasing. Only the order is fixed and repeats are cut. Nothing is added that the owner didn't say.

**What changed from the current preview's narration:**
- **Cut, because the owner didn't say them:**
  - "Imagine…"
  - "It's 9:47"
  - "I was just going home! Just like you!"
  - the wet coat
  - the empty carriages one by one
  - "This is your stop"
  - "You've ridden this line every day for years"
- **Cut as repeats:**
  - "He SCREAMS 'WHY DID THIS HAPPEN TO ME WHY ME'": he screams it himself on frame 0.
  - "then a jumpscare happens": the jumpscare is shown.
- **Order:** the scream moves to the very start (frame 0, the hook). Everything else stays in the owner's order.

The shots, the sounds and the ending stay as they are in the preview. The lines are re-voiced and the cuts re-timed.
