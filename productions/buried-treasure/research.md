# Buried pirate treasure: research

## Summary
There are very few documented cases of pirates burying treasure and no documented historical pirate treasure map. Pirates usually divided loot quickly and spent it. The best-documented case is Captain William Kidd: in 1699, while being pursued as a pirate, he left treasure (gold and other valuables) on Gardiners Island near Long Island, New York. After his arrest in Boston that year, the authorities (under Lord Bellomont) recovered it, and it was sent to England as evidence. Kidd was convicted and hanged at Execution Dock, London, on 23 May 1701. The popular image of buried treasure and "X marks the spot" maps was spread above all by Robert Louis Stevenson's novel Treasure Island (1883).
