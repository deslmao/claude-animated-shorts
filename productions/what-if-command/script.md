# What If… you could command anyone? (draft script for the owner's approval)

**Series:** "What If…" (Thinkora). Supernatural comedy with a dark loop.

**Premise (one sentence):** you discover that anyone who hears you say "Max commands you to…" has to obey, word for
word, until you command the wrong person.

**The rule, said in words at once:** say "Max commands you to…", and anyone who hears it has to do it exactly as
said. Anyone.

**Length:** about 60 s. **Narrator:** the channel's storyteller, second person; you're Max, 14.
**Time cards:** DAY 1 / DAY 2 / DAY 4 / DAY 6. **Ending:** a loop back to the first frame, so it rewatches seamlessly.

**Originality:** the power is ours, as is the rule (it works on anyone who hears it, exactly as said). It has no
symbols, eyes, names or designs from any existing show.

---

**0:00** *(Breakfast. Your little sister's spoon freezes in mid-air. Her eyes go blank.)*
> **MAX:** Max commands you to give me your pancakes.
> **NARRATOR:** Imagine you can make anyone do anything. You just have to say it.

**DAY 1** *(She slides the plate over, smiling, eyes blank, then snaps back: "…Why are you eating my pancakes?")*
> **NARRATOR:** "Max commands you to." Anyone who hears it has to obey. Word for word. Anyone.

**DAY 2** *(Class. The teacher holds up the test papers.)*
> **MAX:** Max commands you to cancel the test.
> **NARRATOR:** She cancels the test. And the next one. And every test, forever. The school has to hire a new
> teacher.

**DAY 4** *(The canteen. Your best friend Leo, mid-laugh at you.)*
> **MAX:** Max commands you to stop laughing.
> **NARRATOR:** Leo hasn't laughed in three days. He watched a video of a dog falling off a sofa. Nothing.
> **LEO** *(dead serious)*: That was hilarious.
> **NARRATOR:** Honestly? The best part is watching people crack.

**DAY 6** *(Home. Mum, tired, nagging. You snap.)*
> **MAX:** Max commands you to leave me alone!
> **NARRATOR:** Word for word. *(The front door closes. Her car pulls away.)* She doesn't come back that night.

*(The school hall, assembly, everyone staring. You on the stage, panicking, at the microphone.)*
> **NARRATOR:** So you fix it. You get the whole school in one room, and the microphone carries it to every
> speaker in town.
> **MAX:** Max commands you to forget I ever had this power!

*(Silence. Every speaker echoes it back: "…forget I ever had this power…" Your own eyes go blank.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Anyone who hears it. Anyone.

*(A cut back to the first frame: breakfast, the pancakes. You blink, as if for the first time.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Day one. Imagine you can make anyone do anything…

*(Silent end card: WHAT WOULD YOU COMMAND FIRST? 👇)*

## Clarity checklist
| The viewer must understand | Told by |
|---|---|
| what the first frame shows | "Max commands you to give me your pancakes" + "make anyone do anything" |
| the rule, and its catch | "Anyone who hears it has to obey. Word for word. Anyone." |
| why the test gag escalates | "And every test, forever." (word for word) |
| who Leo is | "your best friend Leo" |
| why Mum leaves | "Word for word." |
| why the fix backfires (pays off the plant) | "Anyone who hears it. Anyone." (you heard it) |
| the loop | back on the first frame, the same opening line |

## Production notes
- **Reused:** the home and kitchen, the classroom and the school hall sets; the ElevenLabs sound library.
- **New sounds:** a low "command" tone under each command, then a silence; speaker echo.
- **New effect (an opt-in addition):** blank "obeying" eyes. The engine's blank face may already work.
- **Voices:**
  - the narrator;
  - Max (a teen voice, distinct from the narrator);
  - Leo;
  - Mum;
  - the little sister (one line).
- **Music:** a custom comedy score that turns ominous at Day 6 and drops out completely before the loop.
