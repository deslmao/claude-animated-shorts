# Part 2 · The Water Game: viewer script (draft for the owner's approval)

**What this is:** written through the storytelling gate (`productions/research/storytelling-system.md`), and it
follows the locked outline exactly (`../series-outline.md`, Part 2).
- **Narrator:** George, second person ("you" are Ava, as in Part 1). He explains every strange thing as it happens.
- **Cast:** the Part 1 voices: Ava (Alice), Kate (Kasia), Ollie (Leo), and Kuba's words in the toddler voice (Mini).
- **Polish lines** carry English subtitles.
- **The vote result:** most comments said **1, show Mum the video**. In the locked story both choices happen.
- **Length:** about 1:50.

## What the viewer sees and hears
**COLD OPEN.** *(Night, rain. Kate wading into the black river, her back to us.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Your mum is walking into the river. And she can't hear you.
> **AVA:** MUM!

*(Card: 24 HOURS EARLIER · THURSDAY 6:40 AM)*

**THE RECAP.**
> **NARRATOR:** Last time: every night at three a.m., your mum sleepwalks and relives the night her little boy, Kuba,
> drowned in a flood. And your brother Ollie says Kuba's words, in a voice that isn't his.
> **NARRATOR:** Most of you said: show her the video. So you do.

**DAWN.** *(The garden: wet grass, no water. Ava's phone in the grass.)*
> **NARRATOR:** At dawn, the flood is gone. The garden is just wet grass. But your phone is still lying where you
> dropped it, and it filmed everything.

*(The phone: black water over your lawn, stopping at the fence; next door completely dry.)*

**THE KITCHEN.** *(Kate watches the video. Her face drains.)*
> **NARRATOR:** You show your mum.
> **KATE:** He drowned. I let go of him.
> **NARRATOR:** In the flood, she was holding Kuba's hand. And she let go.
> **KATE** *(a whisper)*: What you let go of over the water, the water takes.
> **NARRATOR:** It's an old saying from her grandmother. She thinks the water wants Ollie now.
> **KATE:** Please. Don't tell your dad.

**THE PLAN.**
> **NARRATOR:** So tonight, you keep them apart. Mum locks herself in her room. Ollie sleeps in yours. But your door
> has no lock.

**3 A.M.** *(Ava's room; the clock turns 3:00. Ollie sits up, eyes shut.)*
> **NARRATOR:** At three, Ollie sits up in his sleep and speaks in Kuba's voice.
> **KUBA'S VOICE** *(Polish, subtitled)*: Mamo, woda jest przy drzwiach. *(Mama, the water's at the door.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Then you hear Mum's lock turn. She's unlocking it in her sleep.
> **KATE** *(outside your door, a whisper)*: Kubuś? *(Kuba?)*
> **NARRATOR:** She's looking for him. You hold the handle shut. She doesn't force it. Her footsteps go downstairs.

**THE RIVER.** *(The window: Kate walking into the river alone.)*
> **NARRATOR:** From your window, you see her walk into the river. Alone.
> **AVA:** MUM!
> **NARRATOR:** She can't hear you. So you tell Ollie to stay put, and you run.
> **AVA:** Stay here!
> **NARRATOR:** You grab her arm. But she keeps walking, deeper. She's stronger than you. If you hold on, you'll both
> go under.

**OLLIE AT THE EDGE.**
> **NARRATOR:** Then you see Ollie at the edge. He followed you. And he says Kuba's words.
> **KUBA'S VOICE:** Mamo, woda jest przy drzwiach.
> **NARRATOR:** Mum stops. She turns round, walks back to him, and takes his hand.
> **KATE:** Kubuś, trzymaj mnie za rękę. *(Kuba, hold my hand.)*
> **NARRATOR:** And the flood plays out again, right there at the water's edge.
> **KATE** *(a shout)*: Weź go! *(Take him!)*
> **KUBA'S VOICE:** Obiecałaś, że nie puścisz. *(You promised you wouldn't let go.)*
> **KATE:** Przepraszam. *(I'm sorry.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Her hand opens, and the water pulls him in. But this time, you're already in the river. You catch him.

**THE ENDING.** *(On the bank, soaked. Ollie, awake, in his own voice.)*
> **OLLIE:** If I don't say my words, Mama goes in the water by herself.
> **NARRATOR:** Keeping them apart didn't stop it. It sent her into the river alone. Ollie isn't the one in danger.
> He's the one keeping her alive.

*(Silent end card: WHAT HAPPENED TO KUBA? · comment below · PART 3: WIERZBNO)*
