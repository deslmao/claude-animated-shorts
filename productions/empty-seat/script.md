# The Empty Seat: script (owner-approved, 17 Nov 2026)

**Approved with the clarity fix:** Mr Hale is plainly ill (in hospital) and dies of it, nobody kills him, and line 21 says what seat 14 means. Line 7 was cut to keep the length.

**Series:** Thinkora Ghost Stories, episode 1. The format is the one that worked in *One Too Many*: an animated ghost
story in a school-life setting, one impossible detail, a solved mystery, a twist, and a loop ending.
- **Person:** second person ("you": a regular rider on the bus, never named).
- **Narrator:** `georgeHushed`.
- **One line of dialogue:** Mr Hale, the driver, voiced by `brian`.
- **Length:** about 304 words, about 2:00 (*One Too Many*: 322 words, 2:08).

## Narration
| # | Voice | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | N | Every morning, your school bus has one seat nobody is allowed to sit in. Today, someone is sitting in it. |
| 2 | N | Seat thirteen. By the window. Even when the bus is packed, kids would rather stand. |
| 3 | N | And its window is always fogged up. From the inside. Even in June. |

| 19 | N | And the window by seat thirteen fogs up again. |

**The loop:** the last line echoes the first ("…one seat nobody is allowed to sit in" becomes "…two seats"), the way
*One Too Many* ends on "thirty-two". It ends on the line itself, with no call to action.

## Story check (one pass)
- **The one question** ("why can't anyone sit there?") is answered at line 13, about 60% through: it's Emily's seat,
  kept by the driver who left her behind. Everything after that is consequence, not a new mystery.
- **The fogged window is her breath:** she rides in seat 13 every morning because Hale stops for her at Elm Road.
  - When the new driver skips the stop, she's left behind again. The handprint on the back window is her, chasing the
    bus, with the snow of that morning in 1996 on the road behind it.
  - When the bus stops and waits three seconds, she gets on, and her window fogs up again.
- **The twist is explained by what we already know.** Hale spent thirty years making sure she never missed the bus
  again. The one morning he couldn't come, she nearly did. So after he dies he rides beside her, in seat 14, and keeps
  her seat for good.
- **The dates add up:** 1996 + 30 = 2026. Hale was a young driver then, so he's in his fifties now.
- **The death is non-graphic,** as in *One Too Many*: "She never got there."
- **It's original:** no recreated characters or story; it's built on the same technique as our own *One Too Many*.

## Visual plan, by line
1. **Frame 1:** inside the bus, kids filing in with backpacks, the camera pushing down the aisle to the one empty seat
   with a fogged window. Then Noah's backpack lands on it.
2. **The packed bus:** kids standing in the aisle beside the empty seat.
3. **Close on the window:** fog on the inside of the glass; outside, bright June sun.
4. **Noah,** new and cheerful, flops into the seat.
5. **Every head turns.** The bus slows, its hazard lights clicking.
6. **Mr Hale** in the driver's mirror, then turning round: grey, tired, kind.
7. **The kids look at each other** and shrug.
8. **Outside, Elm Road:** an empty stop with an old sign. The doors fold open, three seconds, and close.
9. **Your phone:** an old route map, ELM ROAD (CLOSED 1996).
10. **The library:** the 1996 yearbook page with a black border, a girl's school photo, EMILY SHAW, 11.
11. **1996, a snowstorm:** the bus pushing past the stop, and a small figure behind the snowbank, waving.
12. **Her small footprints** in the snow along the road, fading.
13. **Mr Hale today,** stopping at Elm Road, then glancing at seat 13 in his mirror.
14. **A different driver:** a young woman, chewing gum.
15. **The Elm Road stop** flashing past the window.
16. **The back window:** a small handprint slapping onto the glass. Behind the bus, snow on a summer road.
17. **You** running down the aisle.
18. **The doors folding open** on the empty stop: one, two, three.
19. **Seat 13's window** fogging up again.
20. **Night:** a dark house, one window.
21. **The bus, morning:** the windows by seats 13 and 14 both fogged.
22. **Two empty seats,** side by side, and kids standing beside them. A slow push-in, ending on the shot the film opened
    with.
