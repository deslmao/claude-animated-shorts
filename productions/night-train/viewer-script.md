# The Man on the Last Train Has No Reflection.: viewer script

What the viewer sees and hears, in order. Nothing else reaches them.

**1.** _frame 0: the banner; a man in a soaked torn coat lunging at you, screaming; the camera shakes; the lights stutter_
> **NTMAN:** Why did this happen to me? WHY ME?!

**2.** _you flinch back in your seat; the two of you across the bay; over your shoulder at him_
> **NARRATOR:** Imagine you're on the last train home, and the man in front of you won't stop screaming.

**3.** _the LED display over the end door: 21:47; over your shoulder: the man on the seats facing yours; his face: staring, unblinking_
> **NARRATOR:** It's 9:47. He's on the three seats in front of you. Facing you. Staring right at you.

**4.** _his face, shouting; his fists clenched on his knees_
> **NTMAN:** I was just going home! Just like you!

**5.** _water dripping off his coat's torn hem; the puddle spreads_
> **NARRATOR:** His coat is soaking wet. It hasn't rained all week.

**6.** _you turn your head to the dark window_
> **NARRATOR:** You look at the window.

**7.** _the dark window: your reflection, the lit carriage, the seats opposite: empty_
> **NARRATOR:** You can see yourself. You can see the empty seats.

**8.** _above: him in his seat; below: the same seat in the glass: empty_
> **NARRATOR:** But where he's sitting… there's nobody there.

**9.** _his face, pushing in: no blink, no expression_
> **NARRATOR:** He has no reflection.

**10.** _the carriage, the lights stuttering; your face, eyes darting_
> **ntThought (voice-over):** Don't look at him. Just get up. Walk.

**11.** _you walk away fast; the end door slides open; you go through; it slides shut_

**12.** _the next carriage: behind you the end door slides open again: he's standing in the gangway_
> **NARRATOR:** Behind you, the door slides open again. He's following you.

**13.** _the empty carriage, swaying; through the end door's glass the next one, empty; over your shoulder, him, at the far end_
> **NARRATOR:** And then you notice: this carriage is empty. So is the next one. It's just you… and him.

**14.** _you run straight at us; side on past the bays; your feet pounding toward the last door; the lights strobing_
> **NARRATOR:** You run. As fast as you can. You don't look back.

**15.** _the last carriage in red emergency light; you stop, gasping; the camera circles you; then nothing but the rails_
> **ntThought (voice-over):** I… I lost him. I must have lost him.

**16.** _the jumpscare: you turn and his face fills the frame; the smile spreads; he whispers_
> **ntManWhisper (voice-over):** This is your stop.

**17.** _the LED display: NEXT STOP ASHFIELD; it glitches; DEATH CITY, in red_
> **ntAnnouncer (voice-over):** The next stop is… Death City.

**18.** _the line map: every stop you know, and DEATH CITY between them, ringed_
> **NARRATOR:** You've ridden this line every day for years. There is no Death City.

**19.** _the brakes; the red exit doors hiss open onto fog and a red DEATH CITY sign_
> **NARRATOR:** The doors open.

**20.** _the last frame: you at the open doors, the fog and the sign; him right behind you, smiling; A · GET OFF / B · STAY ON; cut half a second after the last word_
> **NARRATOR:** Do you get off… or stay on and run for the next stop?

## Clarity checklist

**Premise:** On the last train home, a screaming man with no reflection follows you through an empty train, until the speakers announce a stop that doesn't exist.

| The viewer must understand | Told by the words | Shot |
|---|---|---|
| the situation | "Imagine you're on the last train home, and the man in front of you won't stop screaming." | 2 |
| where he is | "He's on the three seats in front of you." | 3 |
| the first wrong detail | "His coat is soaking wet. It hasn't rained all week." | 5 |
| the impossible thing | "He has no reflection." | 9 |
| he's coming after you | "He's following you." | 12 |
| nobody can help | "It's just you… and him." | 13 |
| the stop | "The next stop is… Death City." | 17 |
| the stop is impossible | "There is no Death City." | 18 |
| the choice | "Do you get off… or stay on and run for the next stop?" | 20 |
