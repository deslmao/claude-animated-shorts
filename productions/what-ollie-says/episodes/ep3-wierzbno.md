## Script
**INT. KATE'S BEDROOM. AFTERNOON.**
Grey light, rain on the glass. AVA's hands push through Kate's coats to the back of the wardrobe: an old biscuit tin with a dinosaur sticker on the lid.
She opens it. On top, a photo: a curly-haired toddler laughing on a swing.

**AVA:** …Ollie?

She turns it over. Faded biro: *Kubuś, 3 latka · 2008* [PL provisional]
*Subtitle:* Kuba, age 3 · 2008

*Title: WHAT OLLIE SAYS AT 3 A.M. · PART 3*

**INT. LANDING. CONTINUOUS.**
Ava holds the photo beside OLLIE's school photo on the wall. The same curls. The same eyes.

*FLASH (2008): a tiled roof in hammering rain. A young woman clutching this toddler.*

**KASIA (2008) [PL provisional L3]:** *Nie puszczę cię. Obiecuję.*
*Subtitle:* I won't let go of you. I promise.

**INT. KATE'S BEDROOM. CONTINUOUS.**
In the tin: an expired Polish passport. A young Kate. KATARZYNA WIŚNIEWSKA · 12.02.1988 · WIERZBNO.

**AVA:** Wiśniewska?

On her phone, she searches the village. A news photo: rooftops in brown water. The translated headline: *Wierzbno: levee breaks shortly before 3 a.m. · 14 July 2008.*

**AVA (V.O.):** Every fourteenth of July, Mum stays in bed.

*Insert: the kitchen calendar, flipped back to July. The 14th, circled in red.*

She searches "Kuba". *Short for Jakub.* Then the memorial page, *Wierzbno — Pamiętamy*: a grey stone. She scrolls down the names.
HELENA WIŚNIEWSKA, 79.
KATARZYNA WIŚNIEWSKA, 20 — *missing.*
TOMASZ KOWAL, 34 — *gave his life saving a child.*
No Jakub.

**AVA** *(whisper)*: Mum's on it.

She types to the page: *Was there a little boy called Jakub? Kuba?* Send.

Deeper in the tin: a hospital wristband, *NN · K · ok. 20 l.*, and a folded note in Polish. Her translator lays English over it:
*"Found at the window at 3 a.m. Did not answer to her name. Woken suddenly: panicked, cut herself on the glass. DO NOT WAKE SUDDENLY."*
At the bottom of the tin, a tiny blue knitted sock with a white stripe. Ava holds it in her palm.

**INT. HALLWAY. EVENING.**
Every phone in the house buzzes at once: FLOOD ALERT. *River levels rising. Properties at risk tonight.*
MARK, in hi-vis, at the front door.

**MARK:** Plant's on alert. Lock up. Top bolt.

**INT. LANDING. NIGHT.**
Ava sits on the floor, back against Ollie's door, hood up, phone in her hand. 2:59. Opposite: her mother's locked door.

3:00. Inside Kate's room, a key turns. The handle moves. KATE comes out: nightdress, cardigan, eyes open. She doesn't blink.

**AVA:** Mum?

Kate walks toward her and past her. Their shoulders brush. Kate doesn't see her. She goes into Ollie's room and kneels by the bed.

**OLLIE [PL provisional L1]:** *Mamo, woda jest przy drzwiach.*

**KATE [PL provisional L2]:** *Kubuś, trzymaj mnie za rękę.*

Ollie gets up and takes her hand. As they pass Ava in the doorway, he looks at his sister: small, sorry. And keeps walking.

**INT. KITCHEN / EXT. GARDEN. NIGHT.**
Without looking, Kate slides back the top bolt. Out into the rain. Down the lawn.

**KATE** *(whisper) [PL provisional L3]:* *Nie puszczę cię. Obiecuję.*

The river has come over the bottom of the garden. Black water across the grass. The gate half under.
At the edge, Ollie stands ankle-deep. Kate lifts his hand high, toward the dark.

**KATE [PL provisional L4]:** *Weź go!*
*Subtitle:* Take him!

Five metres behind, Ava starts forward and stops. *DO NOT WAKE SUDDENLY.*

**OLLIE [PL provisional L5]:** *Obiecałaś, że nie puścisz.*
*Subtitle:* You promised you wouldn't let go.

**KATE [PL provisional L6]:** *Przepraszam.*
*Subtitle:* I'm sorry.

Her hand opens. She turns and walks back up the garden, past Ava, calm, eyes open.
Ollie stands alone in the black water.
Ava runs and scoops him up.

**AVA:** I've got you. I've got you.

Up at the house, the kitchen light. Kate's silhouette climbing the stairs.

**INT. KITCHEN. NIGHT.**
Towels. Both of them shaking. Ava's phone in her hand, ringing DAD. She looks at her brother.

**AVA:** Why do you go with her?

**OLLIE** *(teeth chattering)*: If I don't say my words, Mama goes in the water by herself.

Ava lowers the phone.

CUT TO BLACK. *End card: PART 4: 1,214 NIGHTS*
