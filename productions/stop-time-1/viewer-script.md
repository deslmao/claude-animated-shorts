# What If… You Could Stop Time? · Part 1: Sixty Years: viewer script

What the viewer sees and hears, in order. Nothing else reaches them.

**1.** _frozen: a bullet an inch from Sol's face; the rain hangs in the air; blue_
> **NARRATOR:** Imagine you could stop time.
> **NARRATOR:** Every second costs you a day of your life.

**2.** _Theo walks through the frozen rain toward her_
> **NARRATOR:** This is the girl I spent them on.

**3.** _the Lowline's neon canyon; the gold Towers above the smog_
> **NARRATOR:** In Vantage City, the rich live in the Towers, above the smog.

**4.** _street level: crowds in the rain under the neon_
> **NARRATOR:** The rest of us live under it, in the Lowline. Nobody down here has ever seen the sky.

**5.** _Mum in her medic coat at the door; Theo on the bed_
> **MUM:** Eat something. I'll be back by sunrise.
> **THEO:** You always say that.
> **MUM:** And I always am.

**6.** _the hospital: COVERAGE REQUIRED_
> **NARRATOR:** She wasn't. She got caught in a street fight.
> **NARRATOR:** The hospital wanted a coverage card before they'd touch her. She didn't have one.

**7.** _her locker: the military chip_
> **NARRATOR:** In her locker, I found something she was hiding. A stolen military implant.

**8.** _the implant goes in, sparks_
> **NARRATOR:** I put it in.

**9.** _the counter on his wrist: 21,915_
> **NARRATOR:** A number lit up on my wrist. Twenty-one thousand days. Sixty years.

**10.** _Theo in the rain, his fist closing_
> **NARRATOR:** Squeeze my fist, and time stops.

**11.** _the street frozen blue round him, the rain hanging still_
> **NARRATOR:** Every second I hold it, the number drops by one day.

**12.** _the cold open again: Theo takes her arm_
> **NARRATOR:** The first time I used it, I saved a stranger.

**13.** _time crashes back; the bullet hits the wall; Sol stares at him_
> **SOL:** …How did you do that? You were over there. Then you weren't.
> **THEO:** Lucky, I guess.

**14.** _Sol, her visor glowing, grinning at him_
> **NARRATOR:** Her name was Sol. She could hack anything with a screen. She ran with a crew that stole from the Towers.
> **SOL:** We could use someone who's that lucky.

**15.** _Sol's cracked holo-photo of the sea_
> **tsSol (voice-over):** That's the sea. A real one. Past the wall, past the smog.

**16.** _the rooftop: the two of them on the ledge, the city and the Towers behind_
> **THEO:** Have you ever seen it?
> **SOL:** Not yet. One day I'm going to stand in it. And take this visor off, so I see it with my own eyes.

**17.** _closer: the promise_
> **THEO:** Then I'm coming with you.
> **SOL:** Is that a promise?
> **THEO:** It's a promise.

**18.** _the crew by the van after a job: Dex roaring with laughter_
> **DEX:** Kid, whatever you're doing, keep doing it!

**19.** _the counter falling: 18,240, 9,600, 2,100_
> **NARRATOR:** Every job, something went wrong. Every time, I stopped it. And every time, the number got smaller.

**20.** _under the neon: Sol holding his wrist_
> **SOL:** Theo… what is this number?
> **THEO:** It's how many days I have left.

**21.** _Sol, close, crying_
> **SOL:** Then stop. Promise me you'll stop. I don't want the sea without you.
> **THEO:** I promise.

**22.** _Theo alone on the rooftop, looking at the Towers_
> **NARRATOR:** I meant it. I really did.

**23.** _by the van: the last job_
> **DEX:** One last job. The Tower vault. Two tickets out of this city.
> **SOL:** To the sea.

**24.** _the vault: the alarm, the guards storm in, a gun at Sol's head_
> **GUARD:** Drop it! Now!

**25.** _Sol, a gun at her head, looking at Theo: she knows_
> **SOL:** Theo… don't.
> **NARRATOR:** There were too many of them. To save her, I'd need to stop time for a whole minute.

**26.** _the counter: 41, in red_
> **NARRATOR:** I had forty-one days left.

**27.** _end card: Part 2, the vote_
