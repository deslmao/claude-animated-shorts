# Horror mini-series, round 2: four concepts that survived (for the owner's choice)

2026-11-15. This is a fresh start. Nothing from round 1 is reused. No scripts, assets or production.

**How I tested every idea.** Each one had to pass four tests, and any idea that failed one was discarded rather than fixed:
1. **The opening question:** can a viewer say what's wrong in one sentence from the first shot?
2. **The likely theory:** what will most viewers guess after episode 1? If that guess is the real answer, the idea is out.
3. **Fair clues:** does each twist recolour clues we have already shown?
4. **No filler:** would cutting any one episode lose a major revelation?

## Discarded (and why)
- **Two women claim to be my mother, and the DNA says both are:** a thriller, not horror.
- **A girl in the window of my house, always 15, on every Street View capture since 2008:** a great image, but no clean underlying truth.
- **The neighbour waved at me every morning, and he'd been dead three weeks:** the truth needed too many coincidences.
- **Every night the dads of our street drive to a field in their pyjamas:** a childhood-guilt secret, and *Stand by Me* is too easy to guess.
- **The whole town is grieving me:** viewers would guess "she's a ghost" at once.
- **My family wakes at the kitchen table at 2 a.m. every night:** every answer I found came down to a ghost.
- **Letters from the town's dead confessing their secrets:** good, but not horror.

Four concepts survived. I'm not padding to five.

## 1. WHAT OLLIE SAYS AT 3 A.M. (5 episodes): the strongest
**The mystery:** every night at exactly 3:00, six-year-old Ollie talks in his sleep in fluent Polish, with the words of a small child in a flood. Nobody in the family speaks Polish.

**Protagonist and stakes:** Ava, 15, shares a wall with Ollie and adores him.
- At first she films him for a laugh.
- Then her brother starts changing in front of her.
- Then her own mother turns out to be a stranger.

**The underlying truth (hidden from viewers until episode 4):**
- Mum ("Kate") is really Katarzyna, from a river village in Poland.
- At 17, during a flood, she was clinging to a roof with her two-year-old son Kuba when the rope tore through her hand and the water took him.
- She was rescued, he was never found, and she fled abroad, changed her name and told no one, not even Dad.
- **Kuba lived.** He was found in a tree two days later and grew up in an orphanage. He's 30 now, a baker who gets up for his 4 a.m. shift.
- Since Ollie was born, the two brothers have shared the same dream in the hour before Kuba's alarm. That's 3:00 in England.
- Ollie speaks Kuba's dream. Mum has heard it every night for six years, and she has had the same dream every night for 28 years.

**Clues to plant:**
- It's always exactly 3:00, and Mum is always "up for a glass of water".
- Mum won't swim and goes still when heavy rain hits the windows.
- She has a pale rope-burn scar across her palm, and hums a lullaby with no words.
- Ollie also mumbles about "flour" and "the oven". It sounds like a fire; it's really Kuba's bakery.

| Ep | Revelation | Cliffhanger | Viewers know | Viewers don't know (and will theorise) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · 3:00 | Ava's video, run through a translator: Ollie is saying "The water's at the door. Mama, you said you'd hold on." It's fluent Polish describing a real flood. | In the clip, after Ollie goes quiet, a woman's voice in the dark room whispers in Polish: **"I'm here. I'm so sorry."** | Ollie speaks a language he's never heard, about a drowning, and someone else was in the room. | Whose words? Who answered? Possession, a past life, a ghost? |
| 2 · The Second Voice | A hidden camera shows the voice is **Mum**, kneeling by Ollie's bed and answering him in Polish, in tears. Mum has always said she doesn't speak a word. | Ollie, asleep: "You let go of my hand." Mum whispers **"Kuba"**, then looks straight into the hidden camera. She knows. | Mum understands it, recognises it and has hidden it. | Who is Kuba? Why lie? (Flour and oven: was there a fire?) |
| 3 · Katarzyna | Mum deletes the footage and denies everything. Ava finds a hidden tin: a Polish ID card in another name, and a clipping: **"Flood: two-year-old swept from his mother's arms; search called off."** Mum had a son before Ava. Even Dad doesn't know. Ollie starts calling Mum "Mama" in Polish while awake, and won't answer to "Ollie". | 3:00 a.m., running water. Ava finds **Ollie lying under the water in a full bath, eyes open, calm.** She pulls him out, and he gasps in Polish: **"This time, don't let go."** | Ollie is reliving Kuba's last moments, and it's getting dangerous. | The obvious theory is the drowned child possessing her brother, and we deliberately feed it. |
| 4 · Jakub | Ava posts the clip on the village's flood-memorial page. A man replies: **"My name is Jakub. I was found in a tree two days later. I've had the same dream every night of my life. For six years, there's been a little English boy in it."** Kuba is **alive**. There's no ghost: the brothers share a dream, and "flour" and "oven" are Jakub's bakery. | Jakub: "Does she know I'm alive? I won't come unless she asks." **VOTE.** | The whole truth, except what Mum knows. | Will the truth heal Mum or break her? Why is the link getting stronger? |
| 5 · Finale | One of two endings, by the vote (below). | | | |

**The A/B vote:**
- **A: Tell Mum.** Her 20-year lie falls apart in front of Dad, and Jakub may never forgive her.
- **B: Keep the secret.** The family stays whole, and Kuba never learns his mother survived. Ollie keeps reliving the flood every night, and the bath showed where that leads.

**The endings:**
- **A:**
  - Ava tells Mum, who doesn't break down. She says quietly: **"I know he's alive. I hear him every night."**
  - She has had the dream every night for 28 years and never went to him, out of shame, because she let go.
  - At a riverbank, Jakub asks the question from the dream: "Why didn't you hold on?" She opens her hand and shows him the rope scar: she held on until it tore through her palm. He takes her hand.
  - That night at 3:00, no one wakes in either house. **Final image:** Ollie asleep and silent, Ava lying awake, listening to the quiet.
- **B:**
  - Ava stays silent. Weeks pass, and the 3 a.m. voice grows fainter.
  - One night Ollie says, in Polish: "It's okay, Mama. I'll stop waiting now." After that, there's silence.
  - Ava finds Mum kneeling by Ollie's bed at 3:00, night after night, waiting for a voice that never comes. Ava understands: Mum was never haunted. Those six years were the only time she heard her son, and Ava took them away.
  - **Final image:** Mum at the window at 3 a.m., listening.

## 2. THE ECHO AT HOLLOW GILL (4 episodes)
**The mystery:** ten years ago, Ruby's sister Bea, then 7, vanished on a family walk in the valley of Hollow Gill. On the anniversary, Ruby shouts "BEA!" across the valley, and the echo answers in a seven-year-old's voice: **"Here!"**

**Protagonist and stakes:** Ruby, 15, was five when it happened and remembers nothing of that day.
- Dad was suspected by the police for years and never recovered.
- Mum "drowned herself" a year later. Her body was never found.

**The underlying truth:**
- The valley replays that day in order, a little more on each visit.
- **Mum took Bea.** That morning, in a sun hat and scarf, she waited near the walk.
- **Five-year-old Ruby told "the lady" where Bea was hiding,** because the lady called her "Bean", the family's pet name.
- Mum took Bea to Spain, faked her own death a year later to join her, and told Bea her family hadn't wanted her.
- Dad's shouting in the echo was panic, not anger.

**Clues to plant:**
- The voices in the echo are ten years old.
- "Bean" is the family's pet name for the girls.
- Mum's cards came typed, never handwritten.
- No body was ever found.
- Ruby has a blank in her memory of that day.

| Ep | Revelation | Cliffhanger | Viewers know | Viewers don't know |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Here | The valley answers in Bea's seven-year-old voice. | Ruby shouts "Where are you?" The echo answers in **Ruby's own five-year-old voice**: "She's in the den, behind the big rock." She doesn't remember ever saying it. | Something in the valley holds that day. | Is Bea alive? A ghost? A trick? |
| 2 · The Day | On each visit the echo plays the next part of that day: counting for hide-and-seek, laughter, birdsong. Then **Dad roars: "Get in the car. NOW."** A car door slams. (Viewers: Dad did it, as the police thought.) | A soft woman's voice near the den: **"Hello, Bean. Want to come and see the ponies?"** "Bean" was Mum's name for them, and Mum wasn't on the walk. | The echo is replaying the day she vanished. | Who is the lady? Was Dad covering something up? |
| 3 · The Lady | Dad confirms Mum was at work that day. Among Mum's things Ruby finds a ferry ticket dated the next morning, typed birthday cards, and a "suicide" with no body. The echo plays the worst part: **Ruby, aged five, leading the lady to the den.** | Ruby searches Mum's maiden name: a 17-year-old girl in a Spanish coastal town, Bea's face, **and behind her in the photo, a woman in a sun hat. Mum.** **VOTE.** | Mum took Bea, and Ruby unknowingly helped. | Do they go? What does Bea believe? |
| 4 · Finale | One of two endings, by the vote (below). | | | |

**The A/B vote:**
- **A: Go to Spain.** Bea learns her whole life is a lie, Mum goes to prison, and Bea may hate them for it.
- **B: Leave them be.** Bea is alive and happy, Dad keeps searching the valley, and Ruby carries it alone.

**The endings:**
- **A:**
  - Ruby and Dad stand on a Spanish doorstep. Bea opens the door and doesn't recognise Dad, but she recognises Ruby: "You're dead. She said you…"
  - Mum appears behind her, and says to Ruby: "Hello, Bean."
  - **Final image:** months later in Hollow Gill, Dad, Ruby and Bea shout across the valley, and for the first time the echo answers in their own voices from today. The replay is over.
- **B:**
  - Ruby says nothing.
  - On a grey Sunday she goes to the valley alone and shouts "I'm sorry."
  - The seven-year-old voice answers: **"Ready or not, here I come…"** and the day starts again from the beginning, forever.

## 3. THE REHEARSAL (5 episodes)
**The mystery:** strangers are rehearsing Maya's family life word for word, the night before it happens.

**Protagonist and stakes:** Maya, 14, who lives with Mum, Dad and her brother Sol, 8.
- She spent last spring in hospital after a car crash, and her memory of it is patchy. This is mentioned in passing.
- What's at stake is her family, and then whether anything in her life is real.

**The underlying truth:**
- Her parents and Sol died in the crash. The doctors warned against telling her all at once.
- Her grandmother couldn't bear to tell her, so she hired her old amateur theatre company to play the family until Maya was strong enough.
- They rehearse each night in the school hall, from scripts Gran wrote.
- Gran died last month. "Mum" (Ruth, an actress who lost her own daughter years ago) won't let the show end. She is writing the scripts now, writing the other actors out one by one.

**Clues to plant:**
- Dad laughs at his own joke slightly wrong.
- Every family photo predates the crash.
- "Sol, you've grown." He's a different child actor.
- Mum's handwriting "changed after the hospital".
- Gran cries on the doorstep.

| Ep | Revelation | Cliffhanger | Viewers know | Viewers don't know |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Tomorrow's Breakfast | In the school hall: a perfect copy of her kitchen, actors playing her family, and a girl in Maya's cardigan reading Maya's lines. The director says: "Tomorrow, Dad drops the mug." | Breakfast happens exactly as rehearsed. Dad drops the mug, and **Maya hears herself say the stand-in's line.** | Someone is scripting her life. | Who, and why? (A Truman Show? A cult? Precognition?) |
| 2 · Line? | A script in Dad's coat: "Hart Family, Week 74", tomorrow's scenes, her lines predicted, and stage directions about her: *"If Maya mentions the hall, change the subject. Smile."* Her family is in on it. | She goes off-script at dinner: "Who are you?" Everyone freezes, and Dad turns to the dark garden window: **"…Line?"** A woman with a torch and a script whispers it back from the garden. | Her family is performing. | Why would her own family do this? (Viewers: an experiment, a cult.) |
| 3 · Front of House | Maya follows the prompter to a van: rails of her family's clothes, a cast list (MUM: Ruth Bell, DAD: Martin Hale, SOL: Toby Price), and a programme, "Opening night: the day she came home from hospital". Then a clipping: **"Three killed in crash; girl, 13, critical."** | The director, at the van door: **"Tonight's the last show. Ruth doesn't know yet. When she finds out, don't be alone with her."** | Her family is dead, and actors have played them for 18 months. | Who set this up? Why is Ruth dangerous? |
| 4 · The Writer | Gran set it up out of love, and died last month. Since then Ruth has written the scripts. In her pages, "Dad" goes on a work trip (the actor hasn't been seen for three days), Sol "stays with a friend", and the last page reads: *"Final scene. Just the two of us. Maya forgets the hall. No more curtain."* | Ruth sets down two mugs of cocoa and goes off-script for the first time: **"I know you know. Stay, and I'll be your mum forever. Go, and you'll have no one."** **VOTE.** | Everything. | |
| 5 · Finale | One of two endings, by the vote (below). | | | |

**The A/B vote:**
- **A: Stay.** She's loved and safe, but lives inside a script forever, with Ruth in control.
- **B: Go.** She faces grief, care, and a woman who won't let go.

**The endings:**
- **A:**
  - Months later, at a perfect breakfast, Maya delivers every line flawlessly.
  - The camera pulls back: she is holding tomorrow's script, and writing it.
  - **Final image:** the school hall at night, a new girl rehearsing Maya's part, and Maya in the dark seats saying, "Again. And this time I'm happy."
- **B:**
  - Maya pours the cocoa away and walks out mid-scene.
  - The cast keeps performing to her empty chair, and Ruth whispers "Line?" to no one.
  - Maya kneels at three real graves and finally cries.
  - **Final image:** through the lit window at night, Ruth is still serving breakfast to four empty chairs.

## 4. CHECK-OUT (4 episodes): the weakest of the four, kept for its opening
**The mystery:** Mia wakes in a seaside hotel to find Dad gone. Reception says she checked in alone, and the CCTV shows her chatting and laughing with nobody.

**Protagonist and stakes:** Mia, 14, raised by Dad alone. He insisted on this out-of-season trip to a town he said he'd never been to.

**The underlying truth:**
- Dad grew up in this town, which has an old pact: every November, one person "belonging to the town" is given to the sea in the cave under the cliff.
- He was chosen in 1998 and ran, and the town gave his little sister instead.
- The debt follows the family, and this year it fell on Mia.
- Dad came back to take her place, on one condition: the town erases him, so she never comes looking.
- The CCTV was edited by Tom, the night porter, who was Dad's childhood friend. Dad's "remember your tablets" meant sleeping pills.

**Clues to plant:**
- Dad greeted the porter by name.
- He cried at breakfast.
- Mia slept unusually deeply.
- The café receipt shows two breakfasts.

| Ep | Revelation | Cliffhanger | Viewers know | Viewers don't know |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Room 7 | Dad's things are gone, and the CCTV shows Mia alone, talking to the air. | She freezes a frame: alone in the lobby, but **in the reflection on the glass door, a man walks beside her carrying her bag.** | The footage is faked. | Is she ill? Is Dad alive? |
| 2 · The Guest Books | The café receipt shows two breakfasts. The chip-shop woman blurts out "He's a brave man." In the hotel's guest books, one name is torn out every November back to the 1950s, and the harbour memorial lists one "lost at sea" each year. | The 1998 book, untorn: **Dad's signature at 16. "Room 7. Chosen."** | The town hides a disappearance every year, and Dad has been here before. | Why Dad? Why now? |
| 3 · The Low Tide | Tom tells her everything: the pact, 1998, the sister, and that this year it fell on Mia. Dad asked to be erased so she'd never come looking. The tablets and the tears suddenly make sense. | "If he isn't taken at dawn, they'll come for you." **VOTE.** | Dad is alive in the sea cave. | Is anything really in the sea? |
| 4 · Finale | One of two endings, by the vote (below). | | | |

**The A/B vote:**
- **A: Save Dad.** The town, or the sea, comes for Mia.
- **B: Honour his choice.** She takes the 6 a.m. train with his letter.

**The endings:**
- **A:**
  - Mia cuts Dad free as the tide rises, and they climb up to find the whole town waiting at the top, silent.
  - The sea goes flat calm, and nothing comes. Tom: **"Sixty years. It never came for anyone. It was always us."**
  - **Final image:** in the rear-view mirror, the town still on the cliff, facing the sea.
- **B:**
  - On the train she reads his letter: "You'll hate me for leaving. That's the point. Hate is easier to live with."
  - Months later, the harbour memorial has a new name, Dad's, and beneath it, newly carved: **"Paid in full."**

**Why it's weakest:** the town-sacrifice core and "it was always us" are *The Wicker Man* and *The Lottery*. The fresh parts are the CCTV reflection and Dad asking to be erased.

## The strongest: WHAT OLLIE SAYS AT 3 A.M.
- **The opening is impossible yet simple:** a six-year-old speaking a language he's never heard.
- **The theories evolve:**
  - after episode 1: possession or a past life;
  - after episode 2: Mum knows;
  - after episode 3: the dead child is taking over Ollie, which is the obvious theory, deliberately fed;
  - episode 4 overturns it: Kuba is alive, so there was never a ghost.
- **Every clue pays off in the truth:**
  - Mum at 3:00 for water: she wakes to the dream herself.
  - The rope scar: she held on until the rope tore.
  - Flour and the oven: Jakub's bakery.
  - 3:00 exactly: 4:00 in Poland, his alarm.
- **It gets worse every episode:** a strange video; then Mum's secret; then Ollie losing himself; then the bath, where the danger is physical.
- **The vote is the theme:** truth or peace, and both endings land without needing another series.
- **Visuals:** night-vision bedroom footage, the translated subtitles turning into on-screen text, rain on the windows, the bath, a flood flashback in grey water, a riverbank meeting, two houses in two countries asleep at 3:00.
- **Production note:** most of it happens in one house (bedrooms, bathroom, kitchen). The Polish lines can be voiced and translated in the captions.
