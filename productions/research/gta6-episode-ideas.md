# GTA 6, Explained to Your Girlfriend: episode ideas

These came from a GTA 6 tips video the owner shared on 23 Nov 2026. Only the game facts are kept, in our own words.
None of that video's wording or jokes are reused. Check each fact against a second source before using it.

**They're hidden features, not bugs:** things the game can do but doesn't explain.

Each idea has a "girlfriend twist" in brackets.

| # | Game fact | Girlfriend twist (idea) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Done, Ep 1:** a spare outfit in your car helps lose the cops | "You've worn that shirt for three days" |
| 2 | The game has turn signals; tap left or right on the D-pad. Other drivers ignore them | He signals politely… during a getaway |
| 3 | You can push cars and boats by hand (a boat back into the water) | "You can't open a jar, but you push boats?" |
| 4 | Shooting ranges, fishing and basketball fill your "focus" meter (slow motion) | "So fishing makes you better at… that?" |
| 5 | You can send your partner character off to sell stolen goods for you | Lucy: "So she does the errands. Sounds familiar." |
| 6 | Garages are the only property you can buy, and parking spaces are limited | Gordon saves for a garage, not a flat |
| 7 | It can take 10+ hours of story before you reach the big city | "Six hours and you haven't even got to the city?" |
| 8 | Cheats only unlock after you finish the story | "So you have to finish it to cheat?" |
| 9 | The classic control scheme puts sprint back on X | He tapped X for six hours on the wrong setting |
| 10 | Lost far out at sea, someone comes to rescue you | Even the game won't let him be lost alone |
| 11 | Focus outside combat highlights things you can interact with | Lucy wants the same thing for his room |
| 12 | Binoculars let you tag enemies before sneaking in | He tags the snacks in the kitchen |
