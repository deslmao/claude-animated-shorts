# Critic report, round 2: "GTA 6, Explained to Your Girlfriend · Ep 1"

I judged all 20 full-resolution frames, the motion strip and both reference sheets (35 REF frames). I did not look at any other round.

## Scores table

| Category | Score | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| Characters | 5 | The designs are clean, consistent and readable, but posing and acting are stiff. Gordon's sofa pose repeats (f05, f12, f18), props are rarely really held, and the characters never take on the light of the scene they stand in. |
| Motion | 4 | Frames 0-15 are nearly a still (a slight push-in only). Frames 1395-1410 are a small head bob. The walk at 1140-1155 is the only real body motion. Between the cuts the picture mostly holds. |

## Top fixes (weakest category first)

1. **Light the real-life sets (f05, f06, f07, f09, f12, f18, f20, and f01 by day).**
   - In the night flat, add the TV as an actual light source: a cool blue-white key from camera-left or front onto both characters.
   - Add a warm practical from the teal pendant lamp, and a soft cast shadow under the sofa and the figures.
   - Replace the flat navy window with a lit night skyline or at least a gradient and stars.
   - Give the cartoon figures a per-scene tint and rim:
     - blue rim at night;
     - warm orange key in f17 at sunset;
     - a real ground shadow in f14, f15 and f16.

   This is what separates REF 2_3, 2_34 and 3_10 from our frames.
2. **Put motion and acting into the holds and props (f01, f05-f07, f12, f18, motion 0-15 and 1395-1410).**
   - f01: Lucy visibly holds her phone and types (a thumb tap and a head tilt), instead of standing still behind the bed block.
   - Gordon on the sofa holds the controller in both hands: thumbs moving, a body lean, a small bounce. Do the same in f06, f07 and f18.
   - Use a different angle for each of the three Gordon sofa close-ups (one low over the controller, one over Lucy's shoulder), and add a slow camera drift on every hold longer than 1 s.
   - Fix the arm-overshoot bug on held props in f15 and f16 (the arm must end at the hand that grips).
3. **Clear the collisions and the cut face (f18, f03, f10, motion 1470, f02 and 1461-1470, f13, f15).**
   - f18: reframe so Lucy is fully in frame or fully out.
   - On the phone, counter and receipt inserts, move the caption to the empty lower area (y≈1550-1650), or move the insert content, so it never sits on a bubble or label.
   - Shrink or lower the phone in f02 and 1461-1470 so the banner clears the status bar.
   - f13: fit the receipt total inside the paper.
   - f15: pull the camera back so the rod and feet stay in frame and the HUD doesn't sit on Gordon.
   - Where practical, make the inserts physical objects in the set, as REF 3_18 and 3_42 do: the calendar on the bedroom wall, the receipt on the coffee table.
