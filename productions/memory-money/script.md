# I've Sold 200 Memories of the Girl I Love. This Is the Last One. (viewer script for the owner's approval)

**Format:** a standalone Short, about 2:11 (v2 adds three scenes of the two of them together). An original cyberpunk love story: a power with a price, a cruel city, and a
twist that pays off everything.

**The hook:** the first image and the first two lines carry the whole story.
- **The image:** a glowing vial with a girl's face fading inside it.
- **The lines:** "In my city, you pay with your memories. I've sold two hundred memories of the girl I love. This is
  the last one."

**Narrator:** Kai, 17, telling his own story in the first person.

**Characters:**
| Character | Who they are | Voice and caption |
|---|---|---|
| Kai | the narrator | a young male voice, his own caption colour |
| Lumi, 17 | the girl he loves: silver hair, paper stars, always humming | a soft, bright voice |
| the Broker | runs the memory market: old, smooth, cold | one or two lines |

**Premise (one sentence):** in a city where memories are money, a boy sells every memory of the girl he loves to pay
for her medicine, and finds out she has been selling her own memories to buy his back.

**The rule (said at 0:12):**
- A machine draws a memory out of your head into a glowing vial, and the rich pay to feel it.
- Once it's sold, it's gone from you forever.

---

## COLD OPEN · 0:00
*(A neon memory market. Close on Kai's hand on a steel counter, holding a glowing vial. Inside it, a girl's smiling
face flickers and starts to fade.)*
> **KAI:** In my city, you pay with your memories.
> **KAI:** I've sold two hundred memories of the girl I love. This is the last one.

*(Card: I'VE SOLD 200 MEMORIES OF HER)*

## THE CITY · 0:10
*(The neon city in the rain. Over the street, a giant holo-ad: a rich couple smiling inside a glowing memory.)*
> **KAI:** The rich buy memories, so they can feel things they never lived. The rest of us sell them, just to eat.

*(Inside the market: a chair, a cable to the temple, a vial filling with light.)*
> **KAI:** A machine pulls the memory out of your head and into a vial. And once it's sold, it's gone from you.
> Forever.

## HOW THEY MET · 0:20 *(added in v2, approved)*
*(A memory, in gold: five years earlier, the same market. Kai, twelve and soaked, in the extraction chair. A
silver-haired girl pulls the cable away and pushes a steaming bowl of noodles into his hands.)*
> **KAI:** The first time I saw her, I was twelve. I was about to sell a memory of my mum. For a bowl of noodles.
> **LUMI:** Don't sell that one. Here. Now you owe me a memory. A good one.
> **KAI:** I don't have any good ones.
> **LUMI:** Then we'll make some.

## LUMI · 0:34
*(A warm memory, in golden light. A rooftop at night. A silver-haired girl folds a paper star and puts it in Kai's
hand.)*
> **KAI:** Her name was Lumi. She folded paper stars and hummed songs she made up.
> **KAI:** And every time we met, she'd say the same thing.
> **LUMI:** Remember me?
> **KAI:** And I'd say…
> **KAI** *(in the memory, smiling)*: Always.

## MAKING MEMORIES · 0:45 *(added in v2, approved)*
*(A golden montage: she teaches him to fold paper stars; they run through the rain, laughing; the rooftop at dawn.)*
> **KAI:** So we made them. Hundreds of them.
> **LUMI:** One day, when we're old, we'll sit up here and remember every single one.

## THE GREY · 0:55
*(A tiny flat. Lumi in bed, pale, coughing; grey veins on her wrist. A bottle of pills with a price tag.)*
> **KAI:** Then she got sick. The grey sickness. The medicine cost more than I'd ever earn.
*(Added in v2, approved: Lumi, pale, grabs his sleeve.)*
> **LUMI:** Kai. Promise me you'll never sell us. Not even to save me.
> **KAI:** I promise.
> **KAI** *(narrating)*: I lied.
> **KAI:** So I sold the one thing I had. Her.

*(The market chair. A memory plays in gold: their first kiss on the rooftop. Then it drains out of the frame, colour
first, into the vial.)*
> **KAI:** Our first kiss paid for a month of medicine.
> **KAI:** The night we met paid for two.

*(A counter on the market screen: KAI · MEMORIES OF LUMI: 212… 140… 61… 9.)*

## THE COST · 1:00
*(Lumi's bedside. She's a little better now. She smiles at him.)*
> **KAI:** It worked. She got better. But every time I visited, she felt a little more like a stranger.
> **LUMI:** Remember me?

*(Kai hesitates. A long beat. He can't answer.)*
> **KAI:** I knew I loved her. I just couldn't remember why.

*(Lumi's smile breaks. She looks away.)*

## THE LAST ONE · 1:20
*(Back to the cold open: the market counter, the last vial, her fading face. The Broker leans in.)*
> **KAI:** So here I am. The last memory I have of her: her face.
> **BROKER:** Last one, kid. After this, she's a stranger.
> **KAI:** Then she lives. Sell it.

*(The vial goes into the Broker's case. Kai's eyes go blank. He stands, lost, in the crowd.)*

## THE BUYER · 1:35
*(At the next counter, a hooded figure slides a case of vials across: dozens of them, every one gold.)*
> **KAI:** At the next counter, someone in a hood was buying. Not one memory. All of them.

*(The hood falls back: silver hair. Lumi. She doesn't notice Kai. She takes the vials and walks away.)*
> **KAI:** It was Lumi. I didn't know her face anymore. I only know because she told me.

## THE TWIST · 1:45
*(Outside, in the rain. Lumi finds Kai on the steps. She presses the first vial to his temple. Gold light floods the
frame: the rooftop, the paper star, the first kiss, all of it coming back.)*
> **KAI:** She'd been selling her own memories, to buy mine back. Her childhood. Her family. Her whole life.
> **KAI:** All except one.

*(She sits down beside him, in the rain, a little lost, smiling.)*
> **LUMI:** I don't remember much anymore. But I remember you.
> **LUMI:** Remember me?
> **KAI** *(to Lumi)*: Always.

*(Silent end card: WHAT MEMORY WOULD YOU NEVER SELL? 👇)*
