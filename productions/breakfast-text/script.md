# Don't Eat It. I'm Still at Work. (Part 1 of a vote series: viewer script for the owner's approval)

**Format:** an "Imagine…" POV mystery from `productions/research/winning-patterns.md`, about 70 seconds:
- an everyday setting (your kitchen, 7:42 a.m.);
- three rules;
- two voices you trust that contradict each other;
- one planted clue you can rewatch for;
- it ends on a binary choice, and the most-liked answer decides Part 2.

**Voices:**
| Voice | Who | Caption colour |
|---|---|---|
| The narrator | a calm adult storyteller, second person ("you") | white |
| Mum in the kitchen | Mum's voice, warm and close | warm orange |
| Mum at the door | the same voice, muffled through the door, panicked | cool blue |
| The texts | appear on the phone screen and are read in Mum's voice through a phone filter | phone-screen white |

**Premise (one sentence):** your mum is cooking your breakfast, but she texts you that she's still at work and not to
eat it, and then someone with her voice starts knocking at the front door.

---

## FRAME 1 · 0:00
*(Close on Mum at the stove: smiling, flipping a pancake, humming. Sound from frame 1: sizzle, humming. Your phone
lights up on the table in the foreground.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Imagine your mum is making you breakfast. Then she texts you.

*(Insert: the phone screen, "MUM 💗".)*
> **TEXT:** Don't eat it. I'm still at work.

## WHY IT'S IMPOSSIBLE · 0:06
*(The kitchen clock: 7:42. Mum's hospital lanyard hangs on the hook by the door.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Mum works nights at the hospital. Her shift doesn't end until nine. It's 7:42.

## THE CLUE · 0:11
*(Mum turns, beaming, and holds out the pan.)*
> **MUM (kitchen):** Pancakes, sweetheart. Your favourite.
> **NARRATOR:** Mum has never called you sweetheart. Not once. She calls you Bug.

*(v2, the owner's preview notes: a slow push into Mum's smile; heavy breathing.)*
> **BUG (thought, whispered):** Wait… who is that?

## THE RULES · 0:17
*(Buzz. The phone, in your lap under the table so she can't see.)*
> **NARRATOR:** You're given three rules to follow.
> **TEXT:** Rule one. Don't let her see you read this.
> **TEXT:** Rule two. Don't eat anything she gives you.
> **TEXT:** Rule three. Get to the front door. Walk. Don't run.

## ESCALATION · 0:27
*(She slides the plate in front of you: three pancakes, steam curling. You don't pick up the fork. Her smile doesn't
move.)*
> **MUM (kitchen):** You haven't touched it.

*(You stand up, slowly. The hallway, and the front door, are behind her.)*
> **NARRATOR:** The front door is behind her.

*(Buzz: loud, on the table. She looks at the phone, then at you.)*
> **MUM (kitchen):** Who's texting you… Bug?
> **NARRATOR:** She knows your name now.

## THE DOOR · 0:44
*(Three hard knocks at the front door.)*
> **MUM (door, muffled):** Bug? It's me! I left my phone at home, let me in!
> **BUG (thought, whispered):** That's… Mum's voice too.

*(Close: Mum's phone on the counter, next to the stove. It lights up: another text, sent. Nobody's touching it.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Mum's phone is on the counter. Right next to the stove. It lights up. Nobody's touching it.
> **TEXT:** Open the door. Now.

## THE CHOICE · 0:57
*(Kitchen Mum steps between you and the hallway, calm, smiling again.)*
> **MUM (kitchen):** Don't, Bug. That isn't me. I've been here all morning. I made your favourite.

*(Pounding at the door.)*
> **MUM (door):** Bug, whatever's in there, don't eat anything!
> **NARRATOR:** One of them is your mum. The one at the stove… or the one at the door? What do you do?

*(Panels pop in: **A · THE STOVE** on "stove", **B · THE DOOR** on "door", **WHAT DO YOU DO?** on "What". Cut to black within half a second.)*

**v2 (owner's preview notes, 21 Nov 2026):** darker, colder light after the first text and the dark closing in round the
frame; heavy breathing and heartbeats; Bug's whispered thoughts (caption pale yellow); the rules introduced by the
narrator; "What do you do?" at the end; a clear close-up of the pan; the clock as 7:42 AM inside the frame; a darker score.

**The rewatch clue for the pinned comment:** at 0:11 she calls you "sweetheart". At 0:38 she says "Bug" only after
your phone buzzes. Mum's lanyard is on the hook from 0:06, so did Mum ever leave?

## Production notes
- **Sets:** the existing home kitchen, and the hallway with the front door (from earlier productions).
- **Inserts:** the phone texts, the 7:42 clock, the lanyard, Mum's phone lighting up on the counter.
- **Sound (ElevenLabs):**
  - a sizzling pan and humming;
  - phone buzzes as beat markers;
  - knocks and pounding;
  - a heartbeat under "She knows your name now";
  - a near-silent drone.
- **Length:** about 70 s, with escalation every 5–10 s and no gap over 1.5 s.
- **The end card is part of the last line:** no silent call to action after it.
