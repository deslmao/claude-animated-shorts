# Spine evaluation packet (phase 1): Wet Paint

format (as claimed by the writer): mystery · factual: no (fiction)

## Premise
- protagonist: Mira, fifteen, who designed the school mural and photographs it every morning for the county art prize
- want: to win the prize with her mural, and stop whoever is spoiling it
- loses: her painted bench and her signature, which the roller would erase (shown: Mr. Okafor's warning, then the roller); and, once she understands, the person she thought she was
- obstacle: the painter is never seen at work, and the truth is about Mira herself
- conflict: A, Leo is vandalising the mural for revenge; B, someone is using the mural to tell a story about something that really happened

## Rules of the situation
- The mural is judged at 9:00 on Friday for the county youth art prize. Mira designed it. Its bottom corner is her painted bench under a tree, with her signature beneath.
- From Wednesday, Mr. Okafor's rule: one more picture and the corner, bench and signature included, is painted over before the judges.
- The school's cameras cover the gates and the bike sheds, not the courtyard wall.
- Breakfast club opens at 6:45. Children sign a new sheet each day at the hall door, which opens onto the courtyard. The staff stay in the hall kitchen until 7:30, and most children arrive after 7:30. Old sheets stay on the clipboard.
- The mural's paint tins sit under a tarp by the wall. The paint stays wet for under an hour.
- Leo, suspended for spray-painting the bike sheds, signs his six-year-old brother in at breakfast club at 6:55 and leaves.
- Mira arrives at 7:30 every day to photograph the mural with her camera, which hangs on an orange strap. She shares photos from it to her phone.
- Asha, new this term, is at breakfast club from 6:47 because her mum starts work early. She sits on the real bench by the wall, drawing.
