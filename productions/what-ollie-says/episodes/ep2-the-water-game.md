## Script
**INT. OLLIE'S BEDROOM. NIGHT. (HIDDEN-PHONE FOOTAGE)**
Grey-green infrared. Timestamp 03:00:02. The lens peers past a plastic stegosaurus. OLLIE asleep.
The door opens. A tall pale figure in a long nightdress crosses to the bed and kneels.

*Title: WHAT OLLIE SAYS AT 3 A.M. · PART 2*

Her face slides into the light: KATE. Eyes wide open, shining in the infrared. Tears on her cheeks. She doesn't blink.

**INT. AVA'S BEDROOM. DAWN.**
AVA at her desk, watching it on her old phone, hand over her mouth.

**ON THE FOOTAGE:** Kate takes Ollie's hand. His eyes open, sleepy, unsurprised.

**OLLIE [PL provisional L1]:** *Mamo, woda jest przy drzwiach.*
*Subtitle:* Mama, the water's at the door.

**KATE** *(whisper) [PL provisional L2]:* *Kubuś, trzymaj mnie za rękę.*
*Subtitle:* Kuba, hold my hand.

Ollie climbs out of bed and holds her hand. They walk.

**KATE** *(whisper) [PL provisional L3]:* *Nie puszczę cię. Obiecuję.*
*Subtitle:* I won't let go of you. I promise.

They leave the frame. Ava's lips shape the word: *Kuba?*

She drags the timeline. 03:01. 03:05. 03:09. At 03:09:40 Ollie comes back alone. He climbs into bed and lies with his eyes open, staring at the ceiling.

Ava lays her first recording over the new one. Two waveforms slide together and match perfectly, down to the pause.

**INT. KITCHEN. MORNING.**
Kate at the stove, her back to them. Ava slides onto the bench next to Ollie.

**AVA** *(low)*: What do you do at night?

**OLLIE:** The water game.

**AVA:** What's the water game?

**OLLIE:** Mama's sad at night. If I say my words, she stops crying.

**AVA:** Since when?

**OLLIE** *(shrugs)*: Since always.

**AVA:** Who's Kuba?

**OLLIE:** Me. At night-time.

At the stove, Kate's spoon stops. She doesn't turn round.

Ollie runs off for his school bag. Ava holds the phone out to her mother. Kate watches herself kneeling at her son's bed.

*FLASH (2008): a rope tears through a palm.*

**KATE:** …When was this?

**AVA:** Last night. Mum, you were—

**KATE:** Delete it.

She takes the phone and deletes the video herself.

**KATE:** Don't tell your father. Please.

**AVA:** Who's Kuba?

**KATE:** It's a dream I have.

She goes. Ava is left alone in the kitchen.

**INT. KATE'S BEDROOM. EVENING.**
Kate locks her own door from the inside and drops the key into her cardigan pocket. She sits on the bed by the window. Below the sill, rain hammers the flat roof of the kitchen extension. She presses her scarred palm to her mouth.

**INT. OLLIE'S BEDROOM. NIGHT.**
Out on the landing, a key turns in a lock. Ava tucks Ollie in.

**OLLIE:** Three knocks?

**AVA:** Three knocks.

She hesitates.

**AVA:** Ollie. Who's Kuba? Really?

**OLLIE** *(as if it's obvious)*: The boy who was here before me.

**AVA:** …Here?

**OLLIE** *(eyes closing)*: He's in Mama's tin.

Ava looks out across the landing, at her mother's locked door.

CUT TO BLACK. *End card: PART 3: WIERZBNO*
