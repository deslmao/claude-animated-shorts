## Research summary
The men's marathon at the St. Louis Olympics (30 August 1904) was run in about 90 °F heat on a hilly course of dusty
roads, with cars churning up dust alongside the runners and only one water source, a well at about the
12-mile (halfway) mark. 32 men started and 14 finished. Fred Lorz crossed the line first but had ridden much of
the course in a car. He was disqualified and banned for life (the ban was soon reduced), and won the 1905 Boston
Marathon legitimately. The official winner, Thomas Hicks, was kept going by his handlers with strychnine sulphate
(a legal stimulant then), egg white and brandy. He finished in 3:28:53, the slowest Olympic winning time. Cuba's
Félix Carvajal ran in cut-down street clothes after losing his money gambling on the way, ate rotten apples, lay down
for a nap and still finished fourth. Len Taunyane of South Africa was chased about a mile off course by a dog or dogs
and finished ninth. William Garcia nearly died from the dust (a stomach haemorrhage).

## Script
1. A car ride. Rat poison. A nap. This was the 1904 Olympic marathon.
2. Thirty-two runners, ninety-degree heat, dusty roads, and one official water stop.
3. The organizer reportedly wanted to study dehydration.
4. Fred Lorz got cramps at mile nine and rode the next eleven in a car.
5. When it broke down, he jogged in and finished first.
6. **milo:** It was a joke!
7. Officials didn't laugh. He was banned for life.
8. The real winner, Thomas Hicks, was fed egg whites, brandy and strychnine. Rat poison, but legal back then.
9. He staggered home in three hours twenty-eight minutes, the slowest Olympic winning time ever.
10. Cuba's Félix Carvajal gambled away his travel money, ran in street clothes, ate rotten apples, napped... and still finished fourth.
11. South Africa's Len Taunyane was chased a mile off course by a dog, and still finished ninth.
12. You see, it was only the third Olympic marathon: no drug rules, little water, and car dust that nearly killed one runner.
13. Today, races hand out water, and strychnine is banned.
14. And Lorz? His ban was cut short, and next spring he won the Boston Marathon. On foot.
