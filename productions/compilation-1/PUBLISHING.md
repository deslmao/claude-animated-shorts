# Long-form compilation #1: "5 Stories That Shouldn't Be Possible" (about 12:50)

**Why:**
- It builds the YouTube watch hours that monetization needs; Shorts-feed watch time doesn't count toward them.
- It reuses our own finished Shorts, so there's no new production.
- It runs past 8 minutes, so mid-roll ads become possible once the channel is monetized.

## How to edit it in CapCut (about 20 minutes)
1. **New project → ratio 16:9 (1920 × 1080).**
2. **Import the six final mp4s in the order above.** Use the final versions you posted, not previews.
3. **Background:** select all clips → Background → **Blur** (or a dark colour). The vertical video sits in the centre
   with its own blurred copy behind it.
4. **Trim the voting end cards.** Remove any end card longer than about 1 s ("Comment A or B", "Would you…"). Viewers
   leave during calls to action. Keep Ollie Part 1's ending, because it leads straight into Part 2.
5. **No transitions,** just straight cuts. Optionally add a 1-second title text at the start of each story (its title,
   top of frame).
6. **Don't add music.** Every story already has its own sound mix.
7. **Export at 1080p, 30 fps.**
