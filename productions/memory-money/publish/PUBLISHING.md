## Which version to post
- **To use the trending songs:** post the **no-music master** and add the song in the app's own sound picker. Songs
  from the app's library are licensed for Shorts and TikTok, so they shouldn't trigger a claim.
- **For zero claim risk:** post the **scored version** as it is.

### Where the songs go (no-music master)
| Time | Scene | Song |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00 – 0:19** | the hook, "In my city…", the memory market | **Numbers (Tempo)** |
| **0:19 – 2:11** | how they met → the ending | **Fallen Down (slowed)** |

- **One song only (most apps allow just one added sound):** use **Fallen Down (slowed)** from 0:00, and keep it low
  (about 15–25 % in the app's volume slider) so the voices stay clear.
- **Both songs:** you'd edit outside the app (for example in CapCut), crossfading at 0:19. Songs added outside the
  app's library can be claimed, and that risk is on that upload.
