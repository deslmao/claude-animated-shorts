## Clarity block (the storytelling gate: everything the viewer must be told, and the words that tell it)

- **Premise:** in Leo's world everyone has a floating power level, and the number decides who gets respect. Leo is Level 1 and bullied; a secret quest system lets him level up in secret, and he beats the bully with a single flick.
- **Power levels exist:** "everyone has a floating power level" (line 1), "Everyone's level glows above their head" (line 2).
- **A high number means respect:** "the higher your number, the more the whole school listens to you" (line 2), then "Teachers bowed to the big numbers..." (line 3).
- **Dex is the Level 50 bully, Leo is Level 1:** "Dex is Level 50. He makes sure I never forget it." (line 4). The books and homework are explained in line 6.
- **The strange window is a quest:** "A blue window floated right in front of my face. It said: Secret Quest..." (line 8). The rule and the penalty are read out in full.
- **Why Leo hides his number:** "I kept my hoodie up and my number hidden. Dex still thought I was Level 1, and I let him." (line 15).
- **Dex's callback line** ("the higher the number, the more people listen") only works because line 2 said it first.
- No line relies on knowledge the viewer wasn't given. Characters speak only after the narrator has set them up (Dex speaks after line 4).

## Audio plan (Step 2, after approval)

- **Score:** Morning Mood (public domain, the channel signature) as the baseline, -14 LUFS, scored and no-music dual export. Ducks **8 dB** under every RPG chime, System Window pop-up and the final bass drop.
- **New SFX needed:** sharp RPG level-up chime, system pop-up blip, typing ticks, a bass drop, a shockwave boom, a finger-flick whoosh, a slide skid, a crowd gasp and roar, a gym ambience. Existing: clock tick, book thud, dribble, crowd roar, whoosh, squeaks, heartbeat.
- **Moments of silence/impact:** a hush before the hoodie drop, then the **bass drop on 'Level 99'**, and a low sting on the unreadable '???' badge.

## What has to be built in Step 2 (honest list)

1. **`lvlBadge` prop:** a crisp 2D-canvas floating badge (LVL n, colour by tier, gold for 99), sitting above the head with a clean margin, with a number roll and an easeOutBack pop. Never over a face or in the caption band.
2. **`systemWindow` prop:** a glowing blue 2D-canvas window with typed lines, in the upper frame with clean margins (the existing `statGraphic` and phone props are the starting point).
3. **`e4Hallway` set** (lockers, fluorescent light) and **`e4Gym` set** (a school gym built from the arena interior: hardwood, banners, packed bleachers). The bedroom and the court are reused unchanged.
4. **A pull-up bar prop** for the park beats, and a **camera blur-in** (0.45 s, 14 px to 0, easeOutQuad). I found no existing blur-keyframe feature, so this is a small opt-in engine addition, Engine 4 only.
5. **Motion** (Dex's charge, the hoodie drop, push-ups, sprint, pull-up, the finger flick and Dex's slide) as eased procedural moves in the existing motion system (`easeOutQuad`, `easeOutBack`, `easeInOutCubic`; no linear).
