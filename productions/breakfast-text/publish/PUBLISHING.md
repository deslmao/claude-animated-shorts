## Before you post
1. **The captions are burned in, one colour per speaker:**
   - white: the narrator;
   - orange: Mum in the kitchen;
   - blue: Mum at the door;
   - pink: the texts;
   - pale yellow: your own thoughts.

   Don't turn on the apps' auto-captions.
2. **AI disclosure.** The voices, effects and score were generated with ElevenLabs.
   - **YouTube:** "No" is reasonable for a cartoon.
   - **TikTok and Instagram:** the AI-generated label is the transparent choice.
3. **Post one Short a day at most,** with no VPN. Pin the comment straight away: the vote drives the comments.
4. **Part 2** opens on the result ("Most of you said the door…"), so check which answer has the most likes after
   about 48 hours.
