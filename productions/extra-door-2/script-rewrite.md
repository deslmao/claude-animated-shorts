## Continuity kept from Part 1
- **The hallway:** seven doors, with an eighth, old door at the end. It has no handle.
- **The photo:** of Milo and Juno, found on the floor, leaning against the old door, with the bare nail above:
  "...that's where our picture hangs."
- **The knocks and the voice:** the clock stopped, three knocks came, and Juno's voice spoke from behind the door,
  the same take twice.
- **The texts:** Milo texted "are you home?". The real Juno replied "No, I'm still at work. Why?". The voice said
  "Don't text her."
- **The handle:** it grew through the wood and turned, and the latch clicked.
- **The vote:** the audience chose **B: RUN DOWNSTAIRS**.

## The door's rules, each shown on screen before the climax
1. **It travels through photographs:** it appears on the wall wherever a photo of the people it copies hangs.
   - **Seen:** the street posters; the monitor (Day 1: a photo pinned in the test room); the lab wall.
2. **It imitates the voice you trust most:**
   - **Seen:** the fake text; the lab-door test; the monitor (Day 6); both voices at the climax.
3. **Removing the photo doesn't stop it.**
   - **Seen:** the monitor (Day 9: a technician takes the photo down and the wood keeps spreading); Juno's failed
     attempt to pull hers off the wall.
4. **It only grows while something watches it** (the foreshadowing for the climax):
   - **Seen on the street:** the poster's handle pushes out while Milo stares and freezes when he looks away.
   - **Seen on the monitor:** on Day 11 the camera feed cuts out for a second, and when it returns the door has
     shrunk.
   - **At the climax:** Milo connects the two clues; Juno realises the lab cameras are still watching it.

**Why Juno studied it and didn't warn him:**
- **Why she studied it:** her team recovered the door from a burned-out house on a demolition job. She is the
  researcher assigned to it, and she spoke to it every day, which is how it learned her voice.
- **Why she used their photo:** she pinned her photo with Milo in the test room as bait, to see whether it would
  move to it. It did.
- **Why she didn't warn him:** it broke out *tonight*. When Milo texted her in Part 1 she didn't know yet. Then she
  assumed it was coming for *her*, not him.
- **How it's shown:** the monitor log does the explaining (dated clips and a breach timestamp), so her lines stay
  short.
