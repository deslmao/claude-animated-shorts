## Cover
**File:** `cover.jpg` (1080 × 1920). One design for all three apps.

**What it shows:** frame 1060 of the film. You are frozen in bed at night, eyes wide ("And you haven't moved at all").
Below, on a dark band: "MY PHONE SAYS I WALKED" / **"4,012 STEPS"** in orange / "I NEVER LEFT MY BED." It doesn't
give the dog away. Nothing is painted in; the picture is the engine's render of that frame.

**Placement:**
- The face sits at about y 380–1000 and the text at about y 1200–1590, both inside Instagram's 3:4 grid crop
  (y 240–1680).
- Inside the player, the apps' bottom text can cover the last line. The number stays clear.

**How to set it:**
- **YouTube Shorts:** upload `cover.jpg` if the Details page offers it. Otherwise choose the frame at **0:35**.
- **TikTok:** in "Edit cover", add `cover.jpg` from your photos, or pick the frame at **0:35**.
- **Instagram Reels:** in "Edit cover", choose "Add from camera roll" and pick `cover.jpg`. Then check the grid preview.

## Before you post
1. **The captions are burned in.** Don't turn on the apps' auto-captions, or the text will appear twice.
2. **AI disclosure.** The narration is synthetic (ElevenLabs).
   - **YouTube:** the altered-content label is for realistic footage, so "No" is reasonable for this cartoon.
   - **TikTok and Instagram:** the AI-generated label is the transparent choice.
3. **Music and sound effects** are original (synthesised in-house), so no copyright claim is expected.
4. **Pin the comment after the video is live.** YouTube doesn't allow pinning on a scheduled video.
5. **First-day check:** look at YouTube Studio's retention graph at 0:10. Above about 80% means the hook works.
