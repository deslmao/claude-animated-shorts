# The truth about buried pirate treasure
mood: adventure

- Pirates almost never buried treasure, and the one famous pirate who did lost all of it.
- Picture a pirate crew after a big raid. Chests of coins, silk, and jewels.
- Bury it on a desert island? Hardly anyone did.
- Loot was shared out straight away, and most pirates spent their share fast, on food, drink, and gambling in port.
- Burying a fortune and sailing off was a great way to never see it again.
- But in 1699, Captain William Kidd did it anyway.
- Hunted as a pirate, he hid gold and jewels on Gardiners Island, near New York.
- kidd: Nobody will ever find it!
- Soon after his arrest, the authorities dug it all up and shipped it to London, as evidence against him.
- Kidd was hanged in 1701.
- So where did the legend of buried treasure come from?
- You see, in 1883, Robert Louis Stevenson wrote Treasure Island, with a hidden hoard and a map where X marks the spot.
- Historians have never found a single real pirate treasure map.
- But the story was so good that it buried the real history.
- So the most famous pirate treasure of all time was hidden by a novelist.
