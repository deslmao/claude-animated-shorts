# Don't Eat It. I'm Still at Work.: viewer script

What the viewer sees and hears, in order. Nothing else reaches them.

**1.** _frame 1: Mum at the stove, smiling, humming, a pancake flipping in the pan; in the foreground your phone lights up on the table_
> **NARRATOR:** Imagine your mum is making you breakfast. Then she texts you.

**2.** _your phone: MUM: Don't eat it. I'm still at work._
> **bfText (voice-over):** Don't eat it. I'm still at work.

**3.** _Mum's hospital lanyard on its hook: NURSE · NIGHT SHIFT 9 PM – 9 AM_
> **NARRATOR:** Mum works nights at the hospital. Her shift doesn't end until nine.

**4.** _the oven clock: 07:42_
> **NARRATOR:** It's 7:42.

**5.** _Mum turns from the stove, beaming, and holds out the pan to you (over your shoulder)_
> **MUM:** Pancakes, sweetheart. Your favourite.

**6.** _you, close: the word sweetheart sinks in_
> **NARRATOR:** Mum has never called you sweetheart. Not once. She calls you Bug.

**7.** _the phone in your lap under the table: rule one; rule two_
> **bfText (voice-over):** Rule one. Don't let her see you read this.
> **bfText (voice-over):** Rule two. Don't eat anything she gives you.

**8.** _past Mum's back at the stove: the arch, the hallway, the front door at the end of it_
> **bfText (voice-over):** Rule three. Get to the front door. Walk. Don't run.

**9.** _she slides the plate in front of you; you don't pick up the fork; her smile doesn't move_
> **MUM:** You haven't touched it.

**10.** _you stand up slowly: Mum between you and the arch, the front door at the end of the hallway behind her_
> **NARRATOR:** The front door is behind her.

**11.** _your phone buzzes on the table, loud, lighting up_

**12.** _Mum, close: she looks at the phone, then at you_
> **MUM:** Who's texting you… Bug?

**13.** _you, close, terrified_
> **NARRATOR:** She knows your name now.

**14.** _down the hallway: three hard knocks; a woman's silhouette in the front door's frosted glass_
> **bfMumDoor (voice-over):** Bug? It's me! I left my phone at home, let me in!

**15.** _Mum's pink phone on the counter by the stove: dark; it lights up by itself; Open the door. Now. Delivered._
> **NARRATOR:** Mum's phone is on the counter. Right next to the stove.
> **NARRATOR:** It lights up. Nobody's touching it.
> **bfText (voice-over):** Open the door. Now.

**16.** _Mum steps between you and the hallway, calm, smiling again; the silhouette behind the glass_
> **MUM:** Don't, Bug. That isn't me. I've been here all morning. I made your favourite.

**17.** _the front door, close: pounding, the leaf shaking, the silhouette_
> **bfMumDoor (voice-over):** Bug, whatever's in there, don't eat anything!

**18.** _the last frame: Mum smiling in front of the arch, the silhouette in the door glass beyond her; A · THE STOVE pops in by her, B · THE DOOR by the door; cut half a second after the last word_
> **NARRATOR:** One of them is your mum.
> **NARRATOR:** The one at the stove… or the one at the door?
