# Thinkora Video 08: "In This World, If Nobody Thinks About You, You Float Away". Script v2

**Format:** story-first emotional short with comedy beats, about 85–89 s, Milo narrating in the first person,
with in-scene dialogue.

**Status:** v2 rewrites v1 to the new story-density standard; awaiting approval. Nothing has been generated or
rendered.

**What v2 changes:** the same premise, twist and ending, with far more on-screen events. v1 told about 16
developments mostly through narration. v2 shows 38: more of them are carried by action, dialogue and visual gags,
and fewer by narration.

---

## Stage A: Concept (unchanged)

**Premise:** In this world, the more people think about you, the heavier you get. When Milo's grandpa Gus starts
floating away, Milo has to make the whole world think about him.

**Characters:**
- **Milo:** a good kid who drifted away without noticing.
- **Gus:** the funniest, heaviest grandpa on the street. He cheats at everything, burns everything, and turns
  every problem into a joke, which is exactly how he hides his own.
- **Juno:** the classmate with two million followers who has never once been nice to Milo. Bored, eye-rolling,
  secretly decent.
- **The rival politician:** a heckler.
- **The movie star and his weary assistant.**

**The twist:** "He started floating the spring I stopped visiting. I was the last one."

**The ending:** a call to action: "He might be holding onto the counter."
