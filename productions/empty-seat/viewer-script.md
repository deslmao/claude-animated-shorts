# The Empty Seat: viewer script

What the viewer sees and hears, in order. Nothing else reaches them.

**1.** _cold open: a dark, snowy bus seen from your seat; frightened faces; a slap on the back door_
> **NARRATOR:** This handprint just hit the back of a moving school bus.

**2.** _cold open: the small handprint on the emergency door window, snow on the road behind_
> **NARRATOR:** In June.

**3.** _the time card types out over the bright June bus, seen from your seat_

**4.** _seat 13's empty bench by its fogged window, the 13 plate_
> **NARRATOR:** Every morning, your school bus has one seat nobody sits in. Seat thirteen. Even when the bus is packed, kids would rather stand.

**5.** _kids standing in the aisle beside the empty bench_

**6.** _Mr Hale at the wheel, a glance up at his mirror_
> **NARRATOR:** Mr Hale, the driver, says it belongs to a girl called Emily. Nobody has ever met her.

**7.** _Noah, the new kid in a red hoodie, walks up the aisle toward you, asking_
> **NARRATOR:** Then one day, a new kid gets on.
> **NOAH:** Is anyone sitting here?

**8.** _Noah in seat 13; every head turns to him_
> **NARRATOR:** He sits down in seat thirteen. The whole bus goes quiet. Mr Hale pulls over.

**9.** _Mr Hale turns round in his seat_
> **HALE:** Not that one, son. That's Emily's.

**10.** _Noah gets up and moves away; the seat is empty again_

**11.** _seat 13's window: fogged on the inside, bright June sun outside_
> **NARRATOR:** And here's what nobody can explain. It's June. It's hot. But the window by seat thirteen is always fogged up, from the inside. As if someone is breathing on it.

**12.** _the doors fold open on the empty Elm Rd stop; he counts quickly; they shut_
> **NARRATOR:** Every day, Mr Hale stops at Elm Road, even though that stop was taken away a long time ago. Nobody is ever there. He opens the doors and counts to three.
> **HALE:** One, two, three.

**13.** _at home on your bed: your phone: elm road bus stop -> STOP CLOSED 1996_
> **NARRATOR:** When you get home, you can't stop thinking about it. Why does your driver always stop at Elm Road? So you look it up.

**14.** _1996: the bus passes the Elm Rd stop in a snowstorm; a small girl waves behind the snowbank_
> **NARRATOR:** In 1996, in a snowstorm, the bus drove straight past the Elm Road stop. The driver didn't see the little girl waiting behind the snow.

**15.** _the 1996 yearbook memorial page: EMILY SHAW, aged 11_
> **NARRATOR:** Her name was Emily Shaw. She was eleven. She tried to walk to school, and she never made it.

**16.** _Mr Hale at the wheel; he looks up at seat 13 in his mirror and says good morning to her_
> **NARRATOR:** The driver that morning was a young Mr Hale. Every day since, he stops for her and waits three seconds, just long enough for her to climb on. And he keeps her seat.
> **HALE:** Morning, Emily.

**17.** _a new driver, a young woman, at the wheel; the bus radio_
> **NARRATOR:** Then one morning, Mr Hale got sick. A new driver covered his route.
> **esRadio (voice-over):** Bus nine, Ron Hale's in hospital. You're covering his route.
> **SUB:** Copy that.

**18.** _from your seat, you call up the bus to the new driver; she answers over her shoulder_
> **NARRATOR:** She doesn't know about Elm Road. So you tell her.
> **esYouBoy (voice-over):** Miss, you have to stop at Elm Road!
> **SUB:** There's no stop at Elm Road, hon.

**19.** _the Elm Rd stop flashing past the window_
> **NARRATOR:** She drives right past it.

**20.** _the slap: a small handprint on the outside of the emergency door window_
> **NARRATOR:** Then something slaps the back of the bus. A small handprint, on the outside of the emergency door. While the bus is moving.

**21.** _the bus in a snowstorm: white windows, dark cold light, frost, frightened faces_
> **NARRATOR:** The windows turn white. Outside, it's snowing. In June. Just like the morning Emily was left behind. She's been left behind again.

**22.** _you run up the aisle toward the driver_
> **NARRATOR:** So you run to the front and tap the driver's shoulder.
> **esYouBoy (voice-over):** Stop! Please! Go back!

**23.** _your hand on her shoulder; she turns, startled_

**24.** _the doors open on the storm; you count quickly; they shut_
> **NARRATOR:** She stops. The doors open. And you give Emily her three seconds.
> **esYouBoy (voice-over):** One, two, three.

**25.** _sunlight floods back; seat 13's window fogs up again_
> **NARRATOR:** The snow stops. And the window by seat thirteen fogs up again. Emily made it on.

**26.** _a hospital heart monitor: HALE, R.; the line goes flat_
> **NARRATOR:** That night, Mr Hale died in hospital. He'd been very sick for a long time.

**27.** _seat 13's window fogged; the camera turns across the aisle: seat 14's window fogged too_
> **NARRATOR:** The next morning, the window by seat fourteen is fogged up too. Mr Hale is riding with her now, so she'll never be left behind again.

**28.** _a boy about to sit in seat 14 stops_
> **NARRATOR:** A boy goes to sit there. The girl next to you stops him.
> **esPriya (voice-over):** Don't. That's Mr Hale's.

**29.** _end card: WOULD YOU SIT IN SEAT 13? YES / NO_
