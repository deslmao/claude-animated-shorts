## Script
**EXT. FLAT ROOF. NIGHT.**
Rain. KATE stands on the low brick edge of the roof, holding OLLIE's arm out over the black, flooded garden.
AVA climbs out of the window on her hands and knees.

*Title: WHAT OLLIE SAYS AT 3 A.M. · PART 5: WAKE HER*

She gets behind her mother, grips her shoulders and shakes her.

**AVA:** MUM! WAKE UP!

Kate blinks. For the first time, she blinks. Her eyes come into focus.

*Flicker: a roof in 2008, black water, a boat gone. Now: a flooded garden, a small hand in hers.*

**KATE:** KUBA!

She lurches. Her knee skids on the wet felt toward the edge.

**OLLIE:** Mummy!

Her scarred hand stays locked round his wrist. She throws her weight back and drags him against her chest. Ava wraps her arms round both of them. All three crash back against the wall under the window. Breathing. Rain.

Kate stares at Ollie. At Ava. At the drowned garden. At her own bare feet.

**KATE** *(shaking)*: Why am I out here?

**OLLIE:** It's okay, Mummy. It's the water game.

**INT. KITCHEN. 4 A.M.**
Towels. A kettle. Ava turns the laptop round: the grid of 1,214.
Kate watches herself teaching a three-year-old Ollie his words. Then the garden cam: Ollie running down the lawn after her, into the river.

**KATE:** I did this… to him?

**OLLIE** *(wrapped in a towel)*: I kept you out of the water.

Kate folds over, sobbing into her scarred hand.
Ava slides her phone across the table: Ewa's message, in Polish. Kate reads it in her own language. Her lips move.

**KATE:** …Jakub.

*FLASH (2008): the willow. The belt. A helicopter's light.*

**KATE:** I let go.

**AVA:** You let go into the boat, Mum. That's why he's alive.

Kate opens her right hand and looks at the scar as if she's never seen it.

**INT. KITCHEN. DAWN.**
The front door. MARK, soaked, stops in the doorway. Kate stands.

**KATE:** Mark… I had a son.

A long beat. He sits down slowly and takes her scarred hand.

**EXT. 4 FERRY LANE. WEEKS LATER.**
A removal van. Mark carries a box past Kate without a word.

**INT. NEW HOUSE. OLLIE'S ROOM. DAY.**
Ollie at an unfamiliar window.

**OLLIE:** Who'll help Mama now?

**AVA:** We will. Awake.

**INT. NEW HOUSE. KITCHEN. DAY.**
A video call on the laptop. A young man, JAKUB, 21: dark curls, Ollie's eyes. A long silence.

**JAKUB [PL provisional B1]:** *Mamo?*
*Subtitle:* Mum?

**KATE [PL provisional B2]:** *Kubuś.*
*Subtitle:* Kuba.

**JAKUB [PL provisional B3]:** *Dlaczego mnie nie szukałaś?*
*Subtitle:* Why didn't you look for me?

**KATE [PL provisional B4]:** *Myślałam, że cię zabiłam.*
*Subtitle:* I thought I'd killed you.

She holds her scarred palm up to the camera. He looks at it for a long time. Then he slowly raises his own hand to the screen.
Ollie squeezes into the frame.

**OLLIE:** Hello. I'm Ollie. I was you at night.

Jakub laughs through his tears.

**INT. NEW HOUSE. OLLIE'S BEDROOM. NIGHT. (NURSERY CAM)**
*CLIP 1,215 · 03:00.*
The door opens. Kate, awake. She blinks. She smiles. She kneels by Ollie's bed.

**KATE:** No more water game. Goodnight, Ollie.

**OLLIE** *(sleepy)*: Night, Mummy.

She kisses his forehead and goes.
Three soft knocks on the wall. Ollie knocks back three.

CUT TO BLACK. *End card: WHAT OLLIE SAYS AT 3 A.M. · END*
