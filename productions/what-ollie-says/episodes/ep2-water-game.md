# Part 2 · The Water Game: viewer script v2 (for the owner's approval)

**Premise (one sentence):** every night at 3 a.m. your mum sleepwalks back into the flood where she lost her son Kuba,
and your little brother Ollie speaks Kuba's words, which are the only thing that can bring her back out of the river.

**Built like Seat 13:**
- George narrates in the second person ("you" are Ava, 15) and explains every strange thing the moment it appears.
- Every character is introduced before they speak. Each speaker has their own caption colour.
- Every Polish line is subtitled.

**Follows the locked story** (`../story-lock.md`, `../series-outline.md`): all six ritual lines, in order, word for word.
**Length:** about 2:00.

---

## COLD OPEN
*(3 a.m., rain. The black river behind the house. Mum, in her nightdress, wading in up to her waist. Your hand grabs
her arm.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Three in the morning. Your mum is walking into the river, fast asleep.
> **AVA:** MUM!
> **NARRATOR:** She can't hear you. Only one voice can reach her. And it isn't yours.

*(Typewriter card: 24 HOURS EARLIER)*

## THE RECAP
*(Quick flashes from Part 1: Ollie sitting up in bed; the wet photo; the flooded garden.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Last time: your little brother Ollie started talking in his sleep. In Polish. In a baby's voice. He's
> six, and he's never learned a word of it.
> **NARRATOR:** Then you found an old photo your mum hides: a little boy called Kuba, from two thousand and eight. He
> looks exactly like Ollie.
> **NARRATOR:** And at three this morning, Mum sleepwalked Ollie out into the garden. It was flooded with black water.
> Only your garden. And you filmed it.

## DAWN
*(Grey morning. The lawn is wet but there's no water. Your phone lies face down in the grass.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Now it's morning. The water's gone, as if it was never there. But your phone is still lying in the
> grass, where you dropped it.

*(Insert: the phone video. Black water across your lawn, stopping dead at the fence.)*
> **NARRATOR:** On the video, the water stops dead at your fence. Next door's lawn is bone dry.

*(The fence. Your neighbour leans over it with a mug of tea.)*
> **NARRATOR:** You ask the man next door.
> **NEIGHBOUR:** Flood? What flood?

## THE VIDEO
> **NARRATOR:** Most of you said: show her the video. So you do.

*(The kitchen. Mum watches the phone. Her face goes white.)*
> **NARRATOR:** She watches herself lead Ollie into the water. Then she tells you the truth.
> **KATE:** Kuba was my son. Your brother. When he was three, there was a flood. I let go of him. And he drowned.
> **NARRATOR:** To save him, she held him out over the water, to a rescue boat. She let go of his hand. Then the boat
> went under.

*(Close on Mum. A whisper.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Then she says something her grandmother used to say.
> **KATE** *(Polish)*: Co puścisz nad wodą, to woda zabierze. *(What you let go of over the water, the water takes.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Every night, in her sleep, she's back in that flood, letting go of Kuba. Only now, the boy whose hand
> she lets go of is Ollie.

> **NARRATOR:** Your dad works nights. He doesn't even know Kuba existed.
> **KATE:** Please. Don't tell your dad.

## THE PLAN
> **NARRATOR:** So you also try option two: keep Ollie away from her.
> **NARRATOR:** Tonight, Mum locks herself in her bedroom. Ollie sleeps in your room, with you. If she sleepwalks, she
> can't get to him.

*(Insert: your bedroom door. A handle, no lock.)*
> **NARRATOR:** There's just one problem. Your door doesn't lock.

## 3 A.M.
*(Insert: your clock turns 2:59, then 3:00. Ollie, beside you, sits up with his eyes shut.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Three a.m. Ollie sits up, still asleep. And the baby voice comes out of him again.
> **KUBA'S VOICE** *(Polish)*: Mamo, woda jest przy drzwiach. *(Mama, the water's at the door.)*
> **NARRATOR:** That's what Kuba said to wake her up, the night of the flood.

*(You hold Ollie. He goes limp, still asleep. A click across the landing.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Then, across the landing, Mum's lock clicks open. She's unlocking it in her sleep.

*(Under your door: the strip of landing light. Two feet stop in it.)*
> **NARRATOR:** She stops outside your door.
> **KATE** *(a whisper)*: Kubuś? *(Kuba?)*
> **NARRATOR:** She's looking for Kuba. And in her sleep, Ollie is Kuba.

*(Insert: your hand gripping the door handle. A heartbeat.)*
> **NARRATOR:** You hold the handle shut.
> **NARRATOR:** She doesn't try the door. Her footsteps go back down the stairs.

## THE RIVER
*(A beat of silence. Then the back door bangs.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Then you hear the back door.

*(From your window: the garden under black water again. Mum walking down the lawn into the river, alone.)*
> **NARRATOR:** From your window, you see her walking into the river. Without Ollie. She's still looking for him, in
> the water.
> **KATE** *(far off)*: Kubuś! *(Kuba!)*
> **AVA:** MUM!
> **NARRATOR:** She can't hear you.

*(You lay Ollie, asleep, on your bed.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Ollie is still asleep. You leave him on your bed and run.
> **AVA:** Stay here!

*(The river. You grab Mum's arm. She doesn't even look at you.)*
> **NARRATOR:** You grab her arm. She doesn't feel it. She keeps walking, deeper. She's stronger than you. If you
> don't let go, you'll both go under.

## THE EDGE
*(Behind you, at the edge of the water: Ollie, barefoot, eyes shut.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Then, behind you, a small voice. Ollie followed you outside.
> **KUBA'S VOICE:** Mamo, woda jest przy drzwiach. *(Mama, the water's at the door.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Kuba's words. The only voice that can reach her.

*(Mum stops. She turns round and wades back to him.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Mum stops. She turns round, walks back out of the river, and takes Ollie's hand.
> **KATE:** Kubuś, trzymaj mnie za rękę. *(Kuba, hold my hand.)*
> **NARRATOR:** And the night of the flood plays out again, right here, at the edge of the water.
> **KATE** *(a whisper)*: Nie puszczę cię. Obiecuję. *(I won't let go of you. I promise.)*
> **NARRATOR:** This is where she held Kuba out to the boat.

*(Mum lifts Ollie's arm out toward the dark water.)*
> **KATE** *(a shout)*: Weź go! *(Take him!)*
> **KUBA'S VOICE:** Obiecałaś, że nie puścisz. *(You promised you wouldn't let go.)*
> **KATE:** Przepraszam. *(I'm sorry.)*

*(Insert: her fingers open. The current snatches Ollie.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Her hand opens, and the river pulls Ollie in. But you're already in the water. You catch him.
> **NARRATOR:** And Mum turns and walks back to bed, still asleep.

## THE ENDING
*(On the bank, soaked. Ollie is awake now, and calm, in his own voice.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Ollie is wide awake now. And he isn't even surprised.
> **OLLIE:** It's the water game. If I don't say my words, Mama goes in the water by herself.
> **NARRATOR:** Keeping them apart didn't stop it. It sent her into the river alone. Ollie's words are the only thing
> that brings her back. And every night, the water comes a little closer to the house.

*(Silent end card: PART 3 · Would you tell Dad? 1 = YES · 2 = NO)*
