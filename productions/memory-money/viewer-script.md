# I've Sold 200 Memories of the Girl I Love. This Is the Last One.: viewer script

What the viewer sees and hears, in order. Nothing else reaches them.

**1.** _the hook: a hand on a steel counter, a glowing vial, a girl's face inside it, fading_
> **NARRATOR:** In my city, you pay with your memories.
> **NARRATOR:** I've sold two hundred memories of the girl I love. This is the last one.

**2.** _the neon city in the rain; over the street the MEMORIA holo-ad, a rich couple inside a golden memory_
> **NARRATOR:** The rich buy memories, so they can feel things they never lived. The rest of us sell them, just to eat.

**3.** _the extraction room: a stranger in the chair, the Broker at the machine_
> **NARRATOR:** A machine pulls the memory out of your head and into a vial.

**4.** _the cable at the temple, light pouring into a vial_
> **NARRATOR:** And once it's sold, it's gone from you. Forever.

**5.** _a memory: Kai at twelve, soaked, in the extraction chair_
> **NARRATOR:** The first time I saw her, I was twelve. I was about to sell a memory of my mum. For a bowl of noodles.

**6.** _a silver-haired girl pulls the cable away and pushes a bowl of noodles into his hands_
> **LUMIKID:** Don't sell that one. Here.
> **LUMIKID:** Now you owe me a memory. A good one.

**7.** _Kai at twelve, close_
> **KAIKID:** I don't have any good ones.

**8.** _Lumi at twelve, close: the promise of a life_
> **LUMIKID:** Then we'll make some.

**9.** _a memory, in golden light: the rooftop, Lumi gives him a paper star_
> **NARRATOR:** Her name was Lumi. She folded paper stars and hummed songs she made up.

**10.** _Lumi, close, teasing_
> **NARRATOR:** And every time we met, she'd say the same thing.
> **LUMI:** Remember me?

**11.** _Kai, close, smiling_
> **NARRATOR:** And I'd say…
> **KAI:** Always.

**12.** _she teaches him to fold paper stars_
> **NARRATOR:** So we made them. Hundreds of them.

**13.** _running through the rain, laughing_

**14.** _the rooftop at dawn: their dream_
> **LUMI:** One day, when we're old, we'll sit up here and remember every single one.

**15.** _Lumi's tiny flat: she's pale, coughing; Kai beside the bed_
> **NARRATOR:** Then she got sick. The grey sickness. The medicine cost more than I'd ever earn.

**16.** _Lumi, pale, grabbing his sleeve_
> **LUMISICK:** Kai. Promise me you'll never sell us. Not even to save me.

**17.** _Kai, close: he promises, and already knows he won't keep it_
> **KAI:** I promise.
> **NARRATOR:** I lied.

**18.** _the grey veins on her wrist; GREYSTOP, 4,000 a month_
> **NARRATOR:** So I sold the one thing I had. Her.

**19.** _the memory of their first kiss, golden, drains to grey as it is sold_
> **NARRATOR:** Our first kiss paid for a month of medicine.

**20.** _Kai in the chair, eyes closed; the Broker draws out another_
> **NARRATOR:** The night we met paid for two.

**21.** _the seller's screen: MEMORIES OF LUMI 212, 140, 61, 9_

**22.** _the flat: Lumi well again, smiling; Kai, unsure_
> **NARRATOR:** It worked. She got better. But every time I visited, she felt a little more like a stranger.

**23.** _Lumi, close: the old joke_
> **LUMI:** Remember me?

**24.** _Kai, close: he can't answer_
> **NARRATOR:** I knew I loved her. I just couldn't remember why.

**25.** _Lumi's smile breaks; she looks away_

**26.** _back to the opening: the memory exchange, the last vial on the counter_
> **NARRATOR:** So here I am. The last memory I have of her: her face.

**27.** _the Broker, close: smooth, cold_
> **BROKER:** Last one, kid. After this, she's a stranger.

**28.** _Kai, close: he says it anyway_
> **KAI:** Then she lives. Sell it.

**29.** _the vial: her face fades out to nothing_

**30.** _Kai, lost, in the market; at counter B a hooded buyer_
> **NARRATOR:** At the next counter, someone in a hood was buying.

**31.** _the buyer's case: dozens of gold vials_
> **NARRATOR:** Not one memory. All of them.

**32.** _the hood falls back: silver hair. Lumi_
> **NARRATOR:** It was Lumi. I didn't know her face anymore. I only know because she told me.

**33.** _outside in the rain: Kai on the steps; Lumi presses the first vial to him; gold floods back_
> **NARRATOR:** She'd been selling her own memories, to buy mine back. Her childhood. Her family. Her whole life.

**34.** _the rooftop memory, back in gold_
> **NARRATOR:** All except one.

**35.** _side by side on the steps in the rain; she's lost, smiling_
> **LUMI:** I don't remember much anymore. But I remember you.
> **LUMI:** Remember me?

**36.** _Kai, close: he remembers_
> **KAI:** Always.

**37.** _end card: what memory would you never sell?_
