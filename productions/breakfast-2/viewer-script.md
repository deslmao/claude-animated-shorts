# Don't Eat It, Part 2: "She Picked Up" (viewer script for the owner's approval)

**Follows:** Part 1, "Mum's Making Breakfast. Then She Texts: 'Don't Eat It. I'm Still at Work.'" It reached 13.5K views
on Instagram. In the comments:
- the door won the vote;
- the top comment (41 likes) was "None of them. Your mum's at work";
- viewers asked "how did she text you if her phone is inside?"

Part 2 answers the top comment and turns that clue into the next scare.

**Format** (the format system plus what the numbers taught us):
- a lit kitchen on frame 0, with a two-line banner, and the hook read at a glance;
- about 50 s, a visual change every ~1.8 s, 5–6 setups;
- it ends on a new choice for Part 3.

**Voices:** the same cast as Part 1.
| Voice | Who | Caption colour |
|---|---|---|
| The narrator | calm, second person ("you") | white |
| Mum on the phone (the real one, at the hospital) | Mum's voice through a phone speaker | phone white |
| The thing at the stove | Mum's voice, warm and close | warm orange |
| The thing at the door | the same voice, muffled through the door | cool blue |
| Bug (thoughts) | whispered | pale yellow |

**Premise (one sentence):** you ring Mum's work and she answers from the hospital, so neither of the mums in your
house is her, and now both of them want to know who you're talking to.

---

## FRAME 0 · 0:00 (the hook)
*(The bright kitchen, morning light. Your hand in the foreground holds your phone: **Calling… Mum's work 🏥**. Behind it,
out of focus, the thing at the stove keeps flipping pancakes. Banner: **YOU RANG MUM'S WORK / AND SHE PICKED UP**.)*
> **NARRATOR:** You don't open the door. You ring Mum's work instead.
> **MUM (phone):** Hello? Bug?
> **NARRATOR:** And Mum picks up.

## THE TOP COMMENT WAS RIGHT · 0:07
*(Close: the phone at your ear. Hospital sounds behind her voice: a beeping monitor, a pager.)*
> **MUM (phone):** I'm still at work, love. What's wrong?
> **NARRATOR:** Most of you said: neither. You were right. Mum's at the hospital.
> **BUG (thought):** Then who's at the stove… and who's at the door?

## THE HOUSE GOES QUIET · 0:17
*(Wide: the kitchen. The pan sizzles. The thing at the stove has stopped moving, its back to you. The knocking stops.)*
> **NARRATOR:** The knocking stops. The thing at the stove stops moving.
> **MUM (phone, urgent, low):** Bug. Don't say anything. Walk to the back door. Now.

## IT HEARD YOU · 0:25
*(A slow push in: its head turns, too far, smiling.)*
> **THE STOVE:** Who are you talking to, Bug?
*(A hard cut to the front door, close.)*
> **THE DOOR (muffled):** Who are you talking to, Bug?
> **NARRATOR:** The voice at the door says it too. At the exact same time.

## THE TEXT · 0:36
*(Close: Mum's phone on the counter, next to the stove. It lights up on its own. Nobody's touching it.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Mum's phone lights up on the counter. One new text.
> **TEXT (on screen):** Hang up, Bug.
> **MUM (phone):** Don't you dare hang up! Bug, run!

## THE CHOICE · 0:45
*(Frozen: you in the middle of the kitchen, the phone at your ear, the thing at the stove turned toward you, the
front door at the end of the hall. Panels pop in.)*
> **NARRATOR:** The text says hang up. Mum's voice says don't. Who do you trust?

*(**A · HANG UP** / **B · STAY ON THE LINE**. Cut to black within half a second.)*

## Clarity checklist
| The viewer must understand | Told by |
|---|---|
| what you did, in line 1 | "You don't open the door. You ring Mum's work instead." |
| the twist | "And Mum picks up." + "I'm still at work." |
| what that means | "Mum's at the hospital." + "Then who's at the stove… and who's at the door?" |
| the danger | "Walk to the back door. Now." / "Who are you talking to, Bug?" |
| it's one thing, in two places | "The voice at the door says it too. At the exact same time." |
| the phone mystery continues | "Mum's phone lights up on the counter… Nobody's touching it." + "Hang up, Bug." |
| the choice | "The text says hang up. Mum's voice says don't. Who do you trust?" |
