# What If… You Could Stop Time? · Part 1: The Knock: viewer script

What the viewer sees and hears, in order. Nothing else reaches them.

**1.** _frozen: a glowing bullet an inch from a girl's face; the rain hangs in the air; blue_
> **NARRATOR:** Imagine you could stop time.
> **NARRATOR:** Every second costs you a day of your life.

**2.** _wide: the frozen street, the guard aiming, the girl; Theo walks through it in full colour_
> **NARRATOR:** A year ago, I didn't care how many days I had left.

**3.** _looking up: the gold Towers above the brown smog_
> **NARRATOR:** In Vantage City, the rich live in the Towers, above the smog.

**4.** _street level: crowds in the rain under the neon_
> **NARRATOR:** The rest of us live under it, in the Lowline.

**5.** _the tiny flat, evening: Mum in her red medic coat by the door, Theo at the table_
> **NARRATOR:** It was just me and my mum. She was a medic on the night shift.

**6.** _Mum, close: warm, firm_
> **MUM:** Eat something. And go to school tomorrow. I mean it.

**7.** _Theo, close, smiling_
> **THEO:** You always say that.

**8.** _she waves and goes; the door clicks shut_
> **MUM:** And I'll be back by sunrise. I always am.

**9.** _grey dawn: Theo asleep at the table, waiting; a knock; he wakes up grinning_
> **NARRATOR:** At sunrise, someone knocked.
> **THEO:** You forgot your key again…

**10.** _the door opens: a police officer, his cap in his hands_
> **NARRATOR:** It wasn't her.

**11.** _the officer, over Theo's shoulder: gentle, slow_
> **OFFICER:** I'm sorry, son. There was a street fight. Your mum stopped to help someone.

**12.** _Theo's face: the smile gone_

**13.** _the officer: the worst of it_
> **OFFICER:** The hospital wouldn't treat her without a coverage card. She didn't make it.

**14.** _Theo slid down to the floor by the door; the officer in the doorway, looking down; only the rain_
> **NARRATOR:** Nobody in the Lowline has a coverage card. She spent her life saving people the hospitals turned away.
> **NARRATOR:** And they turned her away.

**15.** _her red coat, still on its hook_
> **NARRATOR:** After that, nothing mattered.

**16.** _Theo on the bed in the daylight, not moving; the plate on the table untouched_
> **NARRATOR:** I stopped eating. I stopped answering anyone.

**17.** _the classroom: Theo stands up from his desk and walks to the door_
> **NARRATOR:** My teacher saw me walking out.

**18.** _his teacher at the board, calling after him_
> **TEACHER:** Theo! If you walk out now, don't come back.

**19.** _his empty desk_
> **NARRATOR:** I didn't.

**20.** _Mum's clinic locker room: Theo at her open locker_
> **NARRATOR:** In her locker, I found something she was hiding.

**21.** _a photo of the two of them, smiling_

**22.** _inside: a black case marked MILITARY, a chip_
> **NARRATOR:** A stolen military implant. Someone she'd patched up had paid her with it.

**23.** _her note: Theo. Never use this._
> **NARRATOR:** And a note.
> **tsMum (voice-over):** Theo. Never use this.

**24.** _the back-alley clinic: Theo in the chair, the doc beside him_
> **NARRATOR:** I didn't care what it did. A back-alley doc put it in for me.

**25.** _the implant goes in, sparks_

**26.** _the number lights up on his forearm: 21,915_
> **NARRATOR:** Twenty-one thousand days. Sixty years.

**27.** _the doc, close: the rule; the two of them as he warns him; close again: one day of your life_
> **DOC:** Squeeze your fist, and time stops. But every second you hold it, that number drops by one. That's one day of your life, kid.

**28.** _Theo, close: empty_
> **NARRATOR:** It felt like too many anyway.

**29.** _the street at night: Theo wandering; a girl with pink hair runs past, a Tower guard after her_
> **NARRATOR:** That night, I saw a girl running from Tower security.

**30.** _the guard aims at her and fires_
> **GUARD:** Stop!

**31.** _Theo's hand closes into a fist_
> **NARRATOR:** I didn't think. I squeezed.

**32.** _the frozen frame: the bullet an inch from her face, Theo in colour; WHAT SHOULD HE DO? A SAVE HER / B LEAVE HER, and he looks from one to the other, then at us_
