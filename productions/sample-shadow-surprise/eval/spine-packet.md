# Spine evaluation packet (phase 1): The First Surprise

format (as claimed by the writer): world-rule + emotional · factual: no (fiction)

## Premise
- protagonist: Milo
- want: to give Grandpa Gus the first real surprise of his life, on his 80th birthday
- loses: if it fails, Gus's eightieth is one more polite, fake gasp, and his secret wish stays a wish
- obstacle: in any light, Milo's shadow acts out the party; it's the school holidays and Gus minds him all day
- rule: In light, your shadow acts out what you truly want. In the dark, nobody has a shadow. When you're completely absorbed in what you're doing, your shadow just copies you.

## Rules of the situation
- In light, your shadow acts out what you truly want. It can't speak, only move.
- In the dark, nobody has a shadow, so in the dark nobody can tell what you want (or that you're lying).
- When you're completely absorbed in what you're doing, your shadow simply copies you.
- It's the school holidays: Gus looks after Milo all day, at home.
- Surprise parties fail the same way every time: the birthday person opens the door, the hall light falls on the waiting guests, and their shadows leap up first.
