# Episode 4: 1,214 Nights
**Runtime:** about 112 s, deliberately over the 105 s target.

**Purpose:**
- Show the scale of the ritual, that Mum taught Ollie, and the rule that the child's words decide how it ends.
- **Reveal Kuba survived, and that letting go saved him.**
- Put all three on the roof, then hand the audience the vote.

**Why it runs long:**

| Block | Length |
|---|---|
| The archive | 39 s |
| The 2008 truth | 21 s |
| The roof | 20 s |
| The vote | 7 s |

These are the two biggest reveals in the series, and cutting them under 105 s would rush both.

**Looks:**
- **Nursery-cam footage (NC):** grey-green infrared, fisheye, "NURSERY CAM 03:00:0x", clip number top right.
- **Garden-cam footage (GC):** blue-grey monochrome, wide, rain streaks, "GARDEN 03:0x".
- **2008 (F08):** full sequences this time, still cold teal, slowed, muffled.

**Captions:** Ava's voice-over reads Ewa's message.

## Script
**INT. AVA'S BEDROOM. DAY.**
A wall of tiny grey night-vision thumbnails, every one stamped 03:00, scrolling and scrolling. A counter: 1,214 CLIPS.
AVA's face, lit by them.

**AVA** *(whisper)*: Every night.

*Title: WHAT OLLIE SAYS AT 3 A.M. · PART 4*

**INT. KITCHEN. MORNING.** *(Caption: THIS MORNING)*

**AVA:** Dad. Mum sleepwalks. She took Ollie to the river.

MARK turns to KATE. She looks small and grey.

**KATE:** It used to happen. After the flood.

**MARK** *(gently)*: The nursery cam saves every sound. I'm off tonight. I'll go through it with you.

Ava's eyes go to the airing cupboard.

**INT. AVA'S BEDROOM. LATER.**
A small black hub box from the airing cupboard, plugged into Dad's laptop. The grid loads. She scrolls to the very first clip: 14.07.2023 · 03:00.

**ON THE LAPTOP. NURSERY CAM, CLIP 1.**
The old house. Blue walls. A cot-bed. OLLIE, three, in rocket pyjamas. Kate kneels and whispers slowly.

**KATE [PL provisional L1]:** *Mamo… woda… jest przy drzwiach.*

**OLLIE (3)** *(sleepy, echoing each word)*: *Mamo… woda… jest przy drzwiach.*

**AVA:** She taught him.

**CLIP 388.**
Kate leaves the room. Ollie, four, climbs out of bed, walks right up to the camera and fills the lens.

**OLLIE (4):** Daddy… Mama did the water game again.

He waits. Nobody answers. He goes back to bed. Ava's hand goes to her mouth.

**CLIP 412.**
The moment for his last line. Ollie, four, says something else.

**OLLIE (4):** Mama… don't be sad.

Kate stops. Her sleeping face crumples. She lies down on the floor beside his bed and sleeps there until the morning light creeps in.

**CLIP 903.**
Ollie's bed is empty, a teddy on the pillow. Kate kneels at the empty bed, holding nothing. The timestamp races: 03:10, 03:30, 03:52. Still kneeling. She rises and wanders out.

**KATE** *(whisper) [PL provisional P7]:* *Kubuś?*

**GARDEN CAM. OCTOBER.**
Kate, alone, walks into the river up to her knees, calling.

**KATE [PL provisional P7]:** *Kubuś!*

A small figure in dinosaur pyjamas comes running down the lawn. She takes his hand.

**AVA:** That's why he goes.

Night after night of November on the garden cam: the water climbing the lawn, the two figures meeting it closer each time. A hand opens. Another. Another. 1,214.
Ava shuts the laptop. Silence.

**INT. HALLWAY. EVENING.**
Mark, in hi-vis, on his phone.

**MARK:** Whole plant's called in, the river's over the bank.

**KATE** *(quietly)*: Lock me in.

He looks at her. Then he locks her bedroom door from the outside and presses the key into Ava's palm.

**MARK:** Ollie sleeps with you. Don't open it till I'm back. Six o'clock.

He hugs her and goes.

**INT. AVA'S BEDROOM. NIGHT.**
Ollie under her duvet. Ava holds up her little finger.

**AVA:** No water game tonight. If Mama calls, you wait for me. Promise?

**OLLIE** *(hooking his little finger in hers)*: Promise.

23:04. Her phone lights up: *Wierzbno — Pamiętamy.* A long message. She reads the translation.

**AVA (V.O.):** Jakub was the boy my husband took from his mother's arms.

*2008. Dawn, rain. A small boat noses up to a half-drowned roof. Two old people in blankets in it. A man in a firefighter's helmet loops a rope round the chimney.*

**KASIA (2008) [PL provisional P8]:** *Tomasz!*

*She lowers a toddler (dark curls, red pyjamas, one blue sock) into the man's arms.*

**KASIA [PL provisional L4]:** *Weź go!*

**KUBA (2008)** *(screaming) [PL provisional L5]:* *Obiecałaś, że nie puścisz!*

*The boat jerks. The rope whips tight. It tears through her palm.*

**AVA (V.O.):** When the boat turned over, Tomasz tied him into a willow and went back for the others.

*The boat spins away to a line of willows and flips. Tomasz, in a willow fork, buckles the toddler to the trunk with his belt. He lets go of the branch. The water takes him.*

**AVA (V.O.):** Jakub was found that afternoon. He's twenty-one. He thinks his mother drowned.

*Afternoon. A helicopter's light finds the willow. The toddler, alive. One blue sock.*

**AVA (V.O.):** Who are you? — Ewa Kowal.

Ava, crying.

**AVA:** She let go… and he lived.

She types Ewa's words into the translator as if Kuba were speaking: *Mama, Tomasz is holding me. He tied me to the willow with his belt. I'm alive.* The Polish appears. She whispers the first line, testing each sound.

**AVA** *(whisper) [PL provisional A1]:* *Mamo… Tomasz mnie trzyma. Żyję.*

2:59. Ollie sits bolt upright in her bed.

**OLLIE:** It's time.

**AVA** *(catching his hand)*: Not tonight.

From across the landing, a window latch. The rain roars louder.

**KATE (O.S.) [PL provisional P7]:** *Kubuś? … KUBUŚ!*

Ava unlocks her mother's door. The room is empty. The curtains are blowing. The window is wide open onto the flat roof.

**EXT. FLAT ROOF. NIGHT. CONTINUOUS.**
Kate stands on the low brick edge of the roof, soaked, calling into the flooded garden.

**KATE [PL provisional P7]:** *Kubuś!*

Ollie ducks under Ava's arm and is out of the window before she can grab him.

**OLLIE [PL provisional L1]:** *Mamo, woda jest przy drzwiach!*

Kate turns and grips his wrist with her scarred hand.

**KATE [PL provisional L2]:** *Kubuś, trzymaj mnie za rękę.*

She kneels at the edge with him.

**KATE** *(whisper) [PL provisional L3]:* *Nie puszczę cię. Obiecuję.*

She rises and lifts his arm out over the edge, toward the black water.

**KATE [PL provisional L4]:** *Weź go!*

Ollie's bare toes on the wet brick. He looks back at his sister, and keeps his promise. He says nothing.
Kate waits, holding him there, in the rain. Ava has one leg over the sill.

The rain slows almost to stillness.
