## Reusable changes in this checkpoint
- **`crowd.forBeat`:** a crowd can be staged for a later camera beat. Shot 14's crowd is staged for the rise at the
  end; shot 7's for the captain's point of view.
- **Validator** (made more accurate, not weaker):
  - The face-hidden and foreground checks now use the subjects of the camera beat on screen, not the whole shot. The
    narrower check **caught two new problems**: Juno blocking Milo's whip-pan beat, and a pirate filling a close
    vote shot.
  - They skip frames while a glide or whip is still travelling, as head framing already did.
