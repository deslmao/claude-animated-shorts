# The First Surprise
spec: 2
format: world-rule+emotional
why: One visual rule (your shadow acts out what you truly want) makes secrets impossible, which gives a loving, concrete goal (Gus's first real surprise) and an obstacle the protagonist carries everywhere (his own shadow); the solution uses two rules shown earlier, and the payoff is an image, not a speech.
length: 60
voice: george
voices: milo=liam, gus=brian
mood: warm
factual: no

## Premise
- question: How do you surprise someone in a world where your shadow gives away everything you want?
- protagonist: Milo
- want: to give Grandpa Gus the first real surprise of his life, on his 80th birthday
- fear: his own shadow ruining it, as everyone's always has
- protects: Gus's secret wish: Milo has seen Gus's shadow clap for someone else's surprise
- decision: to hide the guests in the dark, and keep his own shadow quiet by getting completely absorbed in a domino run with Gus
- loses: if it fails, Gus's eightieth is one more polite, fake gasp, and his secret wish stays a wish
- obstacle: in any light, Milo's shadow acts out the party; it's the school holidays and Gus minds him all day
- rule: In light, your shadow acts out what you truly want. In the dark, nobody has a shadow. When you're completely absorbed in what you're doing, your shadow just copies you.
- conflict: none
- action: none

## Spine
depth: deep

### Constraints
- In light, your shadow acts out what you truly want. It can't speak, only move.
- In the dark, nobody has a shadow, so in the dark nobody can tell what you want (or that you're lying).
- When you're completely absorbed in what you're doing, your shadow simply copies you.
- It's the school holidays: Gus looks after Milo all day, at home.
- Surprise parties fail the same way every time: the birthday person opens the door, the hall light falls on the waiting guests, and their shadows leap up first.

### Alternatives
| # | central conflict | key decision | solution | ending |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Milo against his own shadow: he must hide a surprise party from the grandfather who minds him all day | hide the guests in the dark, and silence his own shadow by getting lost in a domino run with Gus | the run ends by switching on the room's lamp from inside, so the light and the guests arrive at the same instant | Gus is genuinely surprised for the first time; his shadow leaps higher than anyone's |
| 2 | a cheat at the town talent contest who hides his true intentions by performing in the dark | Milo floods the stage with light mid-act | the cheat's shadow exposes the trick to the whole hall | justice: the honest act wins |
| 3 | Milo pretends to love the piano lessons Gus pays for, while his shadow keeps walking away from the piano | Milo tells Gus the truth instead of hiding it | Gus's own shadow admits he hated piano as a boy too | honesty: they quit together and go fishing |
- chosen: 1. The want is a gift of love, the obstacle is something Milo carries everywhere, the solution beats the exact way surprise parties always fail, and the ending is one image anyone understands without words.

## Writer notes
The spine was written first and revised once after the phase-1 evaluation (below). The lines and storyboard were written from the revised spine. Open doubt: whether the opening world gags (beats 1–2) are short enough before Milo appears in beat 3.

## Revision
**One revision, at spine level, after the phase-1 evaluation:**
- **The E6 hole is closed:** the blind drops before Gus looks, and in the dark Milo can say "Sun's in my eyes".
- **The rules are consistent:**
  - "a moment before you do" is dropped;
  - parties now fail because the door light hits the guests, and the plan beats exactly that with a light from inside;
  - Milo's shadow turns when he's distracted at E10, and he fixes it within reach;
  - a falling book presses a floor lamp's foot switch, instead of a domino flipping a wall switch.
- **The wish is shown:** Gus's shadow claps at the TV (E3), and Milo sees it. That gives Milo his reason, and the ending pays off a wish.
- **The obstacle is explained:** it's the holidays and Gus minds Milo all day.
- **Milo appears by beat 3.**
