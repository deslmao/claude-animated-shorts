# Episode 5, Finale A: Answer as Kuba
**Runtime:** about 88 s. Produced only if **A** wins the vote.

**Purpose:**
- End the ritual from inside the dream, using what only the rescue story contains.
- Kate is freed without ever knowing.
- **The cost:** Ava keeps the secret, and Kuba never gets his mother back.

**Final image:** 3:00, silence, three knocks answered, Ava awake. The only lit window in the house is hers.

**Why it can work:** see `../story-lock.md` §1.2.
- The first, generic line is rejected.
- The second starts where Kate's memory stops (the willows) and carries on past it (the belt), and the dream ends differently.

## Script
**EXT. FLAT ROOF. NIGHT.**
Rain. KATE stands on the low brick edge of the roof, holding OLLIE's arm out over the black, flooded garden, waiting.
AVA climbs out of the window onto the wet felt on her hands and knees.

*Title: WHAT OLLIE SAYS AT 3 A.M. · PART 5: ANSWER AS KUBA*

She reaches them and grabs a fistful of the back of Ollie's pyjama top. Ollie looks at her. She gives him the smallest nod: *wait.*
Kate stares into the dark, waiting for words.
Ava kneels at Ollie's height and speaks up into her mother's face.

**AVA [PL provisional A1]:** *Mamo… Tomasz mnie trzyma. Żyję.*
*Subtitle:* Mama… Tomasz is holding me. I'm alive.

Nothing. Kate's grip tightens. She lifts Ollie's arm higher.

**KATE [PL provisional L4]:** *Weź go!*
*Subtitle:* Take him!

Ollie's heel slips on the wet brick. Ava's fist holds.
Ava steadies her voice. She starts where her mother's memory stops.

**AVA [PL provisional A2]:** *Przywiązał mnie do wierzby. Paskiem. Żyję, mamo.*
*Subtitle:* He tied me to the willow. With his belt. I'm alive, Mama.

Kate's unseeing eyes fill.

*FLASH (2008): a willow. A wide belt buckling round a toddler, holding him safe against the trunk.*

Kate's arm comes down, slowly. She pulls Ollie back from the edge, into her chest, and sinks down onto the wet roof, rocking him.

**KATE** *(whisper) [PL provisional A3]:* *Żyjesz…*
*Subtitle:* You're alive…

Ava wraps her arms round both of them.

**KATE [PL provisional A4]:** *Dobranoc, Kubusiu.*
*Subtitle:* Goodnight, Kuba.

She doesn't say she's sorry.

**INT. KATE'S BEDROOM. NIGHT. LATER.**
Ava guides them in through the window. Kate lies down and, for the first time, closes her eyes. Slow breaths. Ollie curls up against her.
Ava pulls the duvet over them both, then sits on the floor with her back to the bed, soaked, watching the window turn grey.

6:02. MARK bursts in, hi-vis dripping.

**MARK:** Ava?

**AVA:** She sleepwalked. I got her back to bed.

He kneels and holds her.

**MONTAGE. DAYS LATER.**
On Ava's phone, the nursery-cam app: *03:00: no new clips.* Saturday. Sunday. Monday. Tuesday. The counter stays at 1,214.

**INT. KITCHEN. MORNING.**
Through the window, a FOR SALE board in the front garden. Kate, humming, flips pancakes.

**KATE:** I had the strangest dream. A man in a tree… waving.

She smiles: a real one. Ava has never seen it before.

**AVA:** Sounds nice.

**INT. AVA'S BEDROOM. NIGHT.**
Her phone: Ewa's message. *Who are you?*
She types: *His sister.* She deletes it. She types: *I can't tell you. I'm sorry.*
She looks at the wall: Ollie on the other side, her mother down the landing. She sends it. *Delivered.*
She turns the phone face-down.

2:59. 3:00.
Silence. No voice through the wall.
Ava knocks three times. Three small knocks come back.
She lies on her side, eyes open, watching the wall.

**EXT. 4 FERRY LANE. NIGHT.**
The house is dark. The river is quiet. One window is lit: Ava's.

*End card: WHAT OLLIE SAYS AT 3 A.M. · END*
