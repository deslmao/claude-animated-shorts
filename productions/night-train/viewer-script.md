# The Man on the Last Train Has No Reflection.: viewer script

What the viewer sees and hears, in order. Nothing else reaches them.

**1.** _frame 0: the banner; a man in a soaked torn coat lunging at you, screaming; the camera shakes; the lights stutter_
> **ntManScream (voice-over):** [screaming] WHY DID THIS HAPPEN TO ME?! WHY ME?!

**2.** _you flinch back in your seat; the two of you across the bay; over your shoulder at him_
> **NARRATOR:** Imagine you're on the last train home, and the man in front of you won't stop screaming.

**3.** _the LED display over the end door: 21:47; over your shoulder: the man on the seats facing yours; his face: staring, unblinking_
> **NARRATOR:** It's 9:47. He's on the three seats in front of you. Facing you. Staring right at you.

**4.** _his face, shouting; his fists clenched on his knees_
> **ntManScream (voice-over):** [shouting] I was just going home! Just like you!

**5.** _him, full body, still; the camera zooms into his coat: water dripping off the hem into a puddle; then the close-up of the drips_
> **NARRATOR:** His coat is soaking wet. It hasn't rained all week.

**6.** _you turn your head to the dark window_
> **NARRATOR:** You look at the window.

**7.** _the window, clearly: the bay in the glass, you on your seat, his seat EMPTY; a red ring round it_
> **NARRATOR:** You can see yourself. You can see the empty seats.

**8.** _REAL above: you and him facing you; IN THE WINDOW below: you and an empty seat_
> **NARRATOR:** But where he's sitting… there's nobody there.

**9.** _his face, pushing in: no blink, no expression_
> **NARRATOR:** He has no reflection.

**10.** _the carriage, the lights stuttering; your face, eyes darting_
> **NARRATOR:** You think to yourself…
> **ntThought (voice-over):** Don't look at him. Just get up. Walk.

**11.** _you walk away fast; the end door slides open; you go through; it slides shut_

**12.** _the next carriage: behind you the end door slides open again: he's standing in the gangway_
> **NARRATOR:** Behind you, the door slides open again. He's following you.

**13.** _the carriage you're in: empty, swaying_
> **NARRATOR:** And then you notice: this carriage is empty.

**14.** _the next carriage, end to end: empty seats, the lights stuttering, nobody_
> **NARRATOR:** So is the next one.

**15.** _over your shoulder: him, standing at the far end_
> **NARRATOR:** It's just you… and him.

**16.** _you run straight at us; side on past the bays; your feet pounding toward the last door; the lights strobing_
> **NARRATOR:** You run. As fast as you can. You don't look back.

**17.** _your eyes: the last door in front of you, the view heaving as you gasp for air; then silence but the rails_
> **NARRATOR:** You gasp for air. You think to yourself…
> **ntThought (voice-over):** I… I lost him. I must have lost him.

**18.** _the jumpscare: you turn round (a whip) and his face fills the frame; the smile spreads; he whispers_
> **ntManWhisper (voice-over):** This is your stop.

**19.** _the LED display: NEXT STOP ASHFIELD; it glitches; DEATH CITY, in red_
> **ntAnnouncer (voice-over):** The next stop is… Death City.

**20.** _the line map: every stop you know, and DEATH CITY between them, ringed_
> **NARRATOR:** You know the whole area. That doesn't exist.

**21.** _the brakes; the red exit doors hiss open onto fog and a red DEATH CITY sign_
> **NARRATOR:** The doors open.

**22.** _the last frame: you at the open doors, the fog and the sign; him right behind you, smiling; A · GET OFF into Death City / B · STAY ON with him; cut half a second after the last word_
> **NARRATOR:** Do you get off to escape him… into Death City, a place that never existed?
> **NARRATOR:** Or stay on… with the man who has no reflection?
