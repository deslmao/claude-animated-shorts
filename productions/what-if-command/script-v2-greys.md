# What If… your voice could free a city, but it cost you everyone? (draft v2 for the owner's approval)

**Series:** "What If…" (Thinkora). Emotional dystopia, about 75 s.

**Premise (one sentence):** in a city where the government brands the lowest people "Greys" and takes their names, you
discover you can command anyone, but every person you command forgets you ever existed.

**The rule, said in words at once:** say "I command you to…", and they obey. Then they forget you. Forever.

**Originality:** our own world, label, power and cost. It shares nothing with existing shows: no "Elevens", no masked
rebel, no empire, no eye symbol. The emotional engine is the cost, which is ours.

**Narrator:** the channel's storyteller, second person, quiet. You're Nova, 15. Your little sister is Pip, 8.

---

**0:00** *(Night. A curfew siren. A guard in black grabs a little girl in a grey wristband. You step in front of him.
Your voice drops.)*
> **NOVA:** I command you to let her go.
> *(He lets go. His eyes go empty. He walks away.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Imagine your voice could make anyone obey. But every person you command forgets you ever existed.

**THE CITY** *(Grey wristbands, queues for bread, screens with the Chancellor smiling.)*
> **NARRATOR:** In Ward Four, the government takes your name and gives you a grey band. They call you a Grey. Greys
> don't vote. Greys don't leave. Greys disappear.

*(In bed, in the dark. Pip holds your hand.)*
> **PIP:** Promise you'll never forget me.
> **NARRATOR:** Your little sister, Pip. She's the reason you do this.

**DAY 1–30** *(Montage. Each command lands a low tone, then silence.)*
> **NOVA:** I command you to open the bread store.
> **NARRATOR:** A hundred families eat. The guard forgets you.
> **NOVA:** I command you to tear up the list.
> **NARRATOR:** Forty Greys aren't taken. Your best friend saw you do it. He forgets you too.
> **NARRATOR:** And Mum. One night she asks who you are. Politely. In her own kitchen.

**THE BROADCAST** *(The Chancellor's tower. A live broadcast to every screen in the city. You at the microphone.)*
> **NARRATOR:** One command could end it all. But everyone who hears your voice will forget you. Everyone.
> **NOVA:** I command you… to be free.

*(Every screen. Every street. Grey bands fall. The gates open. People step into the daylight.)*

**THE ENDING** *(Morning. Pip, in a bright yellow coat, runs past you in the crowd. She stops. She looks at you, a
stranger.)*
> **PIP:** Sorry. Do I know you?
> **NARRATOR:** You saved everyone. And no one will ever know your name.

*(Silent end card: WOULD YOU DO IT? 1 = YES · 2 = NO 👇)*

## Clarity checklist
| The viewer must understand | Told by |
|---|---|
| the first frame (the power) | "I command you to let her go." + "your voice could make anyone obey" |
| the cost | "every person you command forgets you ever existed" |
| the world | "the government takes your name… They call you a Grey" |
| why it's urgent | "Greys disappear." |
| who Pip is, and the plant | "Your little sister, Pip." + "Promise you'll never forget me." |
| the cost growing | "He forgets you too." / "she asks who you are" |
| the final choice | "everyone who hears your voice will forget you. Everyone." |
| the payoff (the inverted promise) | "Sorry. Do I know you?" |

## Production notes
- **New sets:**
  - a grey dystopian street (curfew lights, screens, bread queue);
  - the Chancellor's broadcast room;
  - the daylight "free" street.
- **Reused:** the home, bedroom and kitchen.
- **The look:** the city is desaturated, and colour floods in only when the city is freed. That's a strong visual
  arc and an emotional cue.
- **Sound:** a command tone, then silence; sirens; the crowd; the swell at the broadcast, then a single piano for
  the ending (ElevenLabs effects and a custom score).
- **Voices:**
  - the narrator;
  - Nova (her own voice, distinct from the narrator);
  - Pip;
  - the Chancellor (screens);
  - Mum (one line).
