## A2. Section A verification (17 Nov 2026, without a native speaker)
**Method:**
- **A line-by-line grammatical analysis:** spelling and diacritics, case and verb forms, agreement, punctuation, register, and the accuracy of each subtitle.
- **Published references for the forms that could be wrong:**
  - the future tense of *puścić* (*puszczę*, *puścisz*): [Wiktionary, *puścić*](https://en.wiktionary.com/wiki/pu%C5%9Bci%C4%87);
  - the locative *przy drzwiach*: [Wiktionary, *drzwi*](https://en.wiktionary.com/wiki/drzwi);
  - the vocative of pet names: [NCK "Ojczysty — dodaj do ulubionych": *wołacz*](https://nck.pl/projekty-kulturalne/projekty/ojczysty-dodaj-do-ulubionych/ciekawostki-jezykowe/wolacz.ajax) and [Poradnik Językowy, keyword *wołacz*](https://poradnik-jezykowy.uw.edu.pl/keyword/1746);

**Notes (uncertainty, nothing changed):**
1. **The address form *Kubuś* (L2).** The formally correct vocative of the pet name is *Kubusiu*, but using the nominative as the vocative is normal in colloquial spoken Polish, according to the NCK and Poradnik Językowy sources above.
   - Both forms are natural from a mother; *Kubusiu* is slightly softer and more careful.
   - The locked line keeps *Kubuś*. Switching to *Kubusiu* is an option only with the owner's approval.
   - The same applies to *Kubuś?* (P7, Part 2).
2. **Plausibility of L5 for a three-year-old.** It is a full past-tense clause plus a subordinate clause.
   - Polish children use past-tense forms by about 3–4, so it is within range, if at the articulate end.
   - In the story it is the role's line, said every night, not spontaneous speech.
   - A slightly more natural child version would add the object: *Obiecałaś, że mnie nie puścisz.* It is not adopted; the lock is unchanged unless the owner approves.

**Remaining limitations:**
- **No native speaker has read or heard any line.** Errors of tone or idiom that a native ear would catch may remain; the grammar and spelling are verified.
- **Pronunciation is untested until the audition.** Mitigation at the audition:
  - each Polish take is transcribed back with the voice service's speech-to-text, where available through the proxy, and must return the same words;
  - takes are checked by ear for Polish penultimate stress (*o-bie-CA-łaś*, *prze-PRA-szam*) and for clear *ś*, *ę* and *rz*.

  A take that fails either check isn't used.
- **The automated grammar check could not be run.** If api.languagetool.org is allowed in the environment's network settings, it can be run in a minute and recorded here.
