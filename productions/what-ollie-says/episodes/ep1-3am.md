### SCENE 1 · 0:00–0:07 · INT. OLLIE'S BEDROOM, 03:00, WED 11 NOV
*Seen through Ava's phone. The camera screen fills the frame: a red **REC** dot and **03:00** in the corner.*

There's no build-up. In the first frame OLLIE (6) is already sitting bolt upright in bed in his dinosaur pyjamas, eyes shut, lit by the green dot of his nightlight.

His lips move. The voice is calm, high and too small: a three-year-old's.

**OLLIE** *(toddler voice)*: *Mamo, woda jest przy drzwiach.*
*Subtitle:* Mama, the water's at the door.

**AVA** *(behind the phone, a breath)*: …Ollie?

Eyes still shut, he turns his face to the camera. Then he drops back onto the pillow as if his strings were cut, asleep.

*On-screen text, typed like a post:* **He's six. He's never heard Polish. That isn't his voice.**

*Hard cut.*

> *Staging:*
> - Two shots: a medium shot, then a close-up with Ollie facing the camera.
> - Ollie sits up in bed (`bed` spot, mode sit) with eyes-closed expression `sleepy`, then is lowered to the `asleep` spot.
> - The phone-camera overlay is pinned to the lens: REC, 03:00, and the post text from 0:04.

### SCENE 2 · 0:07–0:21 · INT. KITCHEN, MORNING, WED 11 NOV
Rain on the window. Ollie bites his toast into a dinosaur shape and walks it across the table, roaring quietly. KATE (38), in her oatmeal cardigan, stands at the counter with a mug.

AVA (15), in her school jumper with her hood up, slides her phone across the counter and taps play. The tiny voice comes out of the speaker. The translator banner drops down: **POLISH DETECTED**.

**AVA:** Okay. Explain that.

Kate looks at the screen. Her face doesn't move, but the colour goes out of it. Her right hand tightens on the mug, showing a pale scar diagonally across the palm *(insert, 1 s)*.

**KATE** *(quietly)*: Where did he hear that?

**AVA:** You tell me.

**KATE:** I haven't spoken it in eighteen years.

**OLLIE** *(mouth full)*: Mummy, what's Polish?

Kate reaches over and gently turns the phone face down, as if closing a door.

**KATE:** Eat your toast, Ollie.

She turns back to the window and watches the rain.

> *Staging:*
> - Set `ferryKitchen`: Kate at `counter`, Ollie at `tableL`, Ava at `tableR`.
> - Inserts: the translator (`trans`, step 0: line 1 only), then the scarred palm (`palm`), then the phone turned face down.

### SCENE 3 · 0:21–0:29 · INT. KATE'S BEDROOM, EVENING, WED 11 NOV
Ava passes the half-open door, then stops.

Kate sits on the edge of her bed. A biscuit tin with a dinosaur sticker on the lid lies open beside her. She is pressing a tea towel to a photograph, over and over, and crying without a sound.

A floorboard creaks. Kate slides the tin under the bed and wipes her face with the back of her hand.

**KATE** *(not looking up)*: Go to bed, Ava.

**AVA:** It's half eight.

**KATE:** Please.

Ava backs away.

> *Staging:*
> - Kate and Mark's bedroom in `ferryBack` (the room behind the window, lit by the bedside lamp). Kate sits on the bed's edge. Ava's reaction is a close-up at the room's edge.
> - Inserts: the tea towel pressed to the photo; the tin pushed under the bed.

### SCENE 4 · 0:29–0:41 · INT. KATE'S BEDROOM, MINUTES LATER
From downstairs come the kettle and Mum and Dad's voices.

Ava slips in, kneels and slides the tin out from under the bed. She runs her thumb over the dinosaur sticker, one of Ollie's.

She lifts the lid. Inside, the tissue paper is bone dry and crackles. On top lies a photo of a curly-haired toddler laughing on a swing.

The photo is **wet**. Water beads on its surface, and one drop runs off the corner onto her fingers. Ava stares at the dry tin, then at the wet photo Mum has just dried.

She turns it over. Faded ink: ***Kubuś · 2008***.

Ollie's school photo stands in a frame on Mum's bedside table. Ava holds the toddler's photo up beside it. **Match cut:** the toddler's face slides over Ollie's. The curls line up, and so do the eyes. A drop runs down the toddler's cheek like a tear.

**AVA** *(whisper)*: …what?

> *Staging:* inserts carry the scene:
> - the tin lid with the sticker;
> - the open tin, dry, with the wet photo and a running drop;
> - the photo's back;
> - the match: side by side, then overlaid, then the tear drop.
>
> Ava's close-up for "…what?".

### SCENE 5 · 0:41–0:48 · INT. KITCHEN, NIGHT, WED 11 NOV
MARK (42), in his hi-vis jacket, pats his pockets for his keys, a flask under his arm. Ava sits at the table with one hand in her hoodie pocket, over the photo.

**AVA:** Dad. Did Mum have a baby? Before us?

**MARK** *(a surprised laugh)*: What? No. *(He looks at her properly.)* Why, what's she said?

**AVA:** Nothing. Forget it.

He kisses the top of her head and heads for the hall.

**MARK:** River's high, but it never comes over on our side. Lock the back door, yeah?

The front door shuts off screen. Ava gets up and slides the back door's **top bolt** across.

> *Staging:*
> - `ferryKitchen`: Mark exits by the `stairs` spot, toward the hall. Ava goes to `door` and slides the bolt.
> - This plants the bolt Kate opens in Scene 8.

### SCENE 6 · 0:48–0:56 · INT. OLLIE'S BEDROOM, BEDTIME → 02:58, THU 12 NOV
Ava tucks Ollie in.

**AVA:** Shout if you need me.

**OLLIE:** I'm six. I'm not scared.

She stands. His hand shoots out and grabs her sleeve, and he doesn't let go. She sits back down.

*Dissolve:*

- She looks from the photo to his sleeping face and back.
- She reaches up and touches his shoulder.

**OLLIE** *(eyes shut, his own sleepy voice)*: Not tonight, Mama. I'm tired.

Ava goes still.

**AVA** *(barely)*: …Mama?

> *Staging:*

### SCENE 7 · 0:56–1:05 · INT. OLLIE'S BEDROOM, 03:00, THU 12 NOV
Her phone clock flips from **02:59** to **03:00**.

Ollie sits bolt upright, eyes shut: exactly the opening frame, now seen from the floor.

**OLLIE** *(toddler voice)*: *Mamo, woda jest przy drzwiach.*
*Subtitle:* Mama, the water's at the door.

The door swings open. Kate stands there in her long grey nightdress and cardigan, **eyes wide open, unblinking**.

**AVA:** Mum? He's doing it again—

Kate walks past her as if the room were empty. She kneels at the bed and takes Ollie's hand.

**KATE** *(flat, tender)*: *Kubuś, trzymaj mnie za rękę.*
*Subtitle:* Kuba, hold my hand.

Ollie climbs out of bed, eyes still shut. Ava grabs her mother's wrist.

**AVA:** Mum, stop. You're scaring me.

Kate doesn't even look. She keeps walking, and Ava's hand slides off her.

> *Staging:*
> - `ollieRoom`: Kate has `blink: false` and the `blank` face. She kneels at the `kneel` spot (the proven bedside staging); hands meet with `offer`.
> - Ava's grab is `offer` toward Kate, then Kate walks on.
> - Insert: the phone, 02:59 then 03:00.

### SCENE 8 · 1:05–1:15 · INT. KITCHEN → EXT. GARDEN, NIGHT
Ava follows them, filming. Her phone shakes.

- **The kitchen:** mother and son come in from the stairs hand in hand, his bare feet beside hers.
- **The back door:** without looking, Kate reaches up and slides back the **top bolt** Ava locked.
- **The garden** fills the screen with the roar of rain.

**HIGH WIDE (Ava's phone, from the back step):**
- The bottom half of their lawn is under **flat black water**.
- It stops dead at the fence, **standing against the panels like glass**.
- On the other side, under next door's security light, the lawn is **dry**: not a puddle anywhere.

**AVA** *(under her breath)*: That's not… that's not possible.

Down the path, Kate bends to Ollie as they walk toward the water, still holding his hand.

**KATE** *(a whisper)*: *Nie puszczę cię. Obiecuję.*
*Subtitle:* I won't let go of you. I promise.

> *Staging:*
> - `ferryKitchen` (enter from `stairs`, cross to `door`), then `ferryBack`.
> - The high wide is from the back step, with `rain` and the new `ferryWater` variant `garden`: the water only between our side fences, with a visible edge at each fence and a lit, dry neighbouring lawn.
> - The walk is seen from behind (`walk.view: "back"`).

### SCENE 9 · 1:15–1:30 · EXT. GARDEN, THE WATER'S EDGE, NIGHT
Ollie is ankle-deep. Kate stops where the dark water deepens and lifts his arm out over it, toward the blackness.

**KATE** *(a shout that cracks through the rain)*: *Weź go!*
*Subtitle:* Take him!

Ava flinches.

**OLLIE** *(toddler voice, small and broken)*: *Obiecałaś, że nie puścisz.*
*Subtitle:* You promised you wouldn't let go.

**KATE** *(softly)*: *Przepraszam.*
*Subtitle:* I'm sorry.

*Insert:* her hand opens, and his small fingers slide out of hers.

Kate turns and walks back up the lawn, calm, straight past her daughter.

**AVA:** MUM!

Nothing. Kate doesn't turn.

Then the water moves. V-shaped ripples run **against the current**, toward Ollie. His pyjama legs are tugged backward and he slides a step deeper. His eyes snap open, and now it's his own voice:

**OLLIE:** Ava?

He's dragged to his knees in the water.

**OLLIE:** AVA!

The phone hits the grass. *(Cut to the phone's view from the ground, rain falling across it.)* Ava sprints into the frame and splashes in. She gets him under the arms and heaves. The water pulls back and drags her forward a step.

She throws herself backward onto the lawn with him on top of her. The water lets go **with a hiss**, sinks back across the grass and goes flat and still.

They lie there gasping. Up at the house the back door bangs in the wind.

> *Staging:*
> - `ferryBack`, with `garden` water and `rain`.
> - The lifted arm is the `offer` gesture toward the water.
> - Inserts: the hand opening; the pull (V-ripples dragging the pyjama legs).
> - "Dragged to his knees": Ollie is lowered into the water (the kneel staging; the water hides the legs).
> - The phone's view is a low ground-level camera (above 0.05 m).
> - Cut on the heave. The next shot shows them down on the grass (Ava's `fall` pose), and the water's steps stop so it goes still.

### SCENE 10 · 1:30–1:42 · INT. KITCHEN, 03:21, THU 12 NOV
The microwave clock reads **03:21**. Ollie sits at the table wrapped in a towel, teeth chattering, with a puddle under his chair. Ava, soaked, puts the wet photo on the table.

**AVA:** Ollie. Who's this?

**OLLIE:** That's Kuba.

**AVA:** How do you know that?

**OLLIE:** Mummy showed me. In her tin. *(beat)* She cried.

Footsteps. Kate is in the doorway, rubbing her eyes. She **blinks**: she's awake.

**KATE:** Why are you two up?

Then she sees the towel, the puddle and the photo, and goes still.

**AVA** *(voice cracking)*: Mum. Who's Kuba?

A long silence. Rain.

**KATE** *(barely)*: He was my son.

Ollie looks up at her from inside the towel, completely matter-of-fact.

**OLLIE:** You call me Kuba. Every night.

Kate's eyes drop to her own bare feet: wet, with blades of grass stuck to them, and a trail of wet footprints behind her.

*Cut to black.* **PART 2: THE WATER GAME**

> *Staging:*
> - `ferryKitchen`: Ollie at `tableL` (sit) in the towel look; Ava standing at `tableR`; Kate enters at `stairs`. Kate blinks normally here.
> - Inserts: the wet photo on the table; Kate's wet feet; the end card.
