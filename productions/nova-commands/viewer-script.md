# What If… You Could Command Anyone?: viewer script

What the viewer sees and hears, in order. Nothing else reaches them.

**1.** _night, rain: the guard has Pip by the arm; Nova steps in_
> **NOVA:** Nova commands you… to let her go. At this instant!

**2.** _the guard's eyes go empty; he lets go and walks away_
> **NARRATOR:** That was the first time I used it. I didn't know yet what it would cost me.

**3.** _the wall across the street, the Blues' gold towers beyond; Nova small in the rain_
> **NARRATOR:** In my town, there are two kinds of people. The Blues… and us. The Greys.

**4.** _the Blues feasting in the gold hall_
> **NARRATOR:** The Blues have everything. Big warm houses. Food they throw away.

**5.** _the grey band: G-4471_
> **NARRATOR:** We have a grey band on our wrist, and a number instead of a name.

**6.** _Mum scrubbing the end of the table; the Blues don't look at her_
> **NARRATOR:** My mum cleans their tables twelve hours a day. She's not allowed to eat the crumbs.

**7.** _the bread queue; the shutter slams down in front of Pip_
> **NARRATOR:** Every morning, the bread runs out before it gets to us.

**8.** _Dad's photo on the wall_
> **NARRATOR:** Greys don't vote. Greys don't complain. Greys who complain… disappear. Like my dad. They took him two winters ago.

**9.** _in bed, in the dark: Pip whispers_
> **PIP:** Do you think Dad's coming back?
> **NARRATOR:** I didn't answer. I didn't know how.

**10.** _Nova sits up with a gasp, a hand at her throat_
> **NARRATOR:** Then one morning, I woke up… and my voice felt different. Heavy. Like thunder hiding in my throat.

**11.** _Nova faces the officer at the shut shop_
> **NOVA:** Nova commands you to give them the bread.

**12.** _the shutter rolls up; the officer hands out the bread, eyes empty_
> **NARRATOR:** When I say my name, and a command, anyone who hears it has to obey.
> **NARRATOR:** And then they forget me. Like I was never there.

**13.** _night: Pip, fed for once, holding Nova's hand_
> **PIP:** Promise you'll never forget me.
> **NARRATOR:** I promised. I never thought it would be the other way round.

**14.** _the warehouse guard, STORE 4_
> **NOVA:** Nova commands you to open the warehouse.
> **NARRATOR:** A hundred families ate that night.

**15.** _the list of Greys to be taken; it burns_
> **nvNovaCmd (voice-over):** Nova commands you to burn the list.
> **NARRATOR:** The list of Greys they were taking next. Forty names. Forty families who got to stay.

**16.** _Kai walks straight past Nova; she waves; nothing_
> **NARRATOR:** My best friend Kai saw me do it. The next morning, he walked past me at school.

**17.** _the kitchen corner: Mum smiles politely at a stranger_
> **MUM:** Sorry, love… are you one of Pip's friends?

**18.** _Nova, close, breaking_
> **NARRATOR:** My own mum. In our own kitchen. I made her tea, and she thanked me like a guest.

**19.** _the camp roster: Dad, WORKING_
> **NARRATOR:** Then I found out where Dad went. Not gone. Working. In the Governor's camp, behind the wall.

**20.** _the broadcast tower: Nova walks to the microphone_
> **NARRATOR:** If every speaker in town heard me, I could end it. All of it.

**21.** _Pip at home watching the screen, waiting for her sister_
> **NARRATOR:** But everyone who hears me forgets me. Everyone. Even Pip.

**22.** _the last command, to every speaker in town_
> **NOVA:** Nova commands you… every one of you.
> **NOVA:** Open the gates. Share everything. And never call anyone a Grey again.

**23.** _colour floods the town; the gate stands open; the Greys walk out into the light_

**24.** _Dad walks out of the gate; Pip runs to him; Mum crying_
> **NARRATOR:** Dad came home.

**25.** _Pip, in her yellow coat, stops in front of a stranger_
> **PIPY:** Sorry… do I know you?
> **NOVA:** No. But I know you.

**26.** _Nova walks away alone through the bright street_
> **NARRATOR:** I saved my whole town. And not one of them knows my name.
> **NARRATOR:** That's okay. I remember them.

**27.** _end card: the vote_
