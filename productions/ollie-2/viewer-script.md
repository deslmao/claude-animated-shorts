# What Ollie Says at 3 A.M. · Part 2: The Water Game: viewer script

What the viewer sees and hears, in order. Nothing else reaches them.

**1.** _cold open: Kate wading in, her back to us, rain_
> **NARRATOR:** Three in the morning. Your mum is walking into the river, fast asleep.

**2.** _Ava grabbing her arm, shouting; Kate's blank face in front_
> **AVA:** MUM!

**3.** _Kate's face, eyes open, unblinking; slow push_
> **NARRATOR:** She can't hear you. Only one voice can reach her. And it isn't yours.

**4.** _recap: Part 1's opening frame_
> **NARRATOR:** Last time: your little brother Ollie started speaking Polish in his sleep. In a baby's voice.

**5.** _recap: Kuba's photo and Ollie's: the same face_
> **NARRATOR:** Mum hides an old photo of a little boy called Kuba. He looks exactly like Ollie.

**6.** _recap: Part 1's garden, through the phone_
> **NARRATOR:** At three this morning, she sleepwalked him into the flooded garden. Only your garden. And you filmed it.

**7.** _the wet lawn, no water; Ava walks down to where it was_
> **NARRATOR:** By morning, the water's gone.

**8.** _her phone in the wet grass_
> **NARRATOR:** But your phone is still in the grass.

**9.** _the video: the water stops dead at the fence; next door is dry_
> **NARRATOR:** On the video, the water stops dead at your fence. Next door's lawn is bone dry.

**10.** _the vote: option 1, most of you_
> **NARRATOR:** Most of you said: show her the video. So you do.

**11.** _Kate watches the phone at the table, Ava beside her_
> **NARRATOR:** She watches herself lead Ollie into the water.

**12.** _Kate's confession, close_
> **KATE:** Kuba was my son. Your brother. When he was three, there was a flood. I let go of him. And he drowned.

**13.** _2008: she holds him out to a rescue boat; the boat overturned_
> **NARRATOR:** To save him, she held him out to a rescue boat. Then the boat went under.

**14.** _the saying, a whisper; tight_
> **NARRATOR:** Then she says something her grandmother used to say.
> **KATE:** Co puścisz nad wodą, to woda zabierze.

**15.** _Kuba's photo, then Ollie's face over it_
> **NARRATOR:** Every night in her sleep, she's back in that flood. Only now, the boy she lets go of is Ollie.

**16.** _DAD · NIGHTS_
> **NARRATOR:** Your dad works nights. He doesn't even know Kuba existed.

**17.** _Kate begs_
> **KATE:** Please. Don't tell your dad.

**18.** _option two as well_
> **NARRATOR:** So you also try option two: keep Ollie away from her.

**19.** _Kate locks her door_
> **NARRATOR:** Mum locks herself in her room.

**20.** _Ollie asleep in Ava's bed; Ava sitting up beside him_
> **NARRATOR:** Ollie sleeps in yours, with you.

**21.** _Ava's door: no lock_
> **NARRATOR:** But your door doesn't lock.

**22.** _2:59 -> 3:00_

**23.** _Ollie bolt upright, eyes shut: L1 in the toddler voice_
> **NARRATOR:** Three a.m. Ollie sits up, still asleep, and the baby voice comes out of him.
> **OLLIE:** Mamo, woda jest przy drzwiach.

**24.** _Ava holds him; he goes limp_
> **NARRATOR:** That's what Kuba said to wake her up, the night of the flood.

**25.** _Kate's lock turns open in her sleep_
> **NARRATOR:** Then Mum's lock clicks open. She's unlocking it in her sleep.

**26.** _feet stop in the light under the door_
> **NARRATOR:** She stops outside your door.
> **olKateNight (voice-over):** Kubuś?

**27.** _Ava at her door, Ollie asleep behind_
> **NARRATOR:** She's looking for Kuba. And in her sleep, Ollie is Kuba.

**28.** _Ava holds the handle shut_
> **NARRATOR:** You hold the handle shut.

**29.** _Ava listening at the door; the footsteps fade_
> **NARRATOR:** Her footsteps go back downstairs.

**30.** _the bang; Ava turns_

**31.** _from Ava's window: Kate alone, walking into the flood_
> **NARRATOR:** From your window, you see her walk into the river. Alone. She's still looking for him, in the water.

**32.** _Kate calling her son, waist-deep_
> **KATE:** Kubuś!

**33.** _Ava at her window, shouting_
> **AVA:** MUM!

**34.** _Ollie asleep; Ava runs for the door_
> **NARRATOR:** She can't hear you. So you leave Ollie asleep, and run.
> **AVA:** Stay here!

**35.** _the struggle: dragged deeper_
> **NARRATOR:** You grab her arm. But she's stronger than you. If you don't let go, you'll both go under.

**36.** _from the river: Ollie at the edge, barefoot, eyes shut_
> **NARRATOR:** Then, behind you, a small voice. Ollie followed you.

**37.** _L1 at the edge_
> **OLLIE:** Mamo, woda jest przy drzwiach.

**38.** _Kate stops_
> **NARRATOR:** Kuba's words. The only voice that can reach her.

**39.** _Kate wades back to him_
> **NARRATOR:** Mum stops, walks back, and takes Ollie's hand.

**40.** _L2: hand in hand_
> **KATE:** Kubuś, trzymaj mnie za rękę.

**41.** _wide: the three of them, the rain_
> **NARRATOR:** And the night of the flood plays out again.

**42.** _L3, a whisper_
> **KATE:** Nie puszczę cię. Obiecuję.

**43.** _2008 again, a flash_
> **NARRATOR:** This is where she held Kuba out to the boat.

**44.** _L4: she lifts his arm over the water_
> **KATE:** Weź go!

**45.** _L5, the toddler voice_
> **OLLIE:** Obiecałaś, że nie puścisz.

**46.** _L6_
> **KATE:** Przepraszam.

**47.** _her fingers open; his hand slips toward the water_
> **NARRATOR:** Her hand opens, and the river pulls Ollie in.

**48.** _Ava catches him: the heave_
> **NARRATOR:** But you're already in the water. You catch him.

**49.** _Kate walks away up the lawn, past them_
> **NARRATOR:** And Mum turns and walks back to bed, still asleep.

**50.** _Ollie, awake, matter-of-fact_
> **OLLIE:** It's the water game. If I don't say my words, Mama goes in the water by herself.

**51.** _the house, the water high on the lawn; pull back_
> **NARRATOR:** Keeping them apart sent her into the river alone. Only Ollie's words can bring her back. And every night, the water comes a little closer to the house.

**52.** _end card: Part 3, the vote_
