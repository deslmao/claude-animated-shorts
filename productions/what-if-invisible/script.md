# What If… you could turn invisible? (draft script for the owner's approval)

**Series:** "What If…" (Thinkora). Supernatural comedy.

**Premise (one sentence):** you discover you turn invisible whenever you hold your breath. You use it to prank
everyone at school, until you find out you're not the only one.

**The rule, said in words at once:** hold your breath, you vanish; breathe, you're back.

**Length:** about 60 s. **Narrator:** the channel's calm storyteller, in the second person ("you"), with dry comedic
timing. **Time cards:** DAY 1 / DAY 3 / DAY 5 / DAY 7. **Captions:** a colour per speaker.

---

**0:00** *(Bathroom mirror. You hold your breath, cheeks puffed. Your reflection fades to nothing.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Imagine you hold your breath… and disappear.

**DAY 1** *(You gasp; you're back. Hold again; gone.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Breathe out, you're back. Hold it, you're gone. That's the rule.
> **NARRATOR:** So obviously, you use it responsibly.

*(Cut: the lunch queue. An empty space walks straight past twenty kids. A tray rises by itself.)*
> **NARRATOR:** You skip the lunch queue.

*(Planted: a quiet new kid at the end of your table, no food in front of him.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Oh, and there's a new kid in your class. Quiet. Never eats.

**DAY 3** *(Your best friend Jayden at his desk. His pencil slides one inch every time he looks away.)*
> **NARRATOR:** You move your best friend's pencil. One inch. Every time he looks away.
> **JAYDEN** *(whispering to the pencil)*: …I'm sorry, okay?
> **NARRATOR:** Honestly? The best part is watching people crack.

*(The teacher writes "TEST TODAY" on the board. It wipes itself off. She writes it again. Wiped.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Your teacher writes "test today". You wipe it off. She writes it again. You wipe it again.
> **TEACHER** *(putting the pen down, very calmly)*: Nope.
> **NARRATOR:** She goes home early. Test cancelled. You're a hero.

**DAY 5** *(You, invisible, holding in a laugh. Cheeks shaking. You burst out laughing, and pop back into view
holding the board rubber. The whole class stares.)*
> **NARRATOR:** There's just one problem. Laughing counts as breathing.
> **JAYDEN:** …Why are you holding the rubber?

**DAY 7** *(Your lunch tray. Your chips are vanishing, one at a time.)*
> **NARRATOR:** Then your chips start disappearing. One at a time.
> **NARRATOR:** Wait. You're not even invisible right now.

*(Class. You hold your breath and vanish. Silence. Then a voice right next to your ear.)*
> **NEW KID** *(a whisper)*: Took you long enough.
> **NARRATOR:** It's the new kid. He's been invisible all week. Eating your chips.

*(End card, silent: WHAT WOULD YOU DO FIRST? · comment below 👇)*

## Production notes
- **One new engine look (an opt-in addition):** a character turning invisible fades to a faint shimmering outline,
  then vanishes. Props they hold float. Nothing else in older films changes.
- **Reuse:** the school sets, corridor and classroom from One Too Many, the ElevenLabs sound library, and a custom
  comedy score (Eleven Music).
- **Voices:**
  - the narrator;
  - Jayden (an energetic teen voice);
  - the teacher (a dry adult voice);
  - the new kid (a whisper).
