# The First Surprise
spec: 2
format: world-rule+emotional
why: One visual rule (your shadow acts out what you truly want) makes secrets impossible, which gives a loving, concrete goal (Gus's first real surprise) and an obstacle the protagonist carries everywhere (his own shadow); the solution uses two rules shown earlier, and the payoff is an image, not a speech.
length: 75
voice: george
voices: milo=liam, gus=brian
mood: warm
factual: no

## Premise
- question: How do you surprise someone in a world where your shadow gives away everything you want?
- protagonist: Milo
- want: to give Grandpa Gus the first real surprise of his life, on his 80th birthday
- fear: his own shadow ruining it, as everyone's always has
- protects: Gus's happiness; Gus has quietly faked a gasp at every birthday for 79 years
- decision: to hide the party in the dark, and to keep his own shadow quiet by being completely absorbed in a domino run with Gus
- loses: if it fails, Gus gets one more polite, fake gasp at eighty, and Milo will have proved Gus right that it can't be done
- obstacle: in any light, Milo's shadow acts out the party before he does, and he lives with Gus
- rule: In light, your shadow acts out what you truly want, a moment before you do. In the dark, nobody has a shadow. When you're completely absorbed in what you're doing, your shadow just copies you.
- conflict: none
- action: none

## Spine
depth: standard

### Constraints
- In light, your shadow acts out what you truly want, a moment before you do. It can't speak, only move.
- In the dark, nobody has a shadow.
- When you're completely absorbed in what you're doing, your shadow simply copies you, because you want nothing else.
- Milo lives with Gus. Gus is home all day, so the party has to happen at home.
- Shadows show in any light, lamps or sun.

### Easy outs
| obstacle | obvious fix | why it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Milo's shadow gives away the party | stay away from Gus until the party | they live together; at breakfast (E6) Gus is right there, and a missing Milo would be suspicious |
| the guests' shadows give them away | have the whole party in the dark | Gus would never walk into a dark room, and someone has to turn the lights on eventually; the domino run does it at the right moment (E11) |
| Gus would see the preparations | hold the party somewhere else | Milo's shadow would act out the plan all week (E7's rejected option) |
| Gus might guess | blindfold him | in a world without surprises, a blindfold *is* the giveaway (E8) |
