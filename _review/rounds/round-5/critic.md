# Critic report, round 5: "GTA 6, Explained to Your Girlfriend · Ep 1"

Judged only on what is on screen: the 20 full-resolution critic frames, the motion strip, and the reference sheets (REF).

## Scores

| Category | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Characters | 5 | The designs read clearly and have good silhouettes (cap, shades, curls). But the poses are stiff, mostly standing square to camera with arms hanging. The acting is limited to swapping face sprites. Props aren't gripped (the controller lies under a hand in 6, 7, 18, 20). At night Lucy's hair turns into a black blob with a heavy halo, which hides her curls. In the flat, the characters don't feel seated in the world. REF characters lean, point, hold things and overlap the set. |
| Motion | 4 | The strip shows slow push-ins, blinks and face swaps (0-15, 1395-1410), an on-the-spot walk with a static background (1140-1155), and a phone zoom-out with the clock ticking (1455-1470). Bodies barely change pose within a shot. 8 of 20 frames are flat graphic inserts. |
| Light | 5 | The game exteriors are good: warm sun and cast shadows (14, 16), and a coherent sunset grade (17). The flat is lit flat and evenly. There's no TV glow, no falloff and no colour pooling on the characters, and the moon-in-window night looks like day with a dark rectangle. The bedroom in frame 1 is soft but generic. REF night scenes (2_26, 2_34) use torch and moon key light, rim light and deep contrast. |
| World | 5 | The game city has density (14: hotels, palms, a traffic light, cars), but the cars are untextured blocks. The pier/sea (15) is a flat turquoise plane with a cardboard sailboat. The flat is a simple toon set: a sofa, a table, one picture. 8 of 20 frames are 2D cards or phone screens with no world. REF sets (vault, mine, tent, prison) are packed with props and texture. |
| Camera | 5 | There's variety in scale (wide 6, 20; medium 14, 16; extreme close-ups 5, 9, 12). But most shots are centred, eye-level and frontal. The extreme close-ups crop the hands and props at the bottom edge (5, 12). Frame 18 cuts Lucy's head at the left edge. The wall inserts (8, 10, 11, 13) are the same framing four times. REF uses low angles, foreground framing and over-the-shoulder shots (1_26, 1_50, 4_18). |
| Captions | 6 | The captions are legible, consistently sized, outlined and speaker-coloured (pink Lucy in 9, teal Gordon in 12, 18). Mostly they sit at y≈1235, below the faces. Problems: in frame 20 the caption sits on the held controller and hands, in 14 it covers the BAYLUNE neon sign, and the placement jumps between y≈1235, y≈580 and y≈425 across shots (14-17). |

## Top fixes (weakest category first)

1. **Motion.** Give the bodies real pose changes inside shots.
   - In the flat shots (6, 7, 12, 18, 20), animate controller play: thumbs or arm bob, a lean forward, a head tilt toward the TV.
   - In the 1140-1155 walk, track the camera with Gordon instead of a locked frame.
   - Add parallax or a slow drift with foreground depth to the four wall inserts (8, 10, 11, 13). Better, show them over a shoulder (Lucy's hair in the foreground) instead of as flat full-screen cards.
2. **World and light in the flat.**
   - Add a TV glow key: cool blue light from the TV direction, rim on both characters, the room falloff darker, warm practical light from the pendant lamp.
   - Add set dressing: shelves, posters, cables, snacks, a console under the TV.
   - Fix Lucy's night hair so the curl shapes stay visible (lighter fill or rim light, a thinner outline halo).
   - Outside, give the cars textures or detail (windows, trim) and the pier sea normal or wave texture (14, 15).
3. **Props, framing and captions on the failing frames.**
   - Frames 6, 7, 18, 20: put the controller *in* the hands (grip at both hand points, angled up).
   - Frame 18: widen or reframe so Lucy's whole head is inside the frame, with about 60 px clearance on the left.
   - Frame 20: move the caption off the hands and controller (to y≈1400 over the sofa cushion, or above the heads under the banner).
   - Frame 14: keep the caption off the neon sign.
   - Pick one caption height for the game scenes and stick to it.
   - Check "NO VACANSA" on frame 16.
