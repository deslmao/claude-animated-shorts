## Recommended
**`thumbnails/recommended-whats-behind-it.jpg`:** "WHAT'S BEHIND IT?", over frame 400 (0:13.3).
- **The image:** the old door square in the frame, a dark slab against the pale patch of unfaded wallpaper where the
  photo hung. The cold light leaks from under it; Milo and Juno smile up from the photo on the floor.
- **Why:** the door is the subject and the highest-contrast shape, so it reads even at grid size. The question is the
  curiosity gap: the door is closed and the light under it says something is there.

**YouTube frame picker:** if your channel can't upload a custom Shorts thumbnail, pick **0:13.0–0:13.9** (the same
frame, without the text).
