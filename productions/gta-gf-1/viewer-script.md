# GTA 6, Explained to Your Girlfriend · Ep 1: viewer script

What the viewer sees and hears, in order. Nothing else reaches them.

**1.** _frame 0: Lucy, smiling, typing on her bed in the sun; the banner; on 'hey', her phone: 'hey :)' sent_
> **NARRATOR:** Imagine you text your boyfriend "hey".

**2.** _her phone: the clock races 3:02 PM → 9:14 PM, then his reply pops in: 'sorry. gta'_
> **NARRATOR:** Then he replies six hours later: "sorry. GTA."

**3.** _the title card: GTA 6, EXPLAINED TO YOUR GIRLFRIEND · EP 1_
> **NARRATOR:** GTA 6, explained to your girlfriend.

**4.** _Gordon on the sofa, controller up, lit blue by the TV; Lucy beside the sofa, arms folded_
> **NARRATOR:** This is Gordon. This is Lucy. And Lucy wants to know what's so special about a video game.

**5.** _a desk calendar: 2013 (the last one comes out), then the years flip on to 2026_
> **NARRATOR:** First: the last GTA came out in 2013. He's been waiting thirteen years.

**6.** _Lucy, close, deadpan_
> **GGLUCY:** That's longer than he's known me.

**7.** _the first trailer's view counter racing to 90,000,000 in 24 hours; NEW RECORD_
> **NARRATOR:** When the first trailer came out, ninety million people watched it in one day. No video that wasn't a song had ever been watched that fast.

**8.** _COMING SOON, stamped DELAYED, then DELAYED again_
> **NARRATOR:** Then they delayed the game. Twice.

**9.** _Gordon, close, a hand on his chest, wounded_
> **GGGORDON:** We suffered.

**10.** _a till receipt printing on and on; the total: $1,840,000,000 (est.)_
> **NARRATOR:** So when it finally came out, it made an estimated one point eight billion dollars by launch day. That's more than double the last one's launch.

**11.** _the camera pushes into the TV (the game street on it), and through it: Gordon's game self strolls along the sunny pavement, our HUD on screen_
> **NARRATOR:** And inside, it's a whole sunny state you can go anywhere in.

**12.** _the game: Gordon fishing off the pier_
> **NARRATOR:** He can go fishing.

**13.** _the game: shooting hoops by the motel_
> **NARRATOR:** Play basketball.

**14.** _the game: his pink car on the beach road at sunset_
> **NARRATOR:** Or just drive at sunset.

**15.** _Lucy has sat down beside him, slowly coming round_
> **GGLUCY:** So you're not ignoring me…

**16.** _Gordon, blissful_
> **GGGORDON:** I'm on holiday. In Miami. From my sofa.

**17.** _Lucy takes the controller, hooked; on 'once', Gordon, now holding his phone, texting her_
> **NARRATOR:** So she tries it. Just once.

**18.** _Gordon's phone: 'hey?' sent at 3:02 PM; the clock races to 9:14 PM_
> **NARRATOR:** Six hours later…

**19.** _her reply pops in on his phone: 'sorry. gta'; then the two of them: Lucy glued to the game, Gordon staring at his phone; the ending banner_
> **NARRATOR:** Girlfriends: what should we explain to you next? Tell us in the comments.
