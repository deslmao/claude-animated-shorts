# GTA 6, Explained to Your Girlfriend · Ep 2: Meeting His GTA Friends: viewer script

**For the owner's approval. No voices are made until this is approved.**

**The owner's concept** (24 Nov), kept as written: the girlfriend thinks she's meeting his friends, then slowly
realises she's entered a different world. A fast comedy, 45–60 s.

**Our rules, kept:**
- **A narrator sets each scene up before the characters talk:** the storytelling gate. These are the short NARRATOR
  lines I added.
- **The breakfast-pattern hook on frame 0.**
- **The realistic Blender world from the first frame:** the game is our Miami city, and real life is a Blender flat.
- **The motion targets:** almost-still frames about 2.5 %, repeated frames 0.5–1 %, a moving camera in 10–30 %.

The earlier "Lucky Shirt" draft is kept as `viewer-script-lucky-shirt.md` for a later episode.

**Cast:**
- **NARRATOR** (Chris);
- **GORDON**, the boyfriend (Anthony);
- **LUCY**, the girlfriend (Caty);
- two new friends of our own design, each with a designed ElevenLabs voice, heard over his headset:
  - **TANK:** huge, loud, a gold chain and a bucket hat; he shouts everything;
  - **ZIP:** small, fast, a neon visor; dramatic.

**Frame 0 banner:** **WHEN HE INTRODUCES YOU** / **TO HIS GTA FRIENDS**

### Scene 1: The introduction (0–9 s)
**1.** _frame 0, the game: a sweeping pan along the sunny beach road; Gordon's pink convertible pulls up beside two
characters leaning on a car: Tank and Zip_
> **NARRATOR:** Imagine meeting your boyfriend's friends for the first time. Then you realise they all live inside GTA.

**2.** _real life (the Blender flat at night): Gordon hands Lucy a second controller_
> **GORDON:** Babe, come meet my friends. They're actually really nice.

**3.** _the game: Lucy's character drops in beside Gordon; Tank and Zip whip round_
> **TANK** _[shouting]_**:** YO, WHO'S THIS?
> **GORDON:** That's my girlfriend. Be normal.
> **ZIP** _[gasps]_**:** WAIT. YOU HAVE A GIRLFRIEND?

### Scene 2: The interrogation (9–20 s)
**4.** _the game: Tank and Zip circle Lucy; the camera orbits with them_
> **NARRATOR:** Within seconds, it's an interrogation.
> **TANK:** How long have you two been together?
> **LUCY:** Six months.

**5.** _Zip, close, appalled; Gordon behind him, embarrassed_
> **ZIP** _[shouting]_**:** SIX MONTHS? BRO'S BEEN HIDING A WHOLE RELATIONSHIP?

### Scene 3: The disaster (20–36 s)
**6.** _the game: Gordon unlocks his gleaming convertible_
> **GORDON:** Anyway… let's go for a drive.
> **NARRATOR:** But Lucy gets into the driver's seat.
> **GORDON** _[nervous]_**:** Wait. Babe. Don't touch anything.

**7.** _the car lurches off down the road, swerving; the camera whips after it_
> **NARRATOR:** She touches everything.

**8.** _a palm tree; CRASH; steam; then silence. The camera holds on the wreck, still drifting_
> _(silence, a hissing radiator)_

**9.** _Tank and Zip, staring_
> **TANK** _[flat, deadpan]_**:** Bro. She's not the one.
> **ZIP** _[whispers]_**:** She's a fed.

### Scene 4: The betrayal (36–50 s)
**10.** _Gordon steps between them_
> **GORDON:** Guys, relax. It's just a car.
> **TANK** _[shouting]_**:** JUST A CAR? THAT'S A MILLION-DOLLAR CAR!

**11.** _close on Gordon's game phone: a private message pops up from Zip_
> **NARRATOR:** Then Zip sends him a private message.
> **ZIP** _(the message, read in a whisper)_**:** Bro… I think she's using you for your garage.

### The punchline (50–58 s)
**12.** _Lucy climbs out of the smoking car, totally fine, smiling_
> **LUCY:** Actually… I think your friends are really sweet.

**13.** _Tank and Zip instantly shove each other: "I'll drive her home!" "No, I will!"; Gordon, alone, in the middle_
> **TANK & ZIP** _[arguing over each other]_**:** I'll drive her home! / No, I'LL drive her home!
> **NARRATOR:** He introduced his girlfriend to his friends… and accidentally introduced her to his biggest rivals.

## Clarity block
- **Premise:** his girlfriend joins him in GTA to meet his friends, and it goes very wrong. "Imagine meeting your
  boyfriend's friends for the first time. Then you realise they all live inside GTA."
- **Who the friends are:** "Babe, come meet my friends." Then they appear and speak.
- **Why it turns:** "But Lucy gets into the driver's seat." / "She touches everything." Then the crash.
- **The twist:** "I think your friends are really sweet." Then the two friends fight over her, so "his biggest rivals".

## Notes for the owner
- **GTA 6 online.** GTA 6 has no online mode yet (the tips transcript says Rockstar hasn't announced when it's
  coming). So the narration says "inside GTA", never "GTA 6 Online". A sharp-eyed commenter can't call it wrong, and
  "his GTA friends" in the title is the everyday way people say it.
- **The last line.** Your original said "his biggest enemies". I made it "rivals", because the friends now compete
  for her rather than hate him, which makes the joke land. Say if you'd rather keep "enemies".
- **Tank and Zip** are our own characters: no game or real-person lookalikes.

## ElevenLabs, used to the full (the owner: "use every feature")
1. **Voice Design:** two new custom voices made for Tank (deep, booming, loud) and Zip (high, fast, dramatic),
   auditioned before they're used.
2. **Eleven v3 with audio tags** for every character line: [shouting], [gasps], [whispers], [nervous], [deadpan],
   [laughs], and overlapping argument. That gives real performance, not flat reading.
3. **Text to Dialogue (v3, multi-speaker)** for the back-and-forth exchanges (the introduction, the interrogation, the
   argument). The lines come back as one natural conversation, with real timing and interruptions.
4. **Context on every line** (the words before and after), so each delivery fits the moment.
5. **Headset sound:** the friends' voices go through our "radio" voice effect, so they sound like voice chat.
6. **Sound effects:** the engine and pull-up, the tyre screech, the crash and the hissing radiator, the message ping,
   the drop-in whoosh, and ambient beach traffic.
7. **Eleven Music:** a fast comic score with a hard stop at the crash (the silence) and a comic sting on "She's a fed".
8. **Speech to Text with word timings:** checks every generated line against the script (no mumbled or missing words)
   and times the captions and cuts exactly.

**The cost:** v3 lines and voice design use more credits than our usual voices. A ~55 s episode is still well within
a normal monthly plan.
