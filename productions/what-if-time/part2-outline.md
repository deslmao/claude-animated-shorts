# What If… You Could Stop Time? · Part 2: Forty-One Days (outline; the full script comes after Part 1)

About 1:35. It opens on the exact frame where Part 1 cut to black. Theo narrates.

1. **The vote (0:00).**
   - (Black, a heartbeat. Then the vault, the gun at Sol's head.)
   - THEO: "Most of you said: save her. She wanted me to keep my promise. I couldn't do both."
   - Both answers lead into the same scene.
2. **The minute (0:08).**
   - He squeezes his fist. Silence. Everything freezes blue: the guards, the bullet leaving the gun, Dex mid-shout.
   - On his wrist: 41… 40…
3. **Talking to her in frozen time (0:15).**
   - He walks to Sol, frozen with her eyes shut. He moves the gun barrel away from her head.
   - THEO: "You can't hear me. That's okay. I'm going to talk anyway."
   - He carries her out through a frozen city: rain hanging in the air, a frozen crowd, neon signs stopped mid-flicker.
   - The counter keeps falling: 30… 20…
4. **The train (0:40).**
   - He sets her in a seat on the train out of the city, by the window.
   - He records a message on her visor, then puts the second ticket in her hand.
   - The counter: 3… 2…
   - THEO: "I had one day left. I spent it watching her."
   - He lets go of his fist. Time starts. The train pulls away, and he isn't on it.
5. **The sea (1:00).**
   - The only daylight in the film: a white beach and a real blue sea.
   - Sol wakes up and runs into the water. She takes off her visor (Part 1's plant) and sees it with her own eyes.
   - Then the visor in her hand plays his message.
   - THEO (recorded): "If you're watching this, you made it. I'm sorry I broke my promise. Both of them. I hope the sea is
     everything you said."
   - SOL (crying, to the empty beach): "You said you'd come with me."
6. **The last line (1:25).**
   - THEO: "I didn't make it to the sea. But for one second, I stopped time. Just to watch her see it."
   - The last image: Sol in the waves, holding the second ticket.
7. **End card (silent).** WOULD YOU SPEND YOUR LIFE ON SOMEONE? · 1 = YES · 2 = NO 👇

**Payoffs from Part 1:** the visor, the sea, "Then I'm coming with you" / "It's a promise", "Promise me you'll stop",
and the 41 days. Part 2 introduces nothing new, so a viewer who saw Part 1 understands every beat. Line 1 also catches
up anyone who didn't.

**Sound:**
- In stopped time: no rain sound, only a low hum and his heartbeat slowing.
- The counter ticks.
- Waves and gulls on the beach.
- The score, held back the whole time, finally comes in on the sea.
