# 6 7: viewer script

What the viewer sees and hears, in order. Nothing else reaches them.

**1.** _frame 0: in the dark, Isaac floats in the air, both hands clutching his neck, choking; the banner_
> **NARRATOR:** My best friend did the six seven meme on a Ouija board. It was the last thing he ever said.

**2.** _the three of them slumped on the sofa, faces lit by their phones; you, bored; Isaac (glasses), grinning at his phone_
> **NARRATOR:** We were chilling at Glendon's place. In the living room, scrolling mindlessly.

**3.** _Isaac's phone: a cartoon cat bounced up and down, 'huss huss huss'_
> **ssPhone (voice-over):** In this town, everyone is partying except for me. I'm locked in. HUSS HUSS HUSS HUSS.

**4.** _you, scrolling; then, out of nowhere, Glendon's face right in the lens, holding up a Ouija board_
> **NARRATOR:** Then Glendon pulls out a Ouija board.

**5.** _you, clutching your chest_
> **SSME:** Geez, don't scare me like that.

**6.** _candlelight; the three of them lean over the coffee table, hands out to the board_
> **NARRATOR:** Then all three of us hold on to the thing that goes on the Ouija board.

**7.** _three hands on the planchette: it circles slowly round the letters, then settles on 6, then on 7_
> **NARRATOR:** It floats around all of the letters… but goes to six… and seven.

**8.** _Isaac yanks his hand off the board, laughing, doing the 6 7 hands_
> **ssIsaac (voice-over):** [laughing hysterically] SIX SEVEN! SIX SEVEN! SIX SEVEN!

**9.** _you, close, telling him off; Isaac, still doing it; the board, still_
> **SSME:** Isaac, stop playing around mate. You're not supposed to have your hands off the thing, or else you'll get cursed.

**10.** _your hand and Glendon's dragged by the planchette: 7, 6, 7, 6, slow, then faster and faster_
> **NARRATOR:** Then me and Glendon's hands move automatically. Going through six seven. Again. And again.

**11.** _Isaac, close, cracking up, still doing the 6 7 hands_
> **ssIsaac (voice-over):** [laughing hysterically] HAHAHA! SIX SEVEN!

**12.** _Glendon turns to Isaac, panicking, shouting at him; Isaac still laughing beside him_
> **ssGlendon (voice-over):** [panicked shouting] STOP IT, it's getting out of control!!!

**13.** _the lights die; torchlight; Isaac, still laughing in the dark, still doing the hands_
> **NARRATOR:** The lights go off.
> **NARRATOR:** And Isaac's still laughing.

**14.** _torchlight on Isaac, facing us: both hands clutching his neck, choking; the wide reveals he's floating off the floor_
> **NARRATOR:** Then all of a sudden, his neck starts choking. He holds onto it… and he floats in the air.

**15.** _Glendon screams and runs for the door_
> **ssGlendon (voice-over):** [screaming] AAAAAHHH!

**16.** _you, alone on the sofa, frozen_
> **NARRATOR:** And his last words?

**17.** _back to the first frame: Isaac in the air, his last words, muffled; cut to black on '7'_
> **ssIsaacMuffled (voice-over):** [strangled, choking] Six… seven…
