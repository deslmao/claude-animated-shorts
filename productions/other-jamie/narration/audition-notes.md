# The Other Jamie: voice audition

The story is set in a British town, and the default ElevenLabs voices have no young British male, so four voice-library
voices were auditioned on one line each (in `auditions/`). Measured on voiced frames: median pitch, pitch variation
(standard deviation in semitones) and pitch range (5th to 95th percentile).

| Take | Voice | Median pitch | Variation | Range |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sam: "My best friend texted me…" | Johnny Kid (serious, calm narrator) | 105 Hz | 2.7 st | 8.3 st |
| Sam: same line | **Astro (young British storyteller)** | 117 Hz | 3.1 st | **9.7 st** |
| Jamie: "It's for the girl from Mill Lane…" | Archie (warm, upbeat) | 99 Hz | 2.4 st | 7.7 st |
| Jamie: same line | **Toby (youthful, slightly raspy)** | **140 Hz** | **3.1 st** | 7.1 st |

## Casting
- **Sam: Astro.** The most expressive narrator of the four, and about 3 semitones lower than Toby, so the two boys sound clearly different.
- **Jamie: Toby.** The youngest-sounding (140 Hz), with a slower, softer delivery that suits the copy.
- **The thing in the woods: Astro again**, Sam's own voice, but slow (0.8), steady and flat (stability 0.8, no style). It sounds like Sam, but wrong.
