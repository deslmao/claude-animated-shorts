## Checks
- **Wording:** every line matches the approved script exactly.
- **Pronunciation:** names (Gus, Juno, Grandma) and numbers ("Ten thousand. Eighty thousand.") are read as
  written. Line 23's quoted "fake." is read with a natural pause.
- **Pace:** about 3.3 words a second on average while speaking, faster than the 2.8 estimate. Speech totals about
  67 s, so the planned wordless action now fills the rest of the 89.2 s timeline: no padding, and the runtime is
  unchanged.
- **Deliberate slow lines:** "Politicians sink during speeches." (2.2 s), "Stones. Everywhere." (a clear gap
  between the two words), "Ten thousand. Eighty thousand." (spaced out).
- **Silence after "I was the last one.":** 5.4 s of silence in the review file, from 57.6 s to 63.1 s, as
  planned.
- **Loudness:** lines sit at about −22.5 LUFS; the shouted lines (14, 18, 22) are about 2 dB louder, as they
  should be. Gus's "This is your fault." is about 3 dB quieter than the rest: a grumble, which works. If it reads
  too quiet under the music, the mix can lift it; it doesn't need regenerating.

## "I was the last one." needs your ear
- **As generated (preset `liamQuiet`):** the five words take 1.04 s. The voice is calm, but the timing is quick
  for the story's most important line.
- **Free alternative:** the same recording with 0.35 s of silence spliced in after "I was" ("I was… the last
  one."). This is the engine's existing per-line pause feature, which needs no regeneration.
- **If neither feels right,** one slower retake of just this line would cost about 8 credits. It needs a new,
  additive preset, because I didn't change the shared ones.

My recommendation is the pause version, but please listen to both.
